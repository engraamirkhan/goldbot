- **Adaptive risk limits, lane A** (ADR 0031 with Amendment A1, rules frozen before any replay): `goldbot/risk/adaptive.py` computes the account daily/weekly caps, the supervisor caps and the minimum-lot cap from demo fills after each strategy's freeze. The statistic is a daily-R-sum LCB, and q is quarter-Kelly per strategy, the smaller of the trade and day terms, minimum over strategies. Loosening is demo-only, one step at the risk-day roll after 3 qualifying days and 10 new demo trades, never within 5 days of a tighten. Tightening is immediate (DD 4%, 4 losses at -0.8R, cost/model, size-down stage, weak statistic, shadow included). The demo minimum-lot cap starts with an exploration budget: 1.0% for the first 60 exception trades, off for good at -10R cumulative, paused by ATR, stage, cost, DD and the loss streak; then the LCB rule decides. Live accounts stay at the defaults; anything missing or corrupt gives the floors and a dq_event. State and an append-only change log live in core.db migration 4 (`adaptive_state`, `adaptive_changes`), after ADR 0032's migration 3.
