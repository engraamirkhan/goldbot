- **136. Strategy ranking: one builder, net R lower bound first.** Value: the system (and the owner, by his 2026-10-27 directive) sees which strategy families earn after costs and why, so research learns, improves and applies on evidence instead of anecdotes; lifecycle: review and improve; helps FIND an edge (and later RUN one through the allocator's pre-registered path). Spec: docs/design/trades-ux-2026-10-27.md §7; ADR 0053. Scope: one builder over the trade ledger (129) and `GET /api/strategies/ranking` (account, style, window filters), rows per family with timeframe variants, controls band, retired band. Acceptance (tests): (1) the primary sort is the lower bound of the 95% CI of net R per trade, CI clustered by (account, opportunity day); a seeded case where a 12-fill +0.8 R family ranks below a 400-fill +0.10 R family, and a 70%-hit-rate family at −0.09 R ranks below a 45% family at +0.05 R; (2) every column of §7.3 is present with the definition stated there (hit rate counts net £ > 0 after costs; profit factor "no losses yet" with no losers; max drawdown in R on close order; edge vs random uses the ADR 0051 pairing and, after a read, the evaluator's recorded `vs_control_r`); (3) a row under 30 fills or 5 opportunity days has `ranked: false`, no rank, sorts below ranked rows by n; (4) every row before its read or gate carries status "early, not proven"; (5) controls and shadow models are never ranked, cost probes excluded; (6) a rule edit (new `config_hash`) starts a new row; (7) import test: no module under goldbot/engine, the allocator, arm stops, annealing or the evaluator imports the ranking; (8) p95 < 300 ms at 50k fills; API contract regenerated. Depends: 129; ADR 0053 quant sign-off. TRACEABILITY: none yet; implementer adds a U row.
