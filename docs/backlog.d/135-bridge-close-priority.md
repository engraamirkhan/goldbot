- **135. MT5 bridge: closes ahead of reads and orders, abandoned reads dropped (before any real-money account).** Value: trading-safety review of 5d661e94 (MEDIUM, ADR 0052): the MT5 box serves one call at a time (`bridge.serve`, the MetaTrader5 package is not thread-safe), so while the terminal is sending an entry order (Vantage up to 31 s) the engine's tick reads, position reads and closes queue behind it, and reads the brain already gave up on are still served ahead of a close. The worst engine-side exit delay is `order_send.exit_delay_bound_s` (about 45-55 s at a 35 s order timeout); on demo the broker-side stop covers it, on real money it is not acceptable. Acceptance: (1) on the VPS bridge, requests are accepted concurrently and executed by one terminal worker from a priority queue: `close` and `modify` ahead of reads, reads ahead of `place_order`; still one terminal call at a time; (2) a queued request whose client has gone (the socket is closed) or whose age is past its client's timeout is dropped unexecuted (a read) or refused stale (an order, as `ORDER_MAX_AGE_S` today), never run; (3) brain client: a close sent while an order is in flight is not held behind the engine's own tick reads (the tick thread skips or shortens reads while `order_send` has an order in flight; closes keep the 15 s order timeout); (4) a test with an order holding the terminal 30 s shows an engine-side stop close reaching the terminal within 5 s of the stop being crossed; (5) the order is never re-sent and its outcome is still settled by the BACKLOG 72 look-up; (6) protocol version bump and `bridge_compat` handling per ADR 0010 (deploy order stated in the RUNBOOK). Not before: ADR 0052 merged.
