## Trades: Open | Activity | Summary (route tabs `#/trades/open|activity|summary`)

- **Open**: safety strip, decisions (Waiting | Recent, unchanged approval centre), then open trades with entry, stop,
  target (USD/oz), floating money, R now, DEMO/REAL and a probe badge.
- **Activity**: the activity feed (shadow, demo, live, probe trades and refused entries), growing with the page (no
  inner scroll trap), with one line saying a refusal sent no order.
- **Summary**: Today in full (closed/open tiles, costs, meter), the balance card per account (balance, loss budget,
  milestones), the trades summary (period and breakdown switches).
