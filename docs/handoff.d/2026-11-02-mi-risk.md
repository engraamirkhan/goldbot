- Multi-instrument demo, lane L2 (risk), library only: nothing calls it until L3 wires the SymbolBooks, so XAUUSD trading is unchanged (the pre-split decision grid is reproduced with the XAUUSD registry terms passed explicitly, `tests/test_multi_symbol_gate.py`). RiskGate (`goldbot/risk/gate.py`): `Intent` takes the registry terms (`intent_terms(spec)`: correlation_group, leverage_class, currency_profit, point; defaults are XAUUSD's), `profit_fx` converts a non-USD quote via `acct_per_profit` or refuses `fx_conversion_unavailable`. Leverage is per class: FX 30, gold and major indices 20 (`leverage_cap`), other commodities 10, bonds 5. On a multi-symbol state (`AccountState.group_notional` set) the combined cap is notional per correlation group (`group_exposure_cap`) and the 3-lot cap holds for gold only. `RiskLimits.multi_symbol` always projects open risk against the effective daily limit, and `RiskGate.book` reserves each allowed entry on the shared state, so two symbols on one bar cannot pass the daily limit. `exposure_unknown` refuses. `open_risk.account_open_risk` / `group_exposure` value every symbol from its own `SymbolQuote`. The supervisor publishes `exposure.<acct>.groups` and `combined_group_notional`, and a malformed map halts. `others_group_notional` adds them up. Sessions (`goldbot/data/calendar.py`): `SESSION_PROFILES` has one server-time table per registry profile. Each table has its own pauses, Friday cut (`risk/windows.py weekend_cut`) and exchange holidays (CME, CME whole-day, Eurex/ICE Europe). A non-gold profile is intersected with the broker's session list. `profile_disagreement` is used by the symbol check, and the metals profile is today's table. News (`goldbot/data/news.py`): `instrument_blackout` covers tier-1 USD as today, plus high-impact events in the instrument's other currencies and the group's EIA crude (Wed), EIA gas (Thu) and WASDE (12:00 ET, the 8th to 12th) windows.
