# Machine learning in goldbot: performance review (13 Nov 2026)

Principal ML researcher, 2026-11-13. Owner's question: "review the performance of the machine learning part of the
system". This is a read-only review. No trial was run, no parameter was changed, and the holdout was not touched. Every
live number below was read on the brain on 2026-11-13 (about 10:00 UTC), read-only (`aux.db?mode=ro`,
`state/shadow_book.json`, `models/registry.json`, `scheduler_jobs`, `data/news`). The research numbers are quoted from
the result files named in each row. **K added by this review: 0.** The measurements are descriptive reads of logs
that already exist, not trials. Program K was 3,237 at the MLNET confirm (2026-11-02, `mlprob/mlnet-results.md`).

## 1. Summary

**No ML component in goldbot improves a live trading decision today.** Only one model sets anything live: the Thompson
bandit sets arm shares on the demo exploration accounts. Measured against what happened next, it is no better than
equal shares:

| | Thompson-weighted next-day R | Equal-weight next-day R | Spearman (share, next-day R) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bandit, 24 broker-days | +0.085 | +0.086 | +0.003 |

Since 2026-11-09 the bandit has been degenerate. Two arms are left, each the only arm in its style, so each gets the
30 % cap whatever its record. One of them, F1A, has mean R −0.25 over 70 trades.

The other components fall into three groups:
- **Dormant:** the specialist LightGBM meta-models (shadow only, 0 live agents) and their recalibration, drift and
  CUSUM watchers (0 agents watched).
- **Never trained:** the H-62 online meta-labeller (0 model rows; 0 of 19,990 candidates carry a feature snapshot).
- **Switched off or silently inactive:** the N-3 HAR refusal (`enabled: false`), the news LLM scorer (0 of 392
  headlines scored, reason `no client`) and nightly automated discovery.

These results agree with the program's research record (R-07, R-20, MLNET, MLEXEC, PROBTREND, R-18) and with the
13 Nov survey. ML has not created an entry edge on gold here. Where ML can still help is as a feature extractor, a
range, regime or cost layer, or an ensemble weight on signals that already have gross edge.

## 3. Research record of ML in this program (do not re-test)

Each entry below was retired by its own pre-registered rule. Reopening any of them needs new information: a data
source, not a new algorithm or a new subset of the same columns.

- **R-07**: meta-models over primaries with no gross edge. Out-of-fold AUC about 0.50.
- **R-20 / A27** (`meta-label-screen-2026-11.md`), on 2010-2017:
  - ML-M1: top-decile net +0.016 R, 90 % lower bound −0.034; forward −0.015 R; out-of-fold AUC 0.537.
  - ML-M2: −0.513 R.
  - ML-M1c: failed.
  - K +3.
  - This is the one ranking that beat matched random selection (+0.10 R gross), and most of that came from
    side and swap (89 % shorts). Cost per trade (0.13 R) was larger than the gain.
- **MLNET** (`mlprob/mlnet-results.md`), learned selection over 11 trend rules:
  - V3 HGB-TOP3 passed explore (+0.088 R, t 2.22) and failed confirm (−0.012 R, t −0.37, DSR 0.000 at K 3,237).
  - Ridge and HGB-POS: t 1.41 and 1.34.
  - K +4.
- **MLEXEC** (`mlprob/mlexec-results.md`): 55 execution variants, including a learned chooser. 0 pass; the pullback
  limit was adversely selected. K +55.
- **PROBTREND** (`mlprob/probtrend-results.md`): trend-day probability. BSS −0.015 / −0.008; AUC 0.514 / 0.559. Failed
  calibration. K +12.
- **R-18**: discovery grammar v2, 0 / 864.
- **H-16**: quintile leg failed.
- **R-17**: H-17 on HAR-high days. Net −0.35 R.

Literature context, from the survey folder `algo-systems-review-2026-11-13/1-institutional.md` section 6:
- Gu, Kelly and Xiu (2020), RFS, https://www.nber.org/papers/w25398.pdf. US equity cross-section, monthly horizon,
  gross. Not transferable to one instrument on intraday bars.
- The same section reports no robust published net-of-cost single-asset intraday ML return predictor.
- Volatility persistence (HAR): Corsi (2009), *Journal of Financial Econometrics* 7(2), 174-196. Replicated widely.
  Its gold use rests on the in-house H-16 screen; it is a range forecast, not a return forecast.
- Carver's "speed limit": cost at most one third of the gross edge, as cited in the survey (practitioner,
  *(unverified)* page).

## 4. Three highest-value ML improvements, ranked by expected information per trial

None of these has been run, and none is pre-registered yet. Each needs its own pre-registration in the
strategy-researcher format, a `hypotheses.md` row and a research-director slot. Instruments, spend and sizing are owner
decisions.

### A. Range-to-cost gate on the pooled trend signals (the ML acts as the regime and cost layer)

- **Information added.** A day's forecast range in dollars, from HAR plus the H-16 terms that passed (ρ gain +0.20),
  divided by the measured round-trip cost of that hour. The binding problem in every failure above is cost per R: MLNET
  V3 confirm had gross +0.071 R against 0.083 R cost, and ML-M1 had +0.15 R gross against 0.13 R cost. A range forecast
  attacks that ratio directly. It does not pretend to time direction.
- **Base signal.** The 11-rule trend pool, which has a real gross edge (MLNET parity: explore pool net +0.014 R).
- **Honest test.**
  - HAR is refit in-fold, annually on an expanding window, on 2010-2017 explore. Point in time: `asof_join` on
    `available_utc`.
  - The gate is "forecast range ÷ cost in the top half of its trailing 250 days". The cut is frozen before any look.
  - **Continue only if all of these hold:**
    - gated net R minus ungated net R ≥ +0.05 R;
    - clustered t ≥ 2;
    - ≥ 5 of 8 years positive;
    - the gated set beats a random half of the same days.
  - Then one look at 2018-01..2025-09 with DSR at program K. The survey's regime test also applies: 2019-21 and the
    short side must not be negative.
- **Cost:** K +1, about 3 agent-hours. Prior 15-20 %. Partly data-snooped, because H-16 and MLNET are seen.

### B. Point-in-time feature snapshots on every exploration candidate (an enabler, K 0)

- **Information added.** None of the 19,990 logged candidates, each with a propensity and a replay net-R label, carries
  the features the engine saw.
- **The change.** Writing a ≤ 40-column snapshot with `available_utc_max_ns` turns the demo log into a broker-true,
  cost-true and off-policy-correct dataset. Every later regime, sizing or ensemble model can then be measured on our own
  fills instead of on Dukascopy proxies. Today it is the main reason the "learning loop" learns nothing.
- **Honest test.** These are acceptance checks, not a trial:
  - Lookahead parity: recompute 12 cuts × 50 rows from bars as of the decision, and require 0 mismatches.
  - Shuffle-label AUC within 0.50 ± 0.02.
  - The snapshot coverage ratio is reported daily (memory: verify-learning-outputs).
- **Cost:** an engineering lane. No K until a model reads it.

### C. Turn on the news scorer, used first as a range and shock feature, not a direction

- **Information added.** It is the only genuinely new source available to us, and R-20 says that reopening ML needs one.
  It is also the live shock blackout, which is inactive today.
- **Honest test.** This is a forward-only event study, not a return trial:
  - Do |returns| over 30 minutes after shocks with relevance ≥ 0.7 exceed matched same-hour non-shock windows?
  - Run it at a frozen checkpoint of ≥ 300 scored shocks, with a block bootstrap by day.
  - Direction stays collect-only for the 12 months already fixed in BACKLOG 211.
- **Cost:** K +1 at the checkpoint. **Owner decision:** LLM spend, about $0.50/day cap; the API credential goes into
  the keyring.

## 5. What I would stop doing

1. **The Thompson bandit as allocator.** Replace it with equal shares within the existing floor and cap. The evidence:
   ρ 0.003, and a −0.25 R arm at the 30 % cap.
2. **Saturday retrain and recalibration of retired-family LightGBM challengers.** They are stalled 25 days and score
   worse than climatology.
3. **The H-62 retrain path.** It re-tests R-20's feature family with no new information, and cannot train anyway.
4. **Calling any of this "learning"** in reports, until the label count, the weight count and the snapshot count are
   shown.
