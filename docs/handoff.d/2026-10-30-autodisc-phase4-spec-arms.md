- Autonomous discovery phase 4 (ADR 0058, design section 4.1-4.2): demo arms from discovered specs. `goldbot/research/arm_rules_spec.py` is the one interpreter (`RULE_FUNCS["spec_rule"]`): it runs a ledger spec bar by bar through the explore evaluator's own primitives (`LiveProvider` is `PrimitiveProvider` without the explore cut), so a G1 spec's live entry bars, side and ATR stop equal the research masks (tests on a golden synthetic set, a trailing-window warm-up test and a no-lookahead perturbation). A spec arm is refused at load when it cannot run exactly as research read it (structural stop, time-only exit, unsupported leaf, a family spec with a session window, hash or canonical-JSON mismatch). Demo only, at three layers: `load_arms(live_class=True)` and `exploration.config_for` refuse a `spec_rule` arm on any non-demo account; `exploration.Exploration` drops and journals one on a non-demo engine; the RiskGate's existing exploration fence is unchanged and tested against a spec arm. `python -m goldbot.ops.run autodisc-register <hash> --account <demo> --prereg <file#section>` registers an explore survivor (ledger result plus the ledger-wide search null) inside the account's style caps, SHARE_CAP x pot (ADR 0057) and the exploration budget, at most 4 a week (ADR 0045), journalling spec hash, K_search/K_null, ledger head and honesty results in `state/discovery.db` `arm_registrations` (hash-chained) before it writes `config/spec_arms.yaml` and the account's arm line. It places no order and starts nothing. Not built: the auto PR generator, the 8-week broker replay parity read, the forward-read job; the confirm/holdout stages do not exist yet, so a registered arm's stage is `explore_survivor` and its forward demo read is its first out-of-sample evidence. Reviews owed: trading-safety (engine arm selection, config load) and quant (interpreter parity, the registration's survivor rule). Review round 1: a bad row in `config/spec_arms.yaml` is dropped and journalled (`exploration_arm_dropped`), never a startup crash (only the live-class refusal stays fatal); `check` also refuses a family leaf whose arm needs stored bars or replica state and any spec the engine cannot warm up; `autodisc-register` is idempotent (rerun after a crash replays the file edits from the journalled row, no new row or weekly slot), recounts the weekly cap and duplicates inside the journal transaction under a file lock, refuses on main or a detached HEAD (rows go through PR + CI), rounds the cap to pence, hashes every journal column, and `--verify [--head <digest>]` detects a deleted tail.
