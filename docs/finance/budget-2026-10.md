## 1. Bad news first

1. **The system has no income and cannot earn real money before about Dec 2027 at the earliest**, and not at all at
   £1,000 as the gates are written: tiny-live risks 0.1% a trade and the 15m minimum lot needs about £6,850 of live
   equity (`capital-plan.md` s.4, owner decision C2). Every running cost is paid by the owner until then.
2. **Cash running costs are £0 today, but the largest real cost is outside these books.** The owner's own Claude
   subscription, which runs the whole program, is excluded (a personal tool); if the owner wants it counted, it
   dominates break-even. Its amount is not in the repo.
3. **Three ways £0 turns into a bill without a decision:** Oracle Pay As You Go (PAYG) bills anything above the free
   limits, including Object Storage over 20 GB (restic plus bucket versioning grows it); raising the staff-agent cap
   above today's $0.00 spend; and a GitHub spending limit above $0. Each has a guard below (s.2).
4. **The Wine bridge on a 1 GB micro is unproven.** If it fails, the fallback is a paid Windows VPS at about
   £8-15 a month from that day, which moves break-even from 0 to about 1.5 R a month (s.4).

## 4. Break-even

Unit: **£8.20 per R** (15m minimum lot). Break-even = net closed P&L after spread, commission, swap and slippage
equals the cash running costs.

| Scenario | £ a month | R a month | % of £1,000 | Net R per trade at 20 trades | Gross R per trade (adding 0.057 R) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A, as deployed** | **£0** | **0** | **0%** | **0** | **0.057** |
| B, agents $10 | £7.52 | 0.92 | 0.75% | 0.046 | 0.10 |
| D, paid bridge | £12.50 | 1.52 | 1.25% | 0.076 | 0.13 |
| E, B + D | £20.02 | 2.44 | 2.0% | 0.122 | 0.18 |
| C, agents $40 | £30.08 | 3.67 | 3.0% | 0.183 | 0.24 |

Reading: today the only hurdle is trading costs (a gross edge above 0.057 R a trade). The best result so far, tsmom,
is +0.06 R gross on 1h/4h and negative net (scorecard 2026-10). At the gates' +0.1 R net and 20 trades (£16.40 a
month), scenarios A, B and D are covered; C needs 0.18 R net, which no documented systematic gold edge reaches
(ADR 0004 s.8). Break-even falls in proportion to equity: at £6,850 (tiny-live, C2) scenario E is 0.3% a month.

**60-day warning rule (unchanged).** When a free trial, credit or free tier the system depends on ends within 60 days,
or a provider announces a cut, the monthly audit raises a HIGH finding to the program-director with the cost after
the end and the options. The owner rules by T-30; with no paid option recorded by T-7 the resource is deallocated or
migrated, never rolled into billing by default. Exits are never gated during a migration: the bridge moves only with
the book flat or server stops in place. An Oracle limit cut counts as a trigger.
