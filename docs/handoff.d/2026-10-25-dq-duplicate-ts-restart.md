- Engine restart with the market closed blocked entries with `dq_error duplicate_ts` (brain 2026-10-25 08:05, icm-demo and icm-probe). Not DST: the terminal's frozen quote was Friday's last tick (2026-10-23 20:56:59 UTC = 23:56:59 EEST, converted correctly). The previous engine had finalised and stored minute 20:56. The restarted engine loaded it in warm_start, re-built the same minute from the re-delivered tick at the clock-driven close, and `_check_new_bars` saw the stamp twice. The same happens on any restart while the market is closed or quiet. Fix: warm_start records `Engine._history_through` (the last loaded `visible_at`), and a tick stamped before it never goes into the live bar builder, because its minute is already in the history. The block used to clear by itself only at the first decision-bar close after the reopen (~15 min of entries refused). Tests: `tests/test_engine_history_gap.py` (fall-back Sunday and a plain Saturday, plus new minutes are still built and a real duplicate still blocks).
