## 0. Common conventions (apply to every arm unless the arm says otherwise)

- **Size:** 0.01 lot = 1 oz, always. Never resized, never widened. A candidate whose stop breaks its cap is skipped and
  logged (`stop_gt_cap` / `stop_lt_floor`).
- **Timing:** decision on the close of bar t of the arm's timeframe (all inputs from bars <= t, levels known only once
  their defining bars have closed; the lookahead test applies); market entry right after that close (history: the open
  of bar t+1, at the ask for longs / bid for shorts). Clock times are UTC unless a local session clock is named;
  London and New York opens use the local clocks of `session_open` (08:00 Europe/London, 08:30 America/New_York), so DST
  is handled.
- **Barriers:** stop and target are fixed prices set at entry; searched on 1m bars, stop first when both fall in one
  minute; time exit at the close of the arm's hold_bars-th bar. ATR = Wilder ATR(14) of the arm's decision bars on
  mid prices (`goldbot.features.technical.atr`), read at bar t.
- **Filters on every strategy arm:** tier-1 news blackout (`in_blackout == 1`) drops the candidate; no new entry
  20:45-22:15 UTC (rollover: no filled probe at 21 UTC, cost model) or Friday after 19:00 UTC. Random controls use the
  same blackout and windows, so they are the null for the same conditions.
- **Costs (history and the R read):** cost model 2026-10-25, conservative $0.23/oz round trip (spread both legs +
  commission $0.06 + slippage upper CI), slippage never favourable, swap per rollover held. Stop-exit overshoot is
  charged as measured by arm S0R (section 4) once it reads; until then H-60 reports net R twice: overshoot 0 and
  overshoot $0.70 on stop exits.
- **One position per arm at a time unless max_open says more; style caps bind first** (ADR 0043 pots: scalp £50/day,
  <= 10 open, per trade £6.50; intraday £48/day, <= 7 open, per trade £45; swing £40/day, 1 open, per trade £40; host £198,
  host max open 18). Arm max_open values sum above the style caps on
  purpose: the style cap and the Thompson share decide, and every skipped candidate is logged with its propensity.
- **Random controls:** side and (where stated) entry from a seeded, restart-safe hash, identical in history and live:
  `u = int.from_bytes(sha256(f"goldbot-arms-2026-10-25|{arm_id}|{decision_close_utc:%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M}Z").digest()[:8], "big") / 2**64`;
  side = +1 if u < 0.5 else -1. No state, so a restart cannot change a draw; the history run uses the same function.
- **£ figures:** loss at the stop at 0.01 lot = stop $ x 0.752 (GBPUSD 1.33; the fills' measured 0.7469 is slightly
  lower), shown at the recent median ATR; "+cost" adds the $0.23 conservative round trip. Swap is extra on holds
  across 22:00 UTC.

### 1.1 S1 session-VWAP fade (new rule, fully written)
- Anchor: the first 5m bar at or after London open (08:00 Europe/London) each weekday. VWAP_t = sum(w x tp) / sum(w)
  and sigma_t = sqrt(sum(w x (tp - VWAP_t)^2) / sum(w)) over the bars anchor..t, tp = (high + low + close) / 3 on mid,
  w = `tick_count`. Defined from the 6th bar after the anchor; no signal after 20:00 UTC.
  - *Note 2026-10-26 (code correction, not an amendment; quant review passed):* until commit 8d777955 the code read
    the cut on the close's UTC minute of day, so on London winter time (GMT, where the London day is the UTC day) the
    23:55 UTC bar, closing at 00:00 UTC, was wrongly admitted for S1A/S1B (under ~2% of S1 signals; never on BST). The
    code now follows the rule above. Live and shadow rows decided on a 00:00 UTC close since 2026-10-25 are void as
    evidence (reason `spec-bug-midnight`, `scripts/exclude_spec_bug_midnight.py`); arm ids and hashes are unchanged.
- Long: close_t < VWAP_t - 2 sigma_t and close_{t-1} >= VWAP_{t-1} - 2 sigma_{t-1} (the first close outside). Short
  mirrored on the upper band. At most one signal per side per 6 bars.
- S1A target is the VWAP_t price fixed at entry; skipped if it is < 1.0 R away, capped at 3.0 R.
- Mechanism: intraday liquidity-provision reversal of order-flow overshoots (Grossman-Miller 1988 inventory model;
  VWAP as institutional execution benchmark). Weak, unreplicated for gold at this horizon: exploration only.

### 1.2 S2 opening-range break (new rule)
- Range: high/low of the first 3 (S2A) or 6 (S2B) 5m bars starting at London open (08:00 Europe/London) and at NY
  (08:30 America/New_York). Break: within 18 bars after the range ends, the first 5m close > range high (long) or
  < range low (short); one trade per session per arm. Stop at the range mid (H + L) / 2; kept if the distance is
  $3.00-8.00. Hard flat at 20:00 UTC.
- Mechanism: session-open order flow and stop clusters at the opening extremes (Osler 2005). Note our session_open
  15m family showed no gross edge (Q4 2026): the 5m structural-stop form is the only new element.

### 1.3 S5 EMA pullback (new rule)
- 15m trend from the last **closed** 15m bar at the 1m decision time: up when EMA20(15m) > EMA50(15m) and
  close(15m) > EMA50(15m); down mirrored; otherwise no signal.
- Long (trend up): low_t <= EMA20(1m)_t, close_t > EMA20(1m)_t and close_t > open_t. Short mirrored. One signal per
  side per 10 bars.
- Mechanism: trend continuation after shallow pullbacks (TSMOM at the 15m scale, Moskowitz et al. 2012 applied
  intraday). No peer-reviewed support at 1m: exploration only.

### 1.4 S3 and S4 at 5m (existing specialists, constants unchanged)
- S3: `goldbot/specialists/sweep_absorb.py` `sweep_events` and `_scan` logic run on 5m bars: MAX_DEPTH_ATR 0.5,
  RECLAIM_BARS 2, MIN_GAP_BARS 4, STOP_BUFFER_ATR 0.25, stop 0.6-1.0 ATR(5m), London/NY window, blackout; the only
  changes are the timeframe, the $3-8 band, the target (1R / 2R) and hold 48 5m bars (= its 16 x 15m = 4 h clock).
- S4: `goldbot/specialists/box_break_close.py` `decide` on 5m bars with `smc.DEFAULT_PARAMS`: BREAK_DEPTH 0.2,

## 4. S0R resting-stop measurement arm (coordinator request; reading rule fixed now)

- **Question:** is the $0.70 mean stop-exit overshoot (n = 14, cost model caveat 1) exchange slippage or an artefact of
  a polled stop? Same random entries as S0 (seeded hash, its own arm id), but SL and TP are **sent with the order** as
  resting broker orders; the engine only adds the 30-bar time exit. Cost arm: no R read, no posterior, no graduation.
- **Measure:** per stop exit, overshoot = side x (stop price - fill price), adverse positive; also TP fill vs TP price.
- **Read once at n = 100 stop exits** (about 8 trading days at 26 entries a day, ~50% stopped):
  - 95% CI upper bound of mean overshoot < $0.10: the overshoot is a polling artefact. Action: every arm uses resting
    broker stops; the H-60 net R uses overshoot = 0; 1m arms stay at $3 stops.
  - mean overshoot > $0.30 with CI lower bound > $0.10: real. Action: H-60 charges the measured mean on every stop
    exit; 1m arms S0, S5A, S5B are suspended by amendment (their cost would pass 0.15 R at $3) and S0 moves to 5m.
  - otherwise: inconclusive; extend once to n = 200 and apply the same rule; no third look.
- Needs: the order path to accept a broker-side SL/TP on probe-only hosts (engine E7/E2; trading-safety review).

### 8.0 Conventions added to section 0 (bind every wave-2 arm)

- **Causality.** A level, band, pivot, block or anchor exists only from the close of the bar that confirms it (a k-bar
  pivot from bar p+k). Each rule is one function used by the history matrix and live, and its test proves **prefix
  invariance** (appending bars never changes a past candidate).
- **Indicators.** On mid prices of the arm's decision bars: ATR = Wilder ATR(14) (`goldbot.features.technical.atr`)
  read at the decision bar unless the rule says `ATR_{j-1}`; SMA/EMA/SD over closes, SD the population SD. A
  higher-timeframe value comes from the last **closed** bar of that timeframe via `asof_join` on `available_utc`.
- **Brackets.** 0.01 lot. Stop and target are fixed prices set at entry, searched on 1m bars, stop first; "no target"
  means stop and time exit only; time exit at the close of the hold-th decision bar after entry. A stop outside the
  arm's $ band is skipped and logged (`stop_gt_cap` / `stop_lt_floor`) with its propensity, never widened or resized.
- **Clocks.** Local exchange clocks via `zoneinfo` (Asia/Tokyo, Asia/Shanghai, Europe/London, America/New_York);
  trading day = NY date after +7 h (ends 17:00 New York). No entry on CME metals holidays or early-close sessions
  (`config/holidays_cme_metals.yaml`).
- **Common filters** as section 0 (tier-1 blackout; no entry 20:45-22:15 UTC or Friday after 19:00 UTC), plus no entry
  16:55-17:00 or 18:00-18:30 New York (slice A 1.2: reopen spread).
- **Costs on every read:** both nets of `prereg-2026-10-26-time-structure.md` section 0 (research prior; and the worse
  of $0.23 + $0.70 per stop exit or the arm's own signal-time cost), each plus the broker swap per 17:00 NY rollover
  held (Wednesday x3).
- **Read rule (fixed now; the plan's section 2.1 per family):** primary = pooled net R per trade at the measured cost,
  strata weighted equally, day-clustered (CR1) SE; one read at the 300th admissible family fill: **continue** only if
  P(mean > 0) > 1 − 0.05/m and the 2.1 consistency check holds; **stop** at −30 R within 300 fills, or on
  **futility** at 300 (one-sided 95% upper bound of the mean net R < +0.03 R); otherwise **inconclusive**, continue
  collecting with no second outcome read before 1,000. Every read reports family minus its control, paired by day. A
  read says at most *continue*; a family continues or stops as a whole, never a best stratum.
- **£ figures:** stop $ x 0.752 at the median ATRs of section 0. Caps are the plan's section 5 values (15m £17, 1h £30,
  swing £40 on shared hosts, scalp £6.50; on H4 `vantage-swing` only once it exists, at most £100); account caps,
  RiskGate and the exploration budget are unchanged.
