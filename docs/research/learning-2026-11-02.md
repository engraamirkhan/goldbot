# Learning class registration 2026-11-02 (demo only, unqualified)

Owner, 2026-11-02: "fix the limits of our system to trade ... for the system to be not bound to test, learn and improve
signals". Asked whether a demo "learning" class should exist beside the qualification rule (ADR 0061), the owner
answered YES. This document pre-registers the arms of that class, their controls and their stops, before their first
learning fill. Rule: ADR 0061 amendment 2026-11-02; engine and limits: the demo-limits ADR of the same day.

## What a learning arm is, and is not

- A `role: learning` row of `config/exploration_arms.yaml` enters on a demo probe_only account **without** a history
  qualification, inside its `learning` block: this prereg, its own K row in the trial registry (`k_id`, the config hash
  of `research.arms.learning_k_config`), a matched random control (same timeframe, stop, target and hold), a loss cap
  (20 x its per-trade cap) and an end at most 45 days away.
- It is **not** evidence of an edge. Its fills never qualify a strategy (a qualification citing a learning K row is
  refused at load), never enter a family ranking (the perf report and the daily review give it its own section), are
  never allocated by the bandit and never train a style model. A promising read is a lead for a pre-registered history
  qualification (screen 2010-2017, confirm 2018-2025-09), nothing more.
- Minimum lot, one unit: a learning arm with growth sizing or pyramid adds is refused. At most 6 learning positions
  are open on an account at once (`learning_max_open`).

## Stops (the engine, `engine/exploration_learning.py`; whichever comes first)

1. **Read due**: 300 admissible fills (filled, closed by its own barrier, P&L recorded) in the host's ledger. The arm
   stops entering; the read (arm net R against its control on the same risk day x session x side) is the evaluator's,
   and it decides only whether a history qualification is worth a registry trial.
2. **Loss cap**: cumulative net P&L at or below minus `loss_cap_gbp`; a fill whose P&L is unreadable counts as a loss
   at its stop plus its round trip, never 0.
3. **End**: past `until` (2026-12-17, 45 days from registration).

A dedicated control stops once none of the arms it serves may enter. A shared control that was pre-registered on its
own (ZCD, ZCV: H-81's controls) keeps its own role. An unreadable ledger stops every learning entry until a read
succeeds (fail closed). The H-61 and H-81 stops in `config/arm_stops.yaml` still apply on top.

## K and the budget

22 K rows (15 arms + 7 dedicated controls), family `learning_class`, status `learning`, written by
`scripts/record_learning_k_rows.py` on the brain before the first learning fill. Each is a trial: it counts in K and in
the 2026Q4 budget. The script checks the budget under the registry lock and writes nothing if the rows do not fit;
raising the budget is the owner's decision. Until the rows exist every learning arm and dedicated control is refused
(fail closed).
