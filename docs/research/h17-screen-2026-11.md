## 4. What it means for opportunity days (researcher's reading, not a verdict change)

- **Ex-ante range does not select direction.** On HAR-high days the first noise-boundary breach continues no better
  than chance (gross -0.003 R), and *worse* than on HAR-low days (-0.11 R a week, not significant). This is the third
  15m read of the same mechanism with no gross edge (R-02 +0.042 R, R-05 NY -0.006 R, now -0.003 R on range-selected
  days). The Zarattini, Aziz and Barbon (2024) SPY noise-area result (single paper, SPY, net Sharpe 1.33) does not
  transfer to spot XAUUSD 2012-2017, consistent with SoA A4: no close auction, no gamma-hedging flow.
- **The runner did not rescue it either:** V1's gross (+0.019 R) is within noise of V0's. Kaminski and Lo (2014) say
  trailing exits pay only with serial correlation; this sample shows none after the breach.
- **Costs were not the binding constraint.** At 2012-2017 ATR the cost of record is 0.35 R, which alone would kill a
  +0.2 R edge; but with gross at zero, no ATR level turns it positive (a larger ATR
  lowers the cost in R in proportion, but there is nothing gross for it to uncover).
- **What survives in this area:** the HAR-only range forecast as a **size and stand-aside input** (BACKLOG 30b),
  never as an entry selector. Its value has to be shown on a money target (net R of a fixed bracket or USD range in
  cost multiples by forecast tercile), as noted after H-16; that would be a new pre-registration with a new id.
