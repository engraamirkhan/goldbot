Strategy board: families across timeframes, progress to read, anneal state, verdicts vs control.** Value: the owner thinks in strategies; this shows each family across timeframes, progress to its read, its annealing state and, after the read, its verdict against its control (docs/design/trades-ux-2026-10-27.md §2.3, §3.4). Fields: `GET /api/strategies/board` (family_label, style, variants[timeframe, arm_id, is_control, n_counted, n_read_at, fills_today, fills_7d, eta_read_date, band, anneal_state, open_now] and, after the read only, verdict, net_r_per_trade, ci90, hit_rate, vs_control_r, net_gbp, since_read). Acceptance: quant-reviewer signs the payload before build (ADR 0050); no pre-read aggregate in the payload (seeded leak test); ordering by state and progress, never by result; engine strategies show full figures with n and 90% CI; lab totals only with 3+ arms. Lifecycle: find an edge (learning progress, which strategies earn vs control). Depends: 129; quant sign-off of the payload. TRACEABILITY: none yet; implementer adds a U row. Learning progress at the top: arms active / read / retired and fills counted toward reads this week, across all families. Tests: a seeded read arm shows its recorded verdict, net R per trade with 90% CI and the difference vs its control in R with its CI; a seeded unread arm shows only n of its read, ETA, band and anneal state; an engine strategy shows net £ and net R with n and CI; a family is one row with its timeframes as columns (15m, 1h, 4h, 1d), so the owner reads one strategy across timeframes.
