## 3. Money implications of the demo ceilings (ADR 0032, and ADR 0031 as the controller inside them)

Per-account ceiling for `icm-demo` only; at £1,000 equity. **On real money: none. Live and tiny-live may only tighten**
(`clamp` in `goldbot/risk/limits_profile.py` caps every non-demo account at its default; I read the code, did not run it).

| Limit | Default today | Demo ceiling | £ at £1,000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Account daily loss | 2% (and £50 absolute) | 5% | **£20 -> £50 maximum a day** (the £50 absolute still wins above £1,000, ADR 0004) |
| Account weekly loss | 5% | 10% | **£50 -> £100 a week** |
| Per-trade risk with the 0.01-lot exception | 0.5% target, refused above 1.2x | up to 1% | up to **£10 a trade** (2x the 0.5% cap); a gap or slippage can exceed the stop |
| Supervisor combined, all-demo | 1.5% / 4% | 3.75% / 7.5% | **£15 -> £37.50 a day, £40 -> £75 a week** across accounts, and binds before the per-account 5% / 10% |
| Revert | | sticky at 8% (size-down) | £80 drawdown reverts to defaults until the owner re-arms; 12% (£120) halts |

Reading: the maximum demo loss is **£50 in a day, £100 in a week, about £120 before the halt**; no real money is at risk.
The ceilings are 2.5x (daily) and 2x (weekly) the defaults, so a bad demo week costs a full 10% of the demo account.
The weekly £100 ceiling sits above the 8% revert (£80): the revert, not the weekly cap, is the binding stop in a bad
week. The research-director's objection stands: the caps raise trade count, not evidence of edge, and a daily £50 is
5% of equity, an upper bound no documented gold edge sustains (`north-star-feasibility-2026-10-18.md`). Demo results
under raised limits must not be read as evidence for tiny-live; ADR 0032 wires that exclusion (`counts_as_default_evidence`
in `gates_phase.evaluate_gates`, tests in `tests/test_phase_gate_limits_profile.py`; I did not run them).
ADR 0031's pre-registered exploration stop (cumulative -10R on exception trades, about £60 at £6 an R, cap off for good)
bounds the cost of discovering whether the exception works.
