- BACKLOG 135, MT5 bridge close priority (ADR 0056, protocol v7). The bridge now accepts requests concurrently and runs each terminal connection's calls on one worker from a priority queue: closes and stop changes first, then /health, reads, and entry orders last; a request whose client went is never run, a read queued past its client's timeout (`timeout_s`, sent by the brain from v7) is dropped, an order queued past 10 s is refused stale. Opt-in per account (`order_lane: true` in config/accounts.yaml, off everywhere today): an order lane, a child process of the bridge with its own terminal connection that sends entries only, so ticks, closes, the Friday cut and the kill switch keep flowing while the terminal sends one (tests show < 5 s with an entry held 35 s). A lane that dies with an order gives no reply (ambiguous, settled by the BACKLOG 72 look-up, never re-sent) and stays down.
