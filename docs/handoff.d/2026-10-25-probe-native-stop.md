- Mirror cost probes now run at the strategy's own stop and hold, or are skipped (architect trace 2026-10-25: "mirror probes must be evidence of the strategy"). `goldbot/engine/cost_probe.py` `_send`: a challenger whose own stop loses more than the probe's `max_loss` (the configured `cost_probe.max_loss`, or the dynamic budget share when lower) at the minimum lot is journalled `cost_probe_refused:native_stop_above_cap` (with `native_stop_loss`, `probe_max_loss`, `basis`) and sent nothing; the old clamp (`CLAMP_MARGIN`) is gone. When it fits, the gate is asked for the agent's own `stop_atr`, so the order's stop is the shadow trade's. RiskGate stays the only path; minimum lot, budget, probe_only fencing and the stops-level re-check (`probe_loss_above_cap`) are unchanged.
- The hold: `PROBE_ONLY_MAX_BARS` (4) is removed; every mirror probe holds its shadow trade's `max_bars` (the candidate's own when it carries one) on every account. Scheduled M1 probes are unchanged (`hold_s` 30).
- Expect far fewer probes on `icm-probe` until the ceiling moves: a family whose own stop is wider than about $5.3/oz (£4 at 0.01 lot; ATR15 is ~$8) is now skipped instead of clamped. Raising `cost_probe.max_loss` (e.g. for a £10k learning account) is ADR 0037 amendment 6, to be added as a section of `docs/decisions/0037-demo-cost-probe.md` (amendments are sections there, not ADR files); this change also supersedes the clamp text of the 2026-10-19 amendment (its lines on the clamped stop and the 99% aim) and that amendment should say so.
- Skipped probes are recorded (quant review HIGH): each `native_stop_above_cap` skip writes a `SkipRecord` to the new additive aux table `cost_probe_skips` (migration 15) with agent, version, timeframe, side, taken, ATR, native loss and the ceiling. Not in `cost_probes`: no order went out, and that table feeds costs-v2, activity, the broker ledger, the shadow report, the rotation and the untaken cap. The dynamic probe count's median reads the last 20 attempts, sent and skipped, by signal time, so wide own stops lower the count again.
- Rule for reading mirror probes (quant review MEDIUM): sent probes are a tight-stop, lower-volatility, shorter-timeframe sample of each strategy; their costs and outcomes are split by agent x timeframe and read beside `cost_probe.skip_counts` (skips per agent x timeframe with median ATR and native loss), never pooled. Every probe decision now carries `timeframe`, `atr_usd` and `native_stop_loss`.
- Edge label (safety review LOW): a loss above the cap at the strategy's own stop found in `_place` (the gate did not widen the stop) is also `native_stop_above_cap` and recorded as a skip; `probe_loss_above_cap` stays for a stop the gate floored wider.
