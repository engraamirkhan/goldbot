## 0. Cost model used below and the £50 arithmetic (read first)

- **Round trip, $/oz** (`config/settings.yaml` `costs`, `goldbot/execution/costs.py`): spread median ~0.34 (0.19 of a
  mean 15m ATR of $1.80, scalping note §0) + commission 2 x 3.5 USD/lot / 100 oz = 0.07 + slippage prior 0.15 per side
  = 0.30 -> **0.71 $/oz**. In R it is 0.71 / stop: **0.26 R at a 2.7 USD stop (1.5 ATR15), 0.18 R at 3.9 USD (2.25
  ATR15), 0.28 R at 2.5 USD (1 ATR30)**. Swap is zero for intraday holds (flat 16:30 New York). Take-profit as a
  limit order pays no exit slippage; stops do, so the true cost is a little under these figures (conservative).
- **Break-even win rate** with target b R and stop 1 R: p = (1 + c) / (1 + b).
- **£50/day is not reachable on average by any of these.** At `risk_per_trade` 0.005, 0.02 lot at a 2.7 USD stop
  risks ~£4.1, so £50 is ~12 R net in a day; at a 3.9 USD stop size rounds to 0.01 lot (~£3) and £50 is ~16 R. With
  0.5-3 trades/day and a net edge of 0.05-0.15 R the mean is £0.2-2/day; £50 needs a 12-16 R day. The lever is risk
  per trade (owner decision, flagged, not assumed) and selecting opportunity days.

## N1. Time-of-day periodicity of 30-minute returns (session-conditioned; separate group in O-4)

- **Mechanism.** Recurring institutional flows (fix/auction hedging, fund rebalancing, Asian physical demand, US
  data and ETF creation windows) make the sign of a given half-hour slot persist across days: the same slot's mean
  return over the last 5-20 days predicts today's slot. Heston, Korajczyk & Sadka 2010, *J. Finance* 65(4):
  NYSE stocks, 2001-2005, 30-min bins, gross; Bogousslavsky 2016, *J. Finance* 71(6) (infrequent rebalancing, same
  data class, gross); Breedon & Ranaldo 2013, *JMCB* 45(5) (FX hour-of-day effects, 2005-2010, gross; closest
  to gold). Single-market-class evidence; **never tested on XAUUSD or net of CFD costs** -> grade S/untested.
- **Timeframe.** Signal on 30m slots (aggregated from 15m bars), entry at the slot open, hold 2 x 15m bars, flat by
  16:30 New York. Candidate slots 07:00-16:00 UTC only (spread ~0.15-0.18 ATR15).
- **Features (6 columns, separate group `tod_periodicity`, not inside `activity`).** `slot_mean_ret_{5,10,20}d`,
  `slot_hit_rate_20d`, `slot_t_20d`, `slot_ret_over_atr`: same slot on earlier days only; `available_utc` = the
  prior day's slot close; a 15m bar maps to the 30m UTC slot containing its open; `asof_join`; `lookahead_check` 0
  columns. Written as a dated amendment in `preregistration-2027Q1.md` (O-4).
- **Label/exit.** O-4's 15m label unchanged (barrier 3.0/1.5 ATR, 16 bars, flat 16:30 NY); this group only adds columns.
- **Cost.** 0.26 R; break-even p = 1.26/3 = **0.42** (target 2 R). A usable slot needs gross >= ~0.35-0.50 R (scalping
  note §0.2 table); published HKS effects are bps-scale per slot, so the prior it clears this is **low**; its value is
  cheap screening (one group).
- **K.** **0 registry trials**; K_eff ~16 -> ~17, gate-1 base ~66 -> ~67 (83 is the Q2 projection, `hypotheses.md` C).
  Group map not yet pinned, so it goes in before the pin. Owner flag: none.
- **Kill.** Group not in any fold-stable top set of O-4's stability selection, or any O-5/O-6 net <= 0 -> retire
  "slot periodicity at 30m" for gold (R-10 on the verdict). No pre-check is run (a summary of past slot returns is an outcome read, `program-continuous.md`).

## N2. Edge-decay (re-scored) exit for O-4/O-5 survivors (exits/position management; conditional)

- **Mechanism.** A fixed barrier ignores that the model's edge decays within the hold; once the re-scored P(win)
  from out-of-fold in-trade scores falls below the live break-even (1 + c')/(1 + b'), with b' from the remaining
  barrier distances and c' the marginal exit cost only (entry is sunk; labels already pay spread), the position has negative expected value over its remaining path; closing it recovers R that a
  time barrier gives back. Optimal-stopping logic: Leung & Li 2015, *Optimal Mean Reversion Trading* (World
  Scientific, OU model, simulation + ETF pairs, gross) *(unverified)*; dynamic exits in López de Prado 2018, *AFML*
  ch. 3 (label design, no cost evidence); stops pay only under serial correlation, Kaminski & Lo 2014, *JFM* 18
  *(unverified)*. Exits-versus-edge evidence is weak and mostly simulated: **grade S**. It cannot create an edge
  (R-07), only keep more of one.
- **Timeframe/features.** 15m; the survivor's own <= 39 columns recomputed each bar, plus `bars_held`, `unreal_R`
  (position state, available at the bar close). Same `asof_join` discipline; no new data.
- **Label/exit.** Exit at the first bar where re-scored p < break-even or the original barrier/time/16:30 NY. Exit at
  the next bar open. The threshold is derived (not tuned) from the stated b', c'.
- **Cost.** Labels charge the full 0.71 $/oz round trip; an early exit is charged its own bar's spread plus 0.15
  slippage (market exit).
- **Trades/day.** Same as the parent (O-4 top tercile 1-3/day); illustration only: at entry break-even p = 0.42 (b = 2, c = 0.26).
- **K.** **1 registry trial in Q2**, only after O-5/O-6 pass (not just O-4 continue) and by a written research-director note
  from the free reserve; K +1. Never run on O-4's data to choose it (IC-7 MFE rule).
- **Kill.** Parent stop/inconclusive -> not funded. Funded: net R not > parent's by >= 0.03 R on the same events, or
  design gates fail -> retire "model-rescored exit". If parent AUC ~0.50 (R-07), the re-score is noise: stop.

## N3. Metals and USD confirmation of trend-day entries (intermarket gate on H-17; conditional on data)

- **Mechanism.** A gold trend day driven by the common metals/USD factor (silver and EURUSD/USDJPY moving the
  same way) is more persistent than a gold-only move (idiosyncratic order-flow or dealer hedging that reverts).
  Lucey & Tully 2006, *Applied Financial Economics* 16 *(unverified)* (gold-silver link, 1978-2002, daily, no costs);
  Baur & McDermott 2010, *J. Banking & Finance* 34(8) (gold-USD relation, 1979-2009, no costs). Daily evidence only;
  **intraday persistence conditioned on breadth is untested**: grade S.
- **Timeframe.** 15m, H-17's entries (09:00 London .. 10:30 New York), runner 4.5/2.25 ATR, flat 16:30 NY.
- **Features (<= 8, inside H-17's cap).** `xag_ret_{4,8}_bars_over_atr`, `eur_ret_{4,8}`, `jpy_ret_{4,8}`,
  `xau_xag_ret_corr_16`, `breadth_agree` (sign agreement count 0-3); each bar-closed, `asof_join` on `available_utc`.
  **Data cost:** XAGUSD, EURUSD, USDJPY 15m are not in the data release (D+ pull, Dukascopy via `data-dukascopy.yml`);
