- goldbot-deploy: what ran before runs after (incident 2026-10-20, docs/incidents/2026-10-20-deploy-rollback-left-services-stopped.md). The units active before the deploy are recorded first; a failed restart no longer ends the start loop (it used to `return 1` at the first failure, so one engine whose bridge was not logged in left api, scheduler, telegram, news and tv-pull stopped through the rollback too). A rollback starts only those units, and after any rollback every one of them that is not active is started again; the record ends `running: ...; not running: ...`. A newly enabled engine (not active before) is started last with `--no-block` and judged on its own: down with a bridge check not ok (`bridge:`/`terminal:`/`bridge_compat:<account>`) is `deployed_degraded` "engine@<account> blocked by its bridge (<check>)", down with its bridge ok rolls back (and is not restarted by the rollback). An engine active before and down after still rolls back. Tests: tests/test_goldbot_deploy_sh.py (incident scenario, rollback restarts, watcher).
