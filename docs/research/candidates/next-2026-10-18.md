## 0. Cost model used below and the £50 arithmetic (read first)

- **Round trip, $/oz** (`config/settings.yaml` `costs`, `goldbot/execution/costs.py`): spread median ~0.34 (0.19 of a
  mean 15m ATR of $1.80, scalping note §0) + commission 2 x 3.5 USD/lot / 100 oz = 0.07 + slippage prior 0.15 per side
  = 0.30 -> **0.71 $/oz**. In R it is 0.71 / stop: **0.26 R at a 2.7 USD stop (1.5 ATR15), 0.18 R at 3.9 USD (2.25
  ATR15), 0.28 R at 2.5 USD (1 ATR30)**. Swap is zero for intraday holds (flat 16:30 New York). Take-profit as a
  limit order pays no exit slippage; stops do, so the true cost is a little under these figures (conservative).
- **Break-even win rate** with target b R and stop 1 R: p = (1 + c) / (1 + b).
- **£50/day is not reachable on average by any of these.** At `risk_per_trade` 0.005 a trade risks ~£5 (0.01-lot
  granularity: a 2.7 USD stop = 0.02 lot is ~£4), so £50 is ~10 R net on one day. With 0.5-3 trades/day and a net edge
  of 0.05-0.15 R the mean is £0.2-2/day; £50 occurs only on a runner/trend day (R-multiple >= 5 with 2x risk). Say so in
  every report; the lever is risk per trade (owner decision, flagged, not assumed) and selecting opportunity days.

## N1. Time-of-day periodicity of 30-minute returns (session-conditioned; group inside O-4)

- **Mechanism.** Recurring institutional flows (fix/auction hedging, fund rebalancing, Asian physical demand, US
  data and ETF creation windows) make the sign of a given half-hour slot persist across days: the same slot's mean
  return over the last 5-20 days predicts today's slot. Heston, Korajczyk & Sadka 2010, *J. Finance* 65(4):
  NYSE stocks, 2001-2005, 30-min bins, gross; Bogousslavsky 2016, *J. Finance* 71(6) (infrequent rebalancing, same
  data class, gross); Breedon & Ranaldo 2013, *JMCB* 45(5) (FX hour-of-day effects, 2005-2010, gross; closest
  to gold). Single-market-class evidence; **never tested on XAUUSD or net of CFD costs** -> grade S/untested.
- **Timeframe.** Signal on 30m slots (aggregated from 15m bars), entry at the slot open, hold 2 x 15m bars, flat by
  16:30 New York. Candidate slots 07:00-16:00 UTC only (spread ~0.15-0.18 ATR15).
- **Features (6 columns, one group `tod_periodicity`; all point-in-time).** `slot_mean_ret_5d`, `_10d`, `_20d`,
  `slot_hit_rate_20d`, `slot_t_20d`, `slot_ret_over_atr`: each uses only the same slot on days strictly before
  today, so known at the slot open; joined to bars with `asof_join` on `available_utc` (slot close + 0 s);
  `pipeline.lookahead_check` must report 0 columns. Fits the 39-column + `side` cap of O-4.
- **Label/exit.** O-4's 15m label unchanged (barrier 3.0/1.5 ATR, 16 bars, flat 16:30 NY); this group only adds columns.
- **Cost.** 0.26 R; break-even p = 1.26/3 = **0.42** (target 2 R). A usable slot needs gross >= ~0.35-0.50 R (scalping
  note §0.2 table); published HKS effects are bps-scale per slot, so the prior it clears this is **low**; its value is
  cheap screening (one group).
- **Trades/day.** 0.5-1.5 after a |slot_t_20d| >= 2 gate.
- **K.** **0 registry trials**; K_eff +1 group inside O-4 (K ledger ~83 -> ~84). Must be added to O-4's group map

## N2. Edge-decay (re-scored) exit for O-4/O-5 survivors (exits/position management; conditional)

- **Mechanism.** A fixed barrier ignores that the model's edge decays within the hold; once the re-scored P(win)
  from the entry model falls below the live break-even (1 + c)/(1 + b) = 0.42 (with the remaining distance
  re-priced), the position has negative expected value net of the exit cost; closing it recovers R that a
  time barrier gives back. Optimal-stopping logic: Leung & Li 2015, *Optimal Mean Reversion Trading* (World
  Scientific, OU model, simulation + ETF pairs, gross) *(unverified)*; dynamic exits in López de Prado 2018, *AFML*
  ch. 3 (label design, no cost evidence); stops pay only under serial correlation, Kaminski & Lo 2014, *JFM* 18
  *(unverified)*. Exits-versus-edge evidence is weak and mostly simulated: **grade S**. It cannot create an edge
  (R-07), only keep more of one.
- **Timeframe/features.** 15m; the survivor's own <= 39 columns recomputed each bar, plus `bars_held`, `unreal_R`
  (position state, available at the bar close). Same `asof_join` discipline; no new data.
- **Label/exit.** Exit at the first bar where re-scored p < break-even or the original barrier/time/16:30 NY. Fits
  on training folds only; the threshold is `cost/ (1+b)`-derived, not tuned (0 free parameters).
- **Cost.** Each early exit pays the spread again only if market-closed: charge the full 0.71 $/oz round trip plus
  nothing extra (exit at the bid/ask is already inside it); stop slippage 0.15 on early exits.
- **Trades/day.** Same as the parent (O-4 top tercile 1-3/day); break-even p unchanged at 0.42 (b = 2, c = 0.26).
- **K.** **1 registry trial in Q2**, only after the parent reads `continue` and by a written research-director note

- **Kill.** Parent stop/inconclusive -> not funded. Funded: net R not > parent's by >= 0.03 R on the same events, or
