# 0062: XAUUSD247 weekend lane

Status: proposed (principal engineer, 2026-10-31; trading-safety and quant review pending)

## Context
Vantage launched XAUUSD247 in 2026-07. It is a gold CFD that quotes all week: 1 oz a lot, no commission (spread and
swap only). It is listed on a Standard STP account, not on vantage-demo's Raw ECN server, which lists XAUUSD+ only.
The owner opened a Standard STP demo and stored its login and password in the Vantage box keyring
(`mt5-login-vantage-247`, `mt5-vantage-247`). Gold is closed at the weekend, so the system learns nothing from Friday
night to Sunday night. A weekend lane would add demo learning in hours that are otherwise idle.

The engine assumes weekday gold in five places, all hard-wired:
- the session table (closed Friday night to Sunday night);
- the bar builders (they drop every minute that table calls closed);
- the Friday 21:30 server-time cut;
- the cost probe's weekend refusal;
- exploration's Friday-cut hold refusal.

Evidence, costs and learner weights are keyed by broker. A second Vantage account under `broker: vantage` would
therefore pool weekend XAUUSD247 fills with vantage-demo's weekday XAUUSD+ fills:
- `jobs.broker_cost_table` picks whichever account on that broker has the most fills;
- the learner runs once per broker.

## Decision
1. **Per-account calendar, opt-in.** Account `sessions: always_open` (with the broker's own `pauses`, UTC, default
   none) and `weekend_only` with a `weekend_window` (default Fri 21:00 to Sun 21:00 UTC) reach the engine as
   `EngineConfig.always_open`, `pauses` and `weekend_window`.
   - Every account without them keeps its table, its bar builders (`Engine.bar_sessions` is `DEFAULT_SESSIONS`
     unless always-open), its Friday cut and its probe and exploration refusals, byte for byte. The tests pin this.
   - The always-open table never reads the broker's session list: the MT5 adapter reports weekday gold hours, which
     would close it.
2. **The weekend rule of an always-open account** (`goldbot/engine/weekend_lane.py`):
   - there is no Friday cut;
   - outside the window, entries are blocked (`AccountState.weekend`, the gate's existing `weekend` reason);
   - past the flat cut, every position of the engine is closed at market, winners too (`weekend_lane_flat`). The
     close is retried every refresh until done, once per cut, and is persisted as `_weekend_done`. No quote is
     needed, so the no-quote pass flattens at once;
   - no exploration hold may reach the cut;
   - scheduled cost probes run inside the window only.
   Exits are never gated.
3. **No pooling.** vantage-247 gets its own broker key `vantage247` (settings `brokers.vantage247`), its own store
   symbol XAUUSD247 (`Account.store_symbol`, read first by `release_symbol`), its own magic range (260700), cost
   table (`state/costs_vantage-247.json`), day (£20) and arms. Load refuses:
   - an always-open account on a live account;
   - an always-open account without its own store symbol, or with XAUUSD as its store symbol;
   - a weekend_only account with a strategy arm (only arms with a control_share are allowed);
   - a broker key shared by an always-open account and a weekday account (`pooled_brokers`).
4. **Arms.** The arms are two random controls under new ids:
   - WKS0: S0's geometry, 1m;
   - WKI0: I0's geometry, 15m.
   They fire at Saturday and Sunday UTC closes (`random_bracket`'s new `weekdays` param; every existing arm's rule and
   hash are unchanged). They are `forward_only`, so the history matrix never runs them on XAUUSD bars, and
   `role: control`, so they are exempt from qualification (ADR 0061).
   Their two registry trials (family arms_weekend_247) are recorded by the research director against the quarter's
   budget before `enabled: true`. This change records nothing.
5. **Bridge v8.** It adds a read-only, optional `symbols_get(pattern)` and SymbolInfo's swap and margin fields (for
   reporting only), used by `goldbot.ops.symbol_check`. That check confirms the symbol exists, has the declared
   contract size and has trading enabled. MIN_COMPATIBLE stays 5.
6. **Second terminal on the Vantage box.** `mt5_bootstrap.sh --instance Vantage247 --port 8766 --beside vantage-demo`
   runs `mt5-second-terminal.sh`. It installs the terminal at `C:\MT5\Vantage247` in the same Wine prefix, with no
   apt, Wine or Python step. It also writes per-instance systemd drop-ins (`mt5-instance.sh`):
   - the install dir, the guard, the AutoTrading edit and the bridge wait, all scoped to that install;
   - the watchdog on port 8766;
   - the same guards narrowed to `ICMarkets` for vantage-demo.
   The shared unit templates do not change for any other box. The tunnel key's line forwards to every bridge port
   of the box (`goldbot-mt5-authorize '<key>' 8765 8766`).
7. **Health.** The weekday checks call the weekend closed, so `weekend_247` covers the lane:
   - inside its window, the engine state must be fresh and ticking;
   - outside its window, the account must be flat 15 min after the cut.
   The daily bulk tick pull (`tick_record`) skips always-open accounts. Their engine records every tick
   (store `ticks`, source vantage-247, symbol XAUUSD247) and its 1m bars (`bars_1m_live`, source vantage247).

Options weighed:
- (a) **Reuse `broker: vantage`.** Rejected because of the broker-keyed pooling above.
- (b) **A second Unix user with its own Wine prefix.** This isolates the most, but the owner's credentials live in
  mt5's keyring file, and it needs a second deploy tree. Rejected.
- (c) **Edit the shared unit templates to take environment variables.** Rejected: every box's terminal and bridge
  would change, and the pinned guard lessons say no shell variables in the guards (incident 2026-10-19).
- (d) **Per-instance drop-ins.** Chosen.

## Consequences
- **Trading safety.** Weekday accounts are unchanged; the tests compare them with the Friday cut and the
  no-quote rule.
  - The lane cannot hold a position into the Monday reconvergence: entries are blocked outside the window, no hold
    may reach the cut, the flatten runs at the cut, and health FAILs a position still open 15 min after it.
  - The account stays `enabled: false` until its terminal, bridge, symbol check and registry rows exist.
  - Live trading of an always-open symbol is refused at load. Using it with money is an owner decision.
- **The window and the main session, in winter.** The window opens at Friday 21:00 UTC. In winter (US standard time)
  weekday gold trades until 22:00 UTC, so the lane's first hour overlaps the main session's last. This is intended and
  carries no risk: the lane holds no position across the reconvergence; only the opening overlaps.
- **Shared Wine prefix, Xvfb and wineserver (operations risk).** Both terminals run in one Wine prefix as one user,
  on one Xvfb display (:99) and one wineserver. A crash or hang of wineserver or Xvfb stops both terminals together,
  and their exits with them; broker-held stops stay. The same applies to a Wine upgrade, which is why `--instance`
  never upgrades Wine.
  - The watchdogs restart each terminal unit on its own; nothing restarts the shared processes beyond their units.
  - The box has about 1.2 GB available. The second terminal is refused below 500 MB.
  - A separate Unix user with its own prefix was rejected (option b); revisit if a shared crash is ever seen.
- **runner.py line cap.** To stay under it, the weekday Friday rule's body was moved verbatim from
  `Engine._weekend_rule` to `weekend_lane.weekday_rule`. `_weekend_rule` now delegates to it, and its tests are
  unchanged.
- **Research integrity.** WKS0 and WKI0 are controls only. They are never read for edge and never pooled with
  XAUUSD history, labels, learner weights (broker vantage247) or qualification.
  - They lose Friday night (21:00 to 24:00 UTC) because the rule takes whole UTC days.
  - Their Sunday closes after 21:00 are refused by the engine, and each refusal is journaled.
- **Operations.**
  - The data partitions are `bars_1m_live/source=vantage247/symbol=XAUUSD247` and
    `ticks/source=vantage-247/symbol=XAUUSD247`. The brief asked for `source=vantage`; the broker key decides this.
  - The daily state/ticks record does not cover the lane.
  - `mt5-box-upgrade.sh` refuses every phase on a box that has per-instance drop-ins, so the Vantage box is upgraded
    by hand until the script counts per install (follow-up).
  - Outside the window, the lane closes every own position whenever one exists, not only once per cut.
- **What would change this.** If Vantage lists XAUUSD247 on the Raw ECN server, the lane could move to vantage-demo's
  terminal. The broker key must stay separate even then.
