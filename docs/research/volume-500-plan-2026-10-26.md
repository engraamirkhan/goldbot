# 500+ fills a day per demo account: baseline, capacity arithmetic, learning value, annealing rule, rollout

Research director, 2026-10-26 20:10 UTC. Plan only. This file changes no config, code, registry or cap. Real-money
accounts (`icm-live`, `vantage-live`) are not touched. It builds on `arms-expansion-2026-10-26.md` (waves 1-3, 30
families), ADR 0047 (dynamic sleeve limits, tighten-only), `high-volume-learning-2026-10-25.md`, ADR 0042/0043 and
`goldbot/research/exploration_data.py`.

## 0. The directive and how it is read

Owner, 2026-10-26 (verbatim): "each account shuld trade 500 and plus a day, so the model has more and more experience
to learn fromm and as it becomes intelligent, highly profitable and high confidence, it will reduce the number of
trades as it will be able to make profits conssitently and high".

**Reading.**
- The 500 is a **capacity target for learning**, not a quota. It is met by adding *supply*: more arms, more decision
  points and cheaper, shorter trades. It is never met by loosening a rule threshold, a cap or a stop. An arm that fires
  3 times a day stays at 3. This is the H-61 principle.
- A fill counts toward the 500 only if it is **filled, attributable, labelled and not void**:
  - filled at the broker;
  - attributable: `arm_id`, `candidate_id`, `arm_config_hash` and the per-arm magic all reconcile to the broker deal;
  - labelled: `label_sim_r` resolved;
  - not void as evidence (no dq quarantine).
- Cost-probe fills count for at most **20%** of an account's 500. They teach cost, not direction.
- Reports show the count split into strategy arms, controls and probes, so the total cannot be padded.
- **The safety rails are not variables:**
  - the RiskGate is the only path to an order;
  - per-trade caps stay as they are;
  - 2% daily (`daily_loss_limit_abs` £220 / £220 / £200), the 5% daily ceiling and the 6% weekly ceiling;
  - `max_positions` 18, `max_open_total` 17 and the margin and notional caps;
  - ADR 0043's -8% pause and the 12% kill switch;
  - exits are never gated.
- The second half of the directive (fewer trades as confidence grows) is section 4. It is an **annealing rule** that
  steps exploration down only on measured, pre-registered confidence, arm by arm, to a floor that never reaches zero.

## 2. How to reach 500+ per account inside the caps

### 2.1 Units and the capacity inequality

- **Units.** 0.01 lot = 1 oz. $1 of price = £0.75 (GBPUSD 1.33). R = the loss at the stop.
- **The budget that binds first.** On every host it is the **exploration budget**, not the account's 2%:
  - `budget_share` × daily limit = 0.9 × £220 = **£198** (icm-demo and icm-probe), 0.9 × £200 = **£180** (vantage);
  - the engine counts today's **net** realised exploration loss (a win offsets a loss; a net profit adds no room:
    `realised_loss` floors at 0), plus every open position at its stop, plus the new trade.
- **The inequality.** For a host taking n fills a day of mixed types i (n_i fills, risk R_i, per-trade P&L SD
  s_i ≈ 1.0-1.3 R):
  - drift at zero gross edge: μ = n × c, with c the measured round trip;
  - SD: σ = sqrt(D × Σ n_i (s_i R_i)²). D ≥ 1 is the within-day correlation inflation; it is unmeasured, so D = 1 and
    D = 2 are both shown;
  - H = mean open risk at stops = Σ n_i × hold_i × R_i / session minutes;
  - the budget binds on a day when the running net loss reaches B − H. Its probability is the first-passage
    probability of a drifted random walk:
    P = Φ((μ − B′)/σ) + exp(2μB′/σ²) Φ((−B′ − μ)/σ), with B′ = B − H.

## 3. Learning value: when a fill is worth having

### 3.1 How a fill is used today (code paths)

- **Every candidate, taken or refused,** goes through `record_candidate` (`exploration_data.py:210`). Each row
  carries:
  - its propensity (`arm_share`, `branch`, `u`, `mu`, `pi_rand_arm`);
  - the meta-model version;
  - a frozen ≤ 40-feature snapshot as of `available_utc`.
- **The training label** is `label_sim_r`: a 1m bid/ask replay with the broker's own cost model (`label_candidates`).
  Refused candidates are therefore labelled too: **labels do not need fills** (ADR 0047 D5).
- **A fill adds four things a replay cannot:**
  1. the broker's real cost (spread, slippage per leg, commission, overshoot), which calibrates `ReplayCosts`;
  2. `label_fill_r` for the **parity** check of fill against replay (the off-policy gap);
  3. latency, rejections and stale signals;
  4. the **admissible fills** that H-61's single read counts: 300 fills on ≥ 20 session days.
- **The bandit** (`learn`, nightly) runs a Thompson NIG over each arm's `label_sim_r`, keyed by broker and style. The
  shares are bounded at a 5% floor and a 30% cap.
- **The meta-labeller** (H-62) retrains weekly once there are ≥ 1,500 new and ≥ 3,000 total admissible labels per
  (broker, style).
- **The daily review** counts candidates and fills per style against `daily_review.volume_plan`, and reports cost by
  session, refusals, arm health, H-61 stops and void evidence.

### 3.2 The minimum per arm-day for a useful read

Fills inside one day are correlated: same regime, overlapping holds. With an intraclass correlation ρ, n fills of one
arm in one day are worth n / (1 + (n − 1)ρ) independent fills, and that **saturates at 1/ρ**. At ρ = 0.05 (an
assumption, measured at step 1 by D4):

| Fills per arm-day | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 40 | 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Effective n | 4.2 | 6.9 | 8.7 | 10.3 | 13.4 | 16.8 |

- **Minimum useful:** about **10 fills per arm-day**. The day's cluster mean then has an SE of about 0.35 R, and the
  per-arm cost estimate reaches an SE of about $0.02 within about 3 days.
- **Target:** 15-20. H-61's read (300 fills on ≥ 20 session days) lands at day 15-20 either way. **Above about 20 per
  arm-day, the read date does not move** (the 20-day floor binds) and the information gain flattens.
- **So the 500 should buy breadth:** about 25-35 arms × 15-20 fills per host, not 5 arms × 100. This is why section 2
  adds arms, not rate.
- **H-62 is fed by candidates.** At about 500 scalp candidates a day, ICM (icm-probe and icm-demo pool: same cost basis)
  reaches the 3,000-label retrain floor in about 6 trading days. Vantage alone at 150 takes about 20.

### 3.3 A defect that volume makes worse

`learn` feeds the NIG posterior **per trade**. The high-volume plan specified **daily arm sums** to respect day
clustering.
- At 500 a day, a per-trade posterior is overconfident by about sqrt(1 + (n − 1)ρ): 2.2× at 20 fills/day, ρ 0.2.
- The bandit would therefore concentrate early on noise.
- **Fix before step 2:** the posterior uses day sums or a clustered variance (ml-researcher + quant-reviewer, D5).

## 4. The annealing rule: exploration steps down only on measured confidence

### 4.1 States per (broker, arm). Two pre-registered looks, no peeking

| State | Entered when | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **E (exploring)** | registration | normal exploration admission (ADR 0043 pots, ADR 0047 sleeves, Thompson share) |
| **C1 (candidate)** | **at H-61's single read** (the 300th admissible fill, ≥ 20 session days), every condition of 4.2 at the K-adjusted bar | none yet. The next 150 admissible fills are the fresh confirmation sample |
| **P (proven)** | **at the confirmation read** (150 new admissible fills, ≥ 10 new session days), conditions 4.2 with t ≥ 1.645 one-sided on the fresh sample only | the arm leaves the exploration budget's anneal base (4.3). It trades every signal its sleeve admits |
| **R (retired)** | the H-61 stop, or failing the read (H-61: not graduated at 300 → retired) | no entries. Re-entry only by a new pre-registration |

- Demotion runs from P back to E.
- **Exactly two looks per arm,** each on a fixed sample, each read once, by the evaluator against this rule. No
  nightly re-testing, so there is no optional-stopping inflation.
- **"Proven" is not "promoted".** Promotion to `icm-demo`'s roster or to real money still needs:
  - a new pre-registration;
  - gate 1 at the day's K (t 4.59 at K ≈ 358);
  - the holdout scored once;
  - the forward n ≥ 60 on the roster shadow agent (H-61 / high-volume plan section 4).

### 4.2 Conditions (all must hold at a look)

1. **Forward net profit with a CI above zero after the K-adjusted bar.**
   - The data: the arm's admissible fills since registration, net of each fill's **signal-time cost** (`label_fill_r`).
     The SE is CR1, clustered by UTC day.
   - At C1: t_cl ≥ z(1 − 0.05/m), where m is the number of strategy arms on that broker eligible at the read (H-61
     Bonferroni; m = 30 gives 2.94). A family read is pooled across its strata, with equal stratum weights and the
     consistency check of arms-expansion 2.1.
   - At P: t_cl ≥ 1.645 on the fresh 150 only.
   - In both looks, the arm minus its paired control (net R, paired by day) has a point estimate > 0.
2. **Cost at signal time.**
   - The 95% upper bound of the arm's round-trip cost per R (≥ 30 fills) is ≤ its type threshold: scalp 0.10,
     intraday 0.08, swing 0.02 (arms-expansion section 7).
   - **And** the mean net R recomputed with every fill's cost set to that upper bound is still > 0.
3. **Calibration.**
   - (a) **Replay parity:** mean(`label_fill_r` − `label_sim_r`) lies within ±max(0.03 R, 2 SE). The labels the
     model learns from must match the fills it trades.
   - (b) **The H-60 parity alarm is clear:** |live − backtest| ≤ 3 SE, where a history prior exists.
   - (c) **For a meta-filtered arm** (S6/I3): the calibration slope of served p on the look's fills is in
     [0.8, 1.25], and its Brier is ≤ the base-rate Brier.
4. **Admissibility.**
   - void, quarantined and held-out rows are excluded;
   - a refused-candidate label never counts as a fill;
   - fills taken under an ADR 0047 refusal regime are read with IPW, with the unweighted mean reported beside it.

### 4.3 Volume rule (per host, at the 00:00 UTC roll only)

Let B_h be the host's exploration budget in variance units, measured over the last 10 trading days:
Σ over the host's arms of fills/day × R².
- **π_h** = the share of B_h used by P arms.
- The anneal factor for **E arms** (never controls, never P arms) is k_h = clamp(1 − π_h, φ, 1), with **φ = 0.20**.
  Exploration never goes below 20% of its base. A floor of 0 is not allowed.
- **Rate limits.**
  - k_h may fall by at most **0.15 per week**: annealing, not a cliff.
  - It rises at the next roll, without limit, when a P arm is demoted.
  - It never rises above 1.
- **How k is applied: by arm-day rotation, not thinning.**
  - Each E arm is **active for the whole day** with probability p_a = k_h, so expected exploration volume scales by
    k_h. The draw is seeded per (arm, day), logged as the arm-day propensity, and multiplied into each candidate's
    propensity.
  - On an active day the arm runs at its full rate, so it keeps ≥ 10 fills/arm-day (3.2).
  - On an inactive day its candidates are still recorded and replay-labelled, with propensity 0. They are excluded
    from IPW, never from the label read (ADR 0047 D5).
- **Where the freed £ goes.** It stays in the pot (ADR 0047 D5 item 2), so P arms' signals are admitted more often,
  inside their per-trade cap, `max_open` and the Thompson cap of 0.30. **There is no size-up at 0.01 lot.** Size is a
  promotion matter.
- **Controls.** Random controls keep their fixed share whatever k is (ADR 0047 D3: the null is never scaled by
  results).
- **Demotion of a P arm** (back to E, its variance share returned to exploration at the next roll), when any of these
  holds:
  - its forward drawdown from peak reaches the ADR 0047 first step: −10 R scalp, −6 R intraday, −4 R swing;
  - condition 2 fails on a rolling 100 fills;
  - parity 3(a) fails on a rolling 100 fills.
  - A demoted arm can only be proven again by a **new** pre-registered read, on data after the demotion.
- **Missing or stale anneal decision.** The last valid one, if ≤ 36 h old. Else k_h = 1, the pre-registered default:
  full exploration, still inside every cap and under ADR 0047's sleeves. A `dq_event` is raised: `anneal_missing`.

**Expected path, illustrative and not a forecast.** Suppose 5 of 30 arms on a host reach P and use 40% of B_h. Then
k_h = 0.6, and the day is P arms at their natural rates plus 60% of exploration: about 350 instead of 560. If none
prove, volume stays at 500 and drift keeps costing about £58 a day. That is the honest price of learning, and §2.2
item 4 covers it.

### 4.4 Implementation shape (ties to ADR 0047)

- **A new ADR** is needed. ADR 0047 says any new factor or ladder change is a new ADR. Name: "exploration annealing".
  It adds a fifth admission factor, `k_anneal`, applied per E arm-day before `RiskGate.check`.
- **Code:**
  - a pure module `goldbot/risk/anneal.py`;
  - nightly rows in `sleeve_decisions`, append-only, the same job as ADR 0047's `sleeve_job`;
  - the reason `sleeve_anneal` in the journal;
  - the D6 demo-only guard.
- **Tests, failing first:**
  1. k ∈ [φ, 1], and k = 1 with no P arm.
  2. **Monotonicity.** For any input, the set admitted with annealing is a subset of the set admitted without it.
     Annealing can never admit a trade the caps would refuse.
  3. Controls are unaffected by k.
  4. The weekly fall is ≤ 0.15. A rise follows a demotion at the next roll.
  5. Exactly one C1 look at fill 300 and one P look at +150. Calling the evaluator again returns the stored decision.
  6. Void and quarantined rows are excluded.
  7. The day-clustered SE: duplicating fills within a day does not raise t.
  8. The cost-at-upper-bound recomputation.
  9. Arm-day propensities are logged, and an active day's expected fills are ≥ 10.
  10. The missing/stale fallback.
  11. A non-demo account refuses with `anneal_not_demo`.
  12. Restart-safe state from aux.db.
- **Reviews:** quant (4.2 constants, two-look design), trading-safety (admission path, monotonicity, demo guard), sre
  (the nightly job and its alert).

## 5. Rollout: steps, dates, gates

Every step is gated by **three sign-offs**:
- **SRE:**
  - L7 rule time ≤ 10 s per host per 15m close, and 1m-pass compute ≤ 2 s p99;
  - bridge order RTT p95 ≤ 1.5 s, and ≤ 1 retcode 10024 a day per terminal;
  - `exploration_stale` ≤ 2% of candidates;
  - Wine box RSS and swap-in stable over 24 h;
  - 0 engine incidents.
- **Trading-safety reviewer:**
  - the config diff raises no cap: `daily_loss_limit_abs`, ceilings, `max_loss`, `max_positions`, `max_open_total`
    and the `budget_share` totals;
  - the pots sum to ≤ the budget;
  - live accounts are disabled;
  - the RiskGate path and the exits are untouched.
- **Quant reviewer:**
  - void ≤ 5% of fills;
  - propensities on 100% of candidates;
  - parity within the ADR 0037 tolerance;
  - a prereg row for every new arm id before its first fill;
  - K and the Q4 override updated.

## 6. What I would stop

- **Counting candidates, or more than 20% probes, as "fills toward 500".**
