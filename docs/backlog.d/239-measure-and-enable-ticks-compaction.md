- **239. Measure tick part counts and compaction cost on the brain, then enable ticks.** Value: R146 ships the compaction of `ticks` but leaves it out of `compact.tables` because a month of one broker's ticks is about 10^7 rows and nothing was measured on the 4-core brain. Acceptance: run `python scripts/store_parts_report.py --data-root <data_root>` on the brain before (part counts, rows, MB, the largest closed month, read seconds, peak RSS per folder); run `compact_table(store, "ticks", before=now, ...)` once by hand on the largest closed month with the default budget (1 GB, 2 threads, 262,144 rows per group) and record wall time, peak RSS and the spill size; if a month fits inside `compact.time_box_s` with RSS < 1.5 GB, add `ticks` to `compact.tables` in config/settings.yaml; rerun the report after the first night and paste both into the PR.
  **Measured and enabled 2026-11-14.** Before (store_parts_report.py on the brain, footers): ticks 39 folders, 17,360 closed-month parts, **321,590 open-month parts**, 25.4M rows, 1,077 MB; decisions 3 closed / 10,504 open parts; dq_events 5 / 2,035. Timed reads (14 folders sampled before the run was stopped to free the brain's RAM): 139-174 s per folder whatever its size, peak RSS 4.95 GB (the read, not compaction). Trial on a copy of the largest closed month (icm-demo XAUUSD 2026-10: 5,736 parts, 1.38M rows) with the default budget: 10.3 s, peak RSS 1.1 GB (incl. two full reads), spill 0 MB, rows identical, written_utc kept for every row, 29 -> 12.5 MB, read 1.4 s. Within time_box_s and < 1.5 GB: `ticks` added to compact.tables (last). The brain has 2 cores, not 4 (comments fixed). The after-report runs after the first night. The open month dominates: BACKLOG 260.
