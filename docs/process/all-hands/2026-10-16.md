# All-hands — 16 Oct 2026 (called by the owner): performance and efficiency

From the program director, to all 18 roles. **Every lane brief from today starts with this memo**; the
release manager and the product owner hold work to it alongside `docs/AGENT_STANDARDS.md`.

## Where we are

- PR #79 (88 commits) merged today: CI green, security PUSH-OK (0 secrets in 15k added lines),
  trading-safety MERGE-OK. The owner-history CLI golden broke CI first (#80) — see shortfall 4.
- Demo deploy is mid-way; the engine starts Sun 18 Oct 22:02 UTC, gated on BACKLOG 59's re-verify.
- The review loop caught real defects before they could cost money: the gate-1 discovery/holdout
  bypass (HIGH, quant review 16 Oct), the kill-switch narrowing under a failed write (F1, MEDIUM,
  trading-safety 16 Oct), and the machine path in a public golden.

## What went well — keep doing it

- Verdicts grounded in evidence: reviews cited file:line, reproduced failures, and ran narrow tests.
- Research discipline held under pressure: H-01 went AT RISK without the floor moving an inch.
- The job-check loop surfaced every late and stale item within a day of the owner asking for it.

## What fell short — fix the system, not the blame

1. **Unrecorded reviews.** ~10 money-path merges reached the integration branch as bare
   "Merge commit <sha>" with no review on record. Some had been reviewed; unrecorded is
   indistinguishable from undone, and today it cost two full re-reviews.
2. **Claims ahead of their evidence.** ADR 0016 claimed the tripping read closes the whole account —
   false in auto mode (finding F1). fc99e86 said "quant reviewer approved" before any sign-off
   existed. Backlog rows said "no commit yet" for landed work, M43 where M45 was meant, and a
   deploy date three days stale.
3. **Late sign-offs waited to be chased.** Quant sign-offs ran >24 h late on four items and were
   surfaced by the job check, not by the role.
4. **A machine-specific artifact in a public repo.** The CLI golden embedded the developer's home
   path: a username leak and a guaranteed CI failure on any other machine.
5. **Undelivered reports.** Two subagents finished their work but ended without handing the report
   back. An undelivered report wastes the whole lane.
6. **An idle principal.** The architect held no lane and wrote no plan since 10-12 while builders
   queued behind W1-1. Being blocked or idle is itself a finding — raise it.

## Expectations — performance

- **E1. The review is recorded at the merge.** Merge message or handoff fragment carries reviewer,
  verdict, sha and date. The release manager refuses a merge without it.
- **E2. Claims carry their evidence.** "Approved", "fixed", "done" never appear without a citation
  (sha, run id, test name). A status >24 h stale is a defect owned by the role that owns the row.
- **E3. Self-report lateness** before the job check finds it. Slips reach the program director
  within 24 h (owner rule, 16 Oct), with a new date and the reason.
- **E4. Every principal holds a dated lane** and raises risks unasked. Idle or blocked goes to the
  program director the same day.
- **E5. Committed artifacts are machine-independent.** No absolute paths, usernames, hostnames or
  locale assumptions; a golden must pass with a different HOME (S4, S8).

## Expectations — efficiency

- **F1. Iterate narrow, gate once.** `--testmon` or the touched files at `-n 2` while iterating; the
  full gate suite once, at the end, on a tree nobody edits (lane-test-rules; the suite is ~15 min).
- **F2. Reuse recorded evidence.** Cite an existing review or test run by sha instead of re-running
  it. Today's two re-reviews existed only because records were missing (E1).
- **F3. Deliver the hand-back, verdict first.** Tables over prose, within the brief's length (S11).
- **F4. Stay in scope, stop when merged** (S5). Reset the worktree to the integration branch before
  starting; leave files another lane owns alone.
- **F5. Escalate blockers immediately.** A permission block goes to the owner in the owner's words —
  never around the block. A missing input is reported, not guessed.

## North star

£50+ a day on days the market offers it, nothing forced on days it doesn't (ADR 0004). Every lane
either moves the Sunday engine start, protects the money path, or adds evidence the research loop
can act on. If a lane does none of those, say so and take one that does.
