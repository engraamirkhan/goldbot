## 2. Reading rule

Written 2026-10-18, before any outcome.

Each trial is read alone, by this rule, by the evaluator. The verdict is recorded with
`TrialRegistry.record_verdict` before any follow-up is written. B08 (H-04) and B10 (H-01) are first read by their own
reading rules in `preregistration-2027Q1.md`, word for word. A *continue* there allows only model research. To reach
paper they must also meet 2.2-2.6 below.

A trial **PASSES** only if all of the following hold:

1. **Screen (P4, as coded).** Rule-only gross mean R > 0 with t >= 2.0 on >= 1,000 events in the research window
   (2010-01-01..2025-09-30). For daily-signal rows (B10, the two 1d rows of B11): fewer than 150 events is
   *inconclusive*; 150-999 events can retire the row but cannot pass it.
2. **Net, walk-forward.** Rule-only net mean R over every candidate is > 0, charging bar spread + 0.37 $/oz + swap,
   with day-clustered t >= 2.0 (ADR 0022, CR1, G - 1 df).
3. **Deflation at the batch K.** The traded object is the model-filtered out-of-fold trades when a model was fitted,
   otherwise the rule-only trades. It must pass the report's walk-forward gates and have **DSR >= 0.95 recomputed at
   K = 53**, not at the K the run printed. Equivalent bar: per-trade t >= 3.94, about +0.175 R net at 1,000 trades
   (SD 1.4 R) or +0.32 R at 300.
4. **Minimum trades.** >= 300 out-of-fold trades in the traded object, and >= 30 of them between 2024-01-01 and
   2025-09-30 (the current volatility regime).
5. **Stability across years.** Net mean R is positive in >= 60% of the years with at least `MIN_TRADES_PER_YEAR`
   trades. It is positive in **both 2024 and 2025 (to September)**. No single year contributes more than 50% of
   total net R. Long and short are reported separately, and a result that comes from one side only is labelled so
   (shorts carry a 0-swap prior).
6. **Cost stress at today's measured cost.** Net mean R stays > 0 when every trade is charged **0.71 $/oz** beyond the
   bar spread instead of 0.37. That is 0.34 $/oz more per trade, divided by the trade's stop in $. The evaluator
   computes this on the trial's own recorded out-of-fold labels. It costs no trial and selects nothing.

**Other verdicts:**
- **LEAD:** rules 1, 2 and 5 hold but 3, 4 or 6 fails. The configuration may run in shadow only (no orders) to collect
  forward out-of-sample trades. It is not a founder and does not reinstate its family. Under the null, about 0.75 of
  the 33 trials are expected to clear t >= 2.0 by chance (one-sided p 0.023 each), so a LEAD is weak evidence by
  design.
- **FAIL:** the screen fails at or above its event floor, or rule-only net mean R <= 0. The configuration is retired
  with its reason. For a retired family, the retirement is reconfirmed with the date in `hypotheses.md` B and
  `research.retired_families`.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** fewer events than the floor. One re-run is allowed later, only with a pre-registered power gain.

**After the verdicts.** No variant or neighbour of any trial is dispatched in Q4 2026 after its result. A PASS becomes
a founder, and its neighbours (the menu in `program-continuous.md` section 4, at most 3) are written into the Q1 2027
file before any of their data is read. If nothing passes, the batch is reported as 0/33 and the families' status is
updated. It is not re-run with different settings.

**K, stated honestly.** `registry.n_trials_effective` goes from 20 to **53**. No discovery K_eff is added yet, because
no discovery has run. The gate-1 t bar rises from about 3.55 to about **3.94**, using the Bailey and López de Prado
approximation that ignores skew and kurtosis. The 33 trials are correlated (shared families and signals), so the
true number of independent tests is lower. K counts all 33 anyway, as the owner-confirmed conservative default
requires. Every later trial now pays for this batch. Projection for the end of Q1 2027: about 53 + 36 Q1 rows (two
moved here) + H-02 about 10 + H-14 about 17, so about 116. That needs t of about 4.2, or about +0.19 R net at 1,000
trades (the old projection was 67 and t 4.0).
