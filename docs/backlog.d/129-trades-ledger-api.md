- **129. One trade ledger API: every kind incl. exploration, filters, paging, blinding server-side.** Value: exploration trades (95%+ of fills) are invisible to Activity and Trades summary; Activity is capped at 200 events with no filter (docs/design/trades-ux-2026-10-27.md A2, A4). Fields: `GET /api/trades?period,account,family,timeframe,status,kind,exit_reason,evidence,cursor` returns `TradeRow` (docs/design/trades-ux-2026-10-27.md §2.2: trade_id, kind, account_label, family_label, variant, timeframe, arm_id, is_control, side, lots, entry, stop, stop_now, target, exit, exit_reason, censored, opened_utc, held_s, risk_amount, pnl/pnl_now, r/r_now, costs, why, evidence, blinded) plus filter totals; `GET /api/trades/{id}`. Sources: shadow_book, orders, closed_trades, aux cost_probes and exploration_trades; labels from a new config/strategy_labels.yaml. Acceptance: ADR 0050 blinding server-side, with a seeded-arm leak test; every registered arm and family has a label (CI test); p95 under 300 ms at 50k rows; unreadable sources named, never zero; API contract regenerated; quant-reviewer and code-reviewer pass. Lifecycle: run an edge (record keeping) and find an edge (every exploration fill visible). Depends: none (128 for open rows' £). TRACEABILITY: none yet; implementer adds a U row. Completeness (tests on a fixture with one closed trade of every kind): `pnl` is net of costs in account currency and `costs` splits spread, commission and swap with spread + commission + swap = gross − net to the penny; `why` is a non-empty plain-words sentence for every row of every kind (rule or arm and its trigger); every closed row has `exit_reason` from a closed vocabulary (target, stop, trail, time limit, Friday cut, manual, kill switch, broker stop-out) and never null; filter totals (n, net £, costs £) equal the sum of the rows.
