## 1. What the evidence allows (three binding constraints)

1. **The deflation bar is a Sharpe bar.** At K >= 55 a pass needs a per-trade t of about 3.97. A strategy that trades
   all year long has t ≈ annual Sharpe × √years, so 15.75 years needs a **net annual Sharpe of about 1.0** on gold
   alone. Parent #16 has about 239 trades a year at a per-trade gross Sharpe of 0.043, which is about 0.66 a year
   **gross** and -0.86 net. The published gold-alone trend evidence is weak (Huang, Li, Wang, Zhou 2020, *JFE*
   135(3), 55 futures 1985-2015, gross: asset-by-asset t ≈ noise). Every proposal below therefore has a P(PASS) of
   about 1-2%. Their real value is to **retire explanations** and to produce **LEADs** for the forward and holdout
   read at today's costs.
2. **In-sample cost is about 0.14 R per 1h trade** (#16: gross +0.061, net -0.079), 3-5 times today's
   (`costs-binding` §2.2). Wider stops do not fix this under the constant -60 USD/lot/night swap prior. Spread cost in
   R falls as 1/k, but nights held grow about as k², so swap in R grows about as k. Arithmetic on #36's own cost
   split (spread, slippage and commission 0.140 R; swap 0.048 R at 1.5 × ATR, 48 bars) gives about 0.168 R at
   2.0 × ATR with 96 bars, which saves only about 0.02 R. **Cost-amortising exits are therefore not a new
   hypothesis worth a trial.** H-28 (CB-T1) already covers them.
3. **Nothing at 1h or slower is placeable at £1,000.** A 1.5 × ATR1h stop is about $26.6 today. At 0.01 lot that is
   £19.95 of risk, against the default gate's $8.00 ceiling and a £20.45 daily projection above £20
   (`sizability-2026-10-18.md`). Every row here is **shadow only**. It becomes placeable at about £3,330 equity
   (1.2 × 0.5% × E ≥ £19.95), or when ATR1h ≤ $5.33. Equity and risk per trade are owner decisions.

## H-31

- **Family and config.** `tsmom` 1h, the defaults (`lb` 24/120/480 h, `vol_window_h` 96, `min_score` 0.5,
  `schedule_h` 4, target 3.0, stop 1.5, `max_bars` 48), plus a new optional key `"trend_align": "d1_sma200"`.
  Agent `tsmom-g0-95e846e298`, `config_hash` **`7a677f1dab2e`** (not in the 84-row snapshot; re-check at dispatch).
- **Gate definition (point-in-time).** Keep a candidate only when side = sign(C − SMA200). C is the close of the last
  stub-free feature-day bar (`drop_stub_days`) with `visible_at` ≤ the decision bar's close. SMA200 is the mean of the
  200 stub-free daily closes ending at that bar. Fewer than 200 days, or C = SMA200, drops the candidate.
- **Implementation (blocker).** The key does not exist yet. Needed: an implementer lane in `time_series_momentum.py`,
  modelled on `macro_gate` (`candidates_in_context` with the d1 context, fail closed in `candidates`); a lookahead
  test (shifting the daily bars by one settlement must change no earlier candidate); a test that the candidate count
  on 2010-2025Q3 equals 2,527 ± 2% after `one_at_a_time`. Review by quant-reviewer and code-reviewer.
- **Mechanism.** Trend at the 1-20-day horizon fights the 200-day trend less often when it is aligned with it. It
  removes the shorts in gold's long uptrends (2010-11, 2019-25) and the longs in the 2013-15 downtrend.
- **Expected edge versus cost.** My prior for kept-trade gross is +0.06 to +0.12 R (parent +0.061). In-sample cost is
  about 0.14 R, so I expect rule-only net ≤ 0. At today's ATR (scenario A) the cost is about 0.04 R plus about
  0.02 R of swap. Bars at SD 1.42 R and n 2,527: PASS needs net ≥ +0.112 R (gross ≈ +0.25 R); LEAD needs net ≥
  +0.056 R. **P(PASS) ≈ 1%, P(LEAD) ≈ 6%, P(FAIL) ≈ 93%.**
- **Inputs (one run, one trial):**
  ```bash
  gh workflow run research.yml --ref main -f specialist=tsmom -f from_year=2010 -f to_year=2025 -f lot_fit=true -f variants='[{"trend_align":"d1_sma200"}]' -f rule_ref='next-gen-2026-10-18.md#h-31' -f rationale='NEXTGEN-2026-10-18 H-31 1h tsmom aligned with the daily 200-day regime; one change vs parent #16'
  ```
  Dispatch only when no `research.yml` run is in progress. `score_holdout`, `skip_screen` and `macro` stay false.
- Reading rule (fixed now): `batch-2026-10-18.md` §2 rules 1-6 word for word, with K = registry `n_trials_effective` at the verdict, never below 55; plus two controls that can only downgrade. (a) **Filter lift:** the evaluator labels the rejected complement (#16's candidates the gate removes, same barriers, one at a time, rule-only gross); PASS or LEAD also needs gross mean R(kept) − gross mean R(rejected) > 0, and if that difference has Welch t < 1 the verdict is FAIL whatever else holds (the gate adds nothing; the net result is the parent's). (b) **Drift control:** the long trades' gross mean R must exceed that of a drift baseline (a long entry at every 4h-schedule 1h bar with C > SMA200, no tsmom condition, same barriers, one at a time); if not, the long side is labelled "drift, not momentum" and the row can be at most LEAD. PASS: founder, with neighbours (at most 3: 4h execution, d1 EMA100, 2.0/4.0/96 exits) written before any of their data is read. LEAD: shadow only, no neighbours; a holdout look only if the owner allows LEADs one. FAIL: the daily-regime alignment is retired as a filter on 1h-4h tsmom, and R-06 is reconfirmed with the trial number; no further side or regime filter on 1h tsmom without new evidence. INCONCLUSIVE (< 1,000 events): not expected (2,527); no re-run without a written power gain.
- **Trial cost:** 1.
