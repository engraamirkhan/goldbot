## 0. Bottom line

1. **No swing trade can be placed at £1,000. As the rails stand, a standard 1d swing trade cannot be placed at any
   account size.** At £1,000 a 1d minimum-lot trade risks about £79 (7.9%), so it is refused (ADR 0004 §2). The
   owner's £50 absolute daily limit is *projected* at entry: day loss so far + this trade's full stop risk must be
   ≤ £50 (ADR 0004 §3, BACKLOG 29). That check refuses the same £79 trade at every equity while
   1.5 × ATR(1d) × 1 oz is above about $66, which means ATR(1d) above about $44. ADR 0004 estimates ATR(1d) at
   ~$70. So milestone M4 (£7,900, "+1d") is not reachable for a 1.5-ATR(1d) stop under the £50 rule. This is an
   **owner flag** (section 6, F-1). It is not a research decision.
2. **Gate 1 is out of reach by construction for single-instrument swing holds of 8 days or more.** Gate 1 needs
   ≥ 500 model-filtered trades (`gates.backtest_min_trades`). One position at a time with 10-day holds gives at most
   about 394 trades over the whole 2010-01 .. 2025-09 window, and about 270 in the 1d walk-forward's out-of-sample
   years (2015-01 .. 2025-09). Gate 1 can never be met. A swing rule can be **retired** on this window. It cannot be
   **promoted**. The only routes to promotion are forward shadow evidence or a gate change (owner-only; not
   recommended).
3. **Power is the binding constraint, more than cost.** A realistic single-asset swing edge has an annual Sharpe of
   0.3-0.5 before the post-publication haircut. To reach t = 2 it needs **16-44 years** of data, and t ≈ 4 (gate 1's
   DSR at K ≈ 60) needs **64-178 years**. We have 15.75 years. P(t ≥ 2) is at most about 0.5 (section 2.3).
4. **Swap, not spread, is the cost for longs.** The settings prior (−60 USD/lot/night long, 0 short, triple on
   Wednesday) is a flat dollar amount. When gold traded at $1,200-1,800 and ATR(1d) was small (estimated ~$13-30), a
   10-day long hold paid an estimated **0.2-0.4 R** of swap, against ~0.02-0.05 R of spread and commission. The prior
   also gives shorts a free ride before 2022. Every swing rule must be read **long-only as well** (H-01 already does
   this) and against the unconditional drift (section 2.4).
5. **Recommended allocation:** **0 swing trials in Q1 2027.** H-01 is already the lane's Q1 trial, and the Q1 free
   reserve has no swing use. In Q2, **at most one trial**: SW-0, a 1d swing feature discovery that screens every swing
   idea below as a group, from the Q2 free reserve, conditional (section 3). Every other candidate costs 0 trials: it
   is a feature group inside SW-0, a 1w context feature, or blocked on data. The swing lane's main value at this
   account size is **context features for the decision timeframes** and a **forward shadow book**. Its value as a
   standalone book is low.

### 1.4 Power (DX-1 output, arithmetic only; checked by running DX-1)

Under iid annual Sharpe SR over T years, E[t] = SR × √T.

| Annual SR (after the post-publication haircut) | E[t], 15.75 y | P(t ≥ 2), 15.75 y | E[t], OOS 10.75 y | P(t ≥ 2), OOS | Years for E[t] = 2 | Years for E[t] = 4 (gate-1 DSR at K ≈ 60) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.3 | 1.19 | 0.21 | 0.98 | 0.15 | 44 | 178 |
| 0.4 | 1.59 | 0.34 | 1.31 | 0.25 | 25 | 100 |
| 0.5 | 1.98 | 0.49 | 1.64 | 0.36 | 16 | 64 |
| 0.7 | 2.78 | 0.78 | 2.30 | 0.62 | 8 | 33 |

Per trade, at a per-trade SD of about 1.3 R (3:1.5 ATR barriers), the mean R needed is:

| Hold | Max non-overlapping trades | t = 2 | t = 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 days | 788 | 0.09 R | 0.19 R |
| 10 days | 394 | 0.13 R | 0.26 R |
| 20 days | 197 | 0.19 R | 0.37 R |

**Clustering.** ADR 0022 clusters by entry UTC day. For multi-day holds that is necessary but **not sufficient**.
- Trades that share a signal unit are one piece of evidence: one COT report week, one macro regime spell, one
  festival window.
- One position at a time makes trades sequential. The iid t is then close to the clustered t, but serial correlation
  of the signal still inflates it.
- **Pin SW-P1 (quant ruling owed):** for SW-0 and any swing rule, the reading t clusters by the signal's information
  unit (ISO week of the COT release for SW-2; regime spell for SW-3; festival window for SW-6), else by
  non-overlapping 3-week blocks. The AUC lower bound uses a **15-trading-day block** bootstrap instead of the coded
  weekly block (`discovery.auc_lower_bound`), because a 10-day label spans two weeks. If code keeps the weekly
  block, the bound is too narrow and the report must say so.

### 1.5 Costs for multi-day holds (settings `costs`; formulae, with estimates marked)

- Round trip beyond the spread: 0.37 $/oz (2 × 0.15 slippage + 2 × 3.5 USD commission per lot side ÷ 100 oz;
  `execution.costs.settings_extra_cost_usd`). Spread: Dukascopy mean ~$0.34 on normal days
  (`holiday-list-measurement-2026-10.md`). In R at 1d: (0.34 + 0.37) ÷ (1.5 × ATR(1d)), which is **~0.01-0.04 R**.
  Negligible.
- Swap (prior): long −$0.60/oz per rollover, short 0, ×3 on Wednesday, so a hold of h trading days pays about
  h + 2⌊h/5⌋ rollovers. In R that is 0.60 × rollovers ÷ (1.5 × ATR(1d)).

| Hold | Rollovers | Long swap in R at ATR(1d) $15 (estimate, 2013-19) | at $30 (estimate, 2011-12, 2020-23) | at $70 (ADR 0004, 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 days | 7 | 0.19 | 0.09 | 0.04 |
| 10 days | 14 | 0.37 | 0.19 | 0.08 |
| 20 days | 28 | 0.75 | 0.37 | 0.16 |

  The ATR levels are estimates. **DX-4 prints the measured per-year medians** before any swing row is written.
- **Bias of the prior.**
  - The prior overcharges longs in the low-rate years 2010-2021 (settings comment), which is conservative.
  - It gives shorts a 0 swap that was really negative before 2022, which flatters shorts by about 0.1 R per 28-night
    hold (H-01's estimate).
  - Rule for every swing trial (as H-01): read net on both sides together **and** long-only. A short-only pass is
    *inconclusive*.
- **Recommendation (0 trials, data-engineer):** add DFF to `MACRO_RELEASE_SERIES` (public fredgraph CSV, no key).
  Then a rate-linked swap model (long ≈ −(fed funds + broker markup) × price ÷ 360 per oz-night; the markup is
  *unverified* until `costs-v1` measures it) can be reported as a **sensitivity only**. The pinned cost source
  (P-5) is never switched before a run.

### 1.6 The drift problem (a rule for every swing reading)

Gold roughly tripled over 2010-2025, with a 2011-2015 bear market. Any long-biased swing rule therefore shows a gross
"edge" that is unconditional drift, which is beta and not timing. Huang, Li, Wang, Zhou (2020, *JFE* 135(3), 55
futures, 1985-2015, gross) show that single-asset TSMOM profits look like those of a strategy built on historical
means, and that a t of about 2.5 is about what noise produces on one instrument (SoA A1).
**Pin SW-P2 (proposed for every swing trial):** besides the raw net mean R, the report prints the
**drift-adjusted net mean R**: the rule's net mean R minus the net mean R of the side-matched unconditional rule
(the same side, entry times drawn uniformly over the rule's eligible days, the same barriers). It is computed in the
same run on the same candidate set. The reading rule reads the drift-adjusted figure. Without SW-P2 a long-only
swing "continue" is uninterpretable.

### SW-1 Weekly trend persistence (H-01 neighbour; group in SW-0)

- **Mechanism.** Slow-moving investor and CTA flows, plus under-reaction to persistent macro news. This is the slow
  trend horizon, the only one with replicated support.
- **Evidence:**
  - Moskowitz, Ooi, Pedersen 2012, *JFE* 104(2): 58 futures including gold, 1965-2009, gross. R *(unverified DOI)*.
  - Hurst, Ooi, Pedersen 2017, *JPM* 44(1): 67 markets, 1880-2016, net of estimated costs. R.
  - For gold alone the evidence is weak (Huang et al. 2020, above).
  - Commodity momentum combined with reversal: Bianchi, Drew, Fan 2015, *JBF* 59: 423-444. Cross-sectional,
    claimed robust to costs. S *(sample unverified)*.
  - Weekly reversal in 24 US futures: Wang & Yu 2004. S *(unverified)*.
  - Short-term trend was lost on small-tick contracts after ~2009: Kurth et al. 2026 (SoA).
- **Features (point in time, stub-free daily bars; weekly bars are UTC-floored `resample_bars("1w")`, visible a week
  after their open):** close position in the 20/65/250-day channel; weekly close beyond the prior 4- and 13-week
  high or low; consecutive up or down weeks; H-01's `tsmom_score` as one column.
- **Data:** exists. **Diagnostic:** DX-4 (channel signal counts, one-at-a-time lower bounds).
- **Power:** a gold-alone SR of 0.3-0.4 needs 25-44 years for t = 2 (section 1.4).
- **Kill:** H-01 "stop", or group stability < 0.6 in SW-0. **Slot:** inside SW-0.

### SW-4 Gold variance risk premium at the swing horizon (H-03; group in SW-0)

- **Mechanism.** Compensation for jump and crash risk. When implied variance (GVZ²) is high relative to forecast
  realised variance, gold's subsequent excess return is higher. The sign comes from Nguyen et al. (2017) and is fixed
  in H-03.
- **Evidence:** Nguyen, Prokopczuk, Wese Simen 2017 (gold VRP and jump tail premium, in and out of sample, monthly
  horizons 1-24 months), gross, S *(venue unverified)*. BIS WP 619. Haircut by half (McLean & Pontiff 2016, *JF*
  71(1)).
- **Note.** H-02 screens H-03 on a **4h, 12-bar** label, far shorter than the literature's horizon. A null at 4h does
  **not** retire SW-4 at 10 days, and a 4h survivor does not substitute for it. The 10-day label is the closer test.
- **Data:** exists (`macro_vrp`, `macro_gvz`, `macro_gvz_chg20`). GVZ starts 2008-06, so it is fully warm by 2010.
- **Diagnostic:** DX-3 prints GVZ coverage. No new count is needed (one value a day).
- **Power:** a monthly-horizon effect read at 10 days keeps about 270 independent labels OOS. Low.
- **Kill:** group stability < 0.6 in SW-0, which retires the VRP at swing horizons (the 4h verdict stays H-02's).
- **Slot:** inside SW-0.

### SW-2 COT positioning extremes (H-09; group in SW-0)

- **Mechanism.** When managed money is crowded long (short), the marginal buyer (seller) is used up, so the position
  unwinds. The alternative reading is risk transfer: speculators are paid for taking hedgers' risk. Either sign is
  possible, so the model learns it inside the training folds. No sign is hard-coded.
- **Evidence:**
  - Fan, Fernandez-Perez, Fuertes, Miffre 2020, "Speculative pressure", *J. Futures Markets* 40(4): 575-597,
    doi 10.1002/fut.22085 (venue verified by search, 2026-10-17). Long-short **cross-sectional** portfolios of
    commodity, currency and equity futures earn a premium after controls. Gross. S. Sample *(unverified)*.
    **This is not a single-asset time-series test.** It does not transfer to gold alone without a test.
  - Rouwenhorst & Tang 2012, *ARFE*: no prediction *(unverified)*.
  - Basu, Oomen, Stremme (WP): hedging pressure timed gold in 2000-06 *(unverified)*.
  - Overall: mixed for gold.
- **Features (exist):** `pos_cot_mm_net_pct_oi`, `_z52`, `_chg4`, stamped Friday 15:30 ET (holiday-shifted) and
  joined by `asof_join`. Proposed addition (0 cost): `cot_comm_net` as % of open interest (hedging pressure).
- **Diagnostic:** DX-2. It counts the weeks and episodes with |z52| ≥ 1.5 and ≥ 2 by sign and year. Expect **a few
  dozen episodes in 15.75 years** (judgement; DX-2 gives the number). That is why SW-2 is never a standalone rule:
  with ~30 episodes, the SE of a mean 10-day return is about 1.3 R ÷ √30 ≈ 0.24 R.
- **Power:** a standalone rule at |z| ≥ 2 sits below the 150 retire floor, so it is inconclusive by construction.
  As a group inside SW-0 it is screened with the rest.
- **Kill:** group stability < 0.6 in SW-0 retires COT as a swing direction input. H-22 (COT predicts volatility)
  stays separate and lowest.
- **Slot:** inside SW-0. COT is **not** in H-02's frozen universe (H-02 opts in macro, not positioning), so SW-0
  would be COT's first screen.

### SW-5 Equity-stress haven and liquidation rebound (H-07, H-15; group in SW-0)

- **Mechanism.**
  - Haven: investors buy gold for about 15 trading days after an equity shock (Baur & Lucey 2010, *Financial
    Review*; Baur & McDermott 2010, *JBF*; 1979-2009, gross). R.
  - Liquidation: in a dash for cash gold falls with equities, then recovers (episodes 2008 and 2020 only; March 2026
    is in the holdout and is not evidence).
  - The model separates the two by the sign of gold's own move on the shock day.
- **Data:**
  - VIXCLS from 2003: full coverage.

### SW-3 Real-yield / dollar regime as a state (H-06 withdrawn; R-08 retired the sign rule)

### SW-6 Seasonality and calendar (survey #79, #82; group in SW-0)

## 5. Swing book next to intraday under the daily loss limit (allocator)

Today this is moot: no swing trade passes the 1% cap at £1,000, and none passes the £50 projected check at any equity
at a 1.5-ATR(1d) stop (F-1). The design below is for when it applies. **It needs trading-safety review and owner
approval.** Research proposes it; research does not set risk.

The problem:
- The daily caps work on equity (`day_loss = 1 − equity ÷ day_start_equity`), so a swing position's **floating** P&L
  spends the day's budget.
- Its overnight and weekend **gaps** can carry the day past £50 before any intraday trade.
- The rule-table allocator (`allocator/rules.py`) has no swing family and no risk-budget concept: it weights
  families by regime.

Proposal ("swing sleeve", 6 rules):
1. **Fixed sleeve.** Swing gets at most a fraction *s* of the effective daily limit L = min(2% × day-start equity,
   1.5% combined, £50). Recommendation: s = 0.5, an owner decision. Intraday gets L − (swing's reserved risk).
2. **Reserve at day start, never release on gains.** At 00:00 UTC the swing position's remaining stop risk from the
   current price, plus a gap allowance (1 × ATR(1d) beyond the stop on Fridays and before exchange holidays, else
   0.5 × ATR(1d); *proposed numbers, to be calibrated on DX-4's gap distribution*), is reserved. Swing floating gains
   **never** add room for intraday entries. This is BACKLOG 35's `project_open_risk` logic, turned on, plus the gap
   term.
3. **Entry test for swing.** A swing entry needs its full stop risk + gap allowance ≤ s × L. With the minimum lot at
   1.5 × ATR(1d) (£79) and s × L ≤ £25, **no swing entry fits at any equity under £50** (F-1 again).
4. **Same-direction heat.** A swing long plus intraday longs count toward `heat.max_same_direction` (BACKLOG 19), so
   the book does not double up on one move by accident.
5. **Exits are never gated.** The swing time barrier and stops run automatically. The owner's 90-second confirmation
   applies to the swing entry once, at the first 4h bar after the settlement, about 22:00-23:00 UK time.
6. **Priority.** Intraday entries are never refused *because of* the swing book's intraday loss. They are refused
   only by the reserved amount fixed at day start, so the intraday book's budget is known at the open.

Effect on the north-star (£50+ on opportunity days): a sleeve halves the intraday budget on days a swing position is
open. At £1,000 the intraday budget is about two full 15m losses (ADR 0004 §3), so a sleeve would cut it to about
one. **Recommendation: no swing sleeve before an intraday edge is in paper trading**, and in any case none before F-1
is ruled.
