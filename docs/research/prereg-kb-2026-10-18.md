## 1. H-48 `sweep_absorb` (KB-2), rule trial, 1 trial

**Mechanism.** Stop-loss orders sit just beyond prior-day and prior-session extremes (Osler 2005). A sweep that
pierces by < 0.5 ATR15 and is reclaimed within two 15m bars means the stops were hit and absorbed without follow
through: price returns. (The deep, close-beyond arm is a breakout; B03 breakouts and R-04 closed that, so it is not
tested.) Differs from H-08/H-46: no box, no swing break, no retest; first pierce only; stop sized for the cost.

**Config (fixed now).**
- timeframe 15m, mid prices, entries/exits on 1m barriers.
- Reference: previous feature-day high/low (`pdh`/`pdl`) and previous-session high/low (`prev_sess_high/low`),
  known at the bar (`trader._session_levels`).
- Pierce: first bar where high > ref (low < ref) with the previous bar's high <= (low >=) its ref; at most one
  event per ref per 4 bars.
- Absorbed: within bars t..t+2 a close back to or inside ref while the maximum penetration is < 0.5 x ATR14 (15m)
  at t-1 (a penetration >= 1.0 x ATR ends the search).
- Entry: the close of the reclaim bar, fade (short after a high sweep, long after a low sweep). Session gate:
  the entry bar is in the London or New York window (`DEFAULT_SESSIONS`).
- Stop: sweep extreme + 0.25 x ATR15 beyond; kept only if the stop distance is 0.6-1.0 x ATR15 (0.6 is the cost
  floor, 0.09/0.6 = 0.15 R; 1.0 = $8 sizes at 0.01 lot at the median ATR15). Target 2 x stop. Time limit 16 bars.
- News blackout as the batch.

**Counts (2010-01 .. 2025-09).** Absorbed, all sessions 11,564; London/NY 8,113; of which stop 0.6-1.0 ATR15
**3,349** (kept, the trial's events); stop < 0.6 ATR15 1,957 (skipped); stop > 1.0 ATR15 2,807 (skipped). By year
151-254 (2010 254, 2013 174, 2019 211, 2023 233, 2025 151 to Sep). Deep-cascade arm counted but not tested: 6,871 all,
4,833 London/NY. Sizable: all kept events have a stop <= 1.0 ATR15 = $8.0 at the median ATR15, so 0.01 lot passes the
default gate (0.60% risk is within 1.2 x target when ATR15 <= $8.0; above that day the signal is skipped by the
existing S-1 style size check, which is part of the live rule and not of the trial).

**Expectation.** Gross edge needed: net break-even about 0.15 R at the 0.6 floor; P4 gross t >= 2 needs about
+0.04 R at n = 3,349. Prior gross: small positive possible (+0.03-0.08 R from the tight-stop study across all
entries); net after 0.09-0.15 R cost: negative unless gross >= 0.2 R.

**Gates and reading rule (decided now).**
- P4 screen: gross t >= 2 and >= 1,000 events. If gross t < 2 or events < 1,000: **stop**, retire `sweep_absorb`
  and the shallow-sweep fade (H-08 15m form closed too); no neighbour tests.
- If gross t >= 2: apply batch §2 rules at registry K at the verdict (not below 90; t about 3.97). Pass -> promote
  as a founder and spawn only neighbours of the survivor (reclaim <= 1 bar, depth < 0.35, gate on/off).
- If gross t >= 2 but net negative and the gross R < the stop's cost R: **LEAD (cost-bound)**, not a pass; goes
  to the shadow book at 0.01 lot, no further trial this quarter.
- A cascade-arm test is not run unless this passes.

**Cost in trials.** 1 registry trial. K_eff 0 (one configuration, no grid; the depth 0.5, reclaim 2 and stop floor
are fixed by arithmetic and the mechanism, not tuned).

**Code needed.** New `goldbot/specialists/sweep_absorb.py` (about 120 lines: candidates from `trader._session_levels`,
`session_instances`, ATR; stop/target dict as the other 15m specialists; registered in `SPECIALISTS`); a
`preregistration` family entry and `research.yml` inputs: family `sweep_absorb`, timeframe 15m, window
2010-01-01..2025-09-30, `rule_ref: prereg-kb-2026-10-18.md#1`; unit test for look-ahead (perturb bars after t, same
events up to t) and a counts test equal to 3,349 on the pinned series.

## 2. H-49 `eq_liquidity_run` (KB-5), stage 1 diagnostic then 1 conditional trial

**Mechanism.** Equal highs/lows are salient, so stops pile up just beyond them; if so price is drawn through them
more than through a single swing extreme at the same distance. The extension from Osler's order clustering to
chart-salient equal levels is **our inference** (no published test; grade D for the extension).

**Stage 1 (0 trials, K_eff +1).** Base rate: for approaches counted below, P(trade through within 16 bars) for EQH/EQL
vs single confirmed pivots matched on distance in ATR, hour-of-day and ATR tercile, plus the 4-bar forward return
after the cross. Reading rule: lift >= 5 percentage points with a 95% CI excluding 0 **in both 2010-17 and
2018-2025-09** -> stage 2 runs; otherwise **retire "EQH/EQL as magnets"** and stop (no trial). Control pool:
27,415 single-pivot approaches vs 2,079 equal-level approaches.

**Stage 2 config (fixed now, runs only if stage 1 passes).**
- 15m. Equal level: `smc_replica.equal_levels` (pivot length 3, threshold 0.1 x ATR200), the level = the confirmed pivot
  price. Active until a bar trades through it; age 4-96 bars.
- Approach: first bar where the close is 0.3-1.0 x ATR15 from the level on the side the level is not yet hit. Entry at
  that close toward the level (long toward an EQH above, short toward an EQL below).
- Target = level + 0.25 x ATR15 (must trade through, which is the claim), stop 0.75 x ATR15 against ($6 at the
  median ATR15, sizes at 0.01 lot), time 16 bars. Reward:risk 1.1-2.3 (distance 0.3-1.0 plus 0.25 over 0.75).
- No trend filter, no session filter in the primary; London/NY reported as a contrast (1,045 events).

**Counts.** Equal highs formed 2,680, equal lows 2,530; approaches **2,079** (all sessions; by year 87-195, 2010 195,
2025 87 to Sep) and 1,045 London/NY. Both >= 1,000.

**Gates and reading rule.** P4 gross t >= 2 on >= 1,000 events (the all-session set; the London/NY contrast is read
only, never selected). Fail -> retire H-49 and the run-to-liquidity idea (H-46/H-47 targets stay as exits only).
Pass at gross -> batch §2 at registry K (not below 90, about 3.97). Net-negative with gross >= cost R -> LEAD.

**Cost in trials.** Stage 1: 0 trials, K_eff +1. Stage 2: 1 trial, K_eff 0 if stage 1 passes (the design is the
stage-1 band, not a grid).

**Code needed.** `scripts/kb5_magnet_base_rate.py` (stage 1; control matching; reads bars to 2025-09-30 only;
`pivot_points`, `equal_levels`); `goldbot/specialists/eq_liquidity_run.py` for stage 2 with its look-ahead and count
test (2,079 on the pinned series; equal level known only at its confirming bar).

## 3. D-KB1 retest selection (KB-1), diagnostic, 0 trials

**Why it ranks third.** Not a trade rule; it decides whether H-25's limit-at-level entry is adversely selected, which
saves a later trial and is the cheapest information per K_eff.

**Config.** Candidates: the H-37 `box_break` bare-break rung L6 (2,327 on 2010-01..2025-09; recount owed after the
2003-2009 data extension). Retested = price returns to the broken box edge within 3 1h bars (15m: 4). Report forward

structurally adverse-selected (lower its prior in its row); groups differing by < 0.02 ATR (CI includes 0) -> "retest
neutral". Retested count is bounded by H-39 L3 (1,437), so the never-retested group is at least 890.

- Correction to section 2: "reward:risk 1.1-2.3" is an arithmetic slip. With the fixed distances (0.3-1.0 ATR to the

  stop distance as R and a 2 R target. Both size by `barrier_atr` = the stop distance.

  The prereg fixes no feature; a model is pre-registered only after the rule-only screen passes.

### 7a. Quant review notes, 2026-10-19 (before any dispatch; no outcome of either rule exists)

- **Labels on 1m barriers (finding 1).** The first implementation labelled on the 15m bars with the stop first when a
  bar touches both barriers, against section 1's "entries/exits on 1m barriers"; that tie rule is pessimistic by
  about 0.01-0.03 R here, a large share of the +0.04 R that P4 needs at n = 3,349. Both specialists now set
  `barriers_on_1m`: entry at the signal close (ask for longs, bid for shorts) as before, then the stop, target and time
  barrier are searched on the 1m bars (`labels.triple_barrier_1m`; the time barrier is unchanged, the close of
  decision bar t + 1 + 16). The stop counts first only when both fall inside one minute. Research refuses to label
  them without the 1m bars (`research_pass.py` passes them); the CPCV and discovery paths do not pass 1m bars, so a
  CPCV or discovery of these trials is refused loudly, not labelled on 15m.
- **Stage-1 gate is code (finding 2).** `eq_liquidity_run` refuses every dispatch (`research_pass.py`, before any bar
  is read or any trial charged) unless `docs/research/kb5-stage1.json` is committed, records
  `rule_ref: prereg-kb-2026-10-18.md#2` and `stage2_runs: true`, and its stored lifts pass the section-2 reading when
  recomputed (`eq_liquidity_run.stage1_refusal`). Produce it with
  `python scripts/kb5_magnet_base_rate.py --bars raw --out-json docs/research/kb5-stage1.json` and commit it, pass or
  fail.
- **Counts and tolerance (finding 3), pre-registered now.** The scratch count script is replaced by the committed
  `scripts/kb_prereg_counts.py`, which counts with the specialists' own code on the same series (15m mid,
  2010-01-01 .. 2025-09-30, holdout cut before resampling). Rule: each family's candidate count must lie within
  **±3%** of its pre-registered count (sweep_absorb 3,349: 3,249-3,449; eq_liquidity_run 2,079: 2,017-2,141), else
  **stop**: that family is not dispatched until a dated note here explains the gap and is reviewed again. The script
  exits 1 on a stop. It runs before the first dispatch of each family.
- **Choices the text did not fix, now declared (finding 3).** Both rules emit at most one candidate per decision bar.
  Ties: `sweep_absorb` keeps the first level in key order (`pdh`, `pdl`, `prev_sess_high`, `prev_sess_low`;
  `sweep_absorb.py`, the per-bar dedup in `_scan`); `eq_liquidity_run` keeps the earliest-confirmed level
  (`eq_liquidity_run.py`, `_scan`). A bar with candidates on opposite sides yields none in both rules. These choices
  count in the tolerance above: `kb_prereg_counts.py` checks the candidates after them, and reports the raw count
  before them.
- **Model-filtered metrics are not evidence (finding 4).** The declared meta-model inputs are borrowed unchanged from
  `smc_structure` and were not chosen for these rules. Any model-filtered number this dispatch reports (AUC, filtered
  t, DSR of the filtered book) is descriptive only; the verdict reads the rule-only screen (P4 gross t, events) and
  section 2 of the batch rules. A model is pre-registered separately, only after the rule-only screen passes.
- **Stage-1 interval (finding 5).** The bootstrap now resamples the control approaches within each matched cell as well
  as the equal-level approaches; resampling the equal side alone left the 95% interval about 10% too narrow.
- **Delayed labels and per-candidate targets (finding 6).** `triple_barrier_delayed` now honours a candidate's own
  `target_atr`, as `triple_barrier` does (it silently used the spec's target before). Neither rule uses a delayed
  entry; the fix keeps the shared label code consistent.
