### IC-4 Vol-regime, cost-aware tsmom 4h: forward shadow only (0 trials; engineering ask)
- **Idea.** The only gross edge we have (tsmom, +0.06 R) loses to fixed USD costs. Cost in R falls as ATR rises:
  it was 2–3× higher in 2012–19 than in 2024–26 (SoA A7). Trend pays more in extreme markets (Moskowitz, Ooi &
  Pedersen 2012, *JFE* 104(2), the "smile"; diversified futures 1985–2009, gross, grade R; gold alone weak).
  Restricting C4H's candidates to `vol_tercile` >= 1 is a cost filter, not new alpha (H-13 logic).
- **Why no in-sample trial.** C4H's frozen rule retires the 4h horizon if net mean R <= 0, which is the likely
  result. A band-conditioned rerun on the same 2010–2025 sample, chosen after seeing H-19's band split on C4H, would
  be a re-test of a retired idea on the data that suggested it. That is the forking path the DSR cannot repair.
  - In-sample power is poor anyway: about 2,500 candidates in the top two terciles, SD about 1.4 R, so 80% power at
    0.080 R gross, against a plausible net of −0.02 to +0.01 R after swap.
- **What is proposed instead (decided now, before C4H runs).** The variant `tsmom {"timeframe": "4h", "max_bars":
  12}` with candidates where `vol_tercile` >= 1 is tracked **forward only**, from the start of a research-shadow
  ledger, as the "new evidence" `hypotheses.md` B requires before a retired idea returns.
  - **Reading at >= 300 forward trades:** retire for good if the lower 80% bound of net mean R is < 0. Otherwise the
    forward record is cited as new evidence in a pre-registered trial (1 trial, a later quarter).
  - At about 100 forward trades a year this takes about 3 years. So this is a kill switch with a long fuse, and it is
    ranked 4th, not higher.
- **Engineering ask (to the product owner and architect, not built here).** The shadow book hosts model versions
  (challengers and champion; `engine/shadow.py`). A rule-only research family that has not passed is not in it. A
  **research-shadow ledger** is needed for rule-only candidates: no orders, no RiskGate path, the same
  `policy_step` mechanics and MAE/MFE (`labels/excursion.py`). It is the instrument that lets retired or
  sub-threshold ideas collect forward evidence at zero trial cost. It reads no holdout and is not a trial.
- **0-cost diagnostic:** D1 (C4H candidate count by `vol_tercile`, cost and long-swap quartiles in R, target/cost
  multiple for 2/3/4 ATR targets, test-fold counts by tercile).
- **Kill before any ledger work:** if D1's top-tercile median cost plus the side-weighted swap is >= 0.06 R (the
  observed gross), the idea cannot be net positive at the observed edge. It is recorded as dead and no ledger
  slot is asked for it.

### IC-5 Asia→London session-transition continuation (1h; 1 trial, Q2, conditional)
- **Rule (frozen text for a Q2 pre-registration).**
  - Decision bar: the 1h bar closing at 08:00 Europe/London (the London open, DST-aware), Monday to Friday.
  - z = (close at the decision − open of the 1h bar starting 23:00 UTC the day before, the Tokyo open) ÷ (ATR14(1h)
    × √bars).
  - If |z| >= 0.5, enter in the sign of z. Stop 1.5 × ATR14(1h), no target, time exit at the close of the 4th 1h bar
    (12:00 London).
  - One position, swap 0.
  - The 08:00 London decision (07:00 or 08:00 UTC) is outside the US tier-1 blackout.
- **Mechanism (and why it is not R-05 or R-06).** The clientele changes at the handover, from Asian physical and
  ETF demand to London OTC (SoA B2). Price discovery shifts between venues across the day (Hauptfleisch et al. 2016,
  above). Continuation would mean London prices in Asian information slowly.
  - Evidence for cross-session continuation in gold: **none found**.
  - The nearest result is that the first session's return predicts the last session's return in Chinese index
    futures (Li, Shen, Wang & Zhang 2020, *Finance Research Letters* 35,
    https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v35y2020ics1544612319304337.html). That is equities, gross, with no costs
    reported and the sample period not given on the abstract page. Grade S at best.
  - Spot XAUUSD has neither the close auction nor the gamma hedging behind intraday momentum (SoA A4). Prior: low.
  - It differs from R-05: the signal comes from another session, and the formation window ends at the session
    open, not mid-session.
  - It may still repeat R-06 (a 7–9-hour lookback). Hence the overlap kill below.
- **Data:** data-v1 only.
- **0-cost diagnostic:** D2. It gives decision days, counts by |z| bin (< 0.5, 0.5–1.0, >= 1.0) and per-fold test
  counts.
  - **It never computes the sign of the Asia move.** H-04 reads 00:00–07:00 UTC returns, and a signed statistic of
    that window before H-04's run would contaminate H-04.
  - The overlap check (sign agreement with `tsmom_z_24`) is therefore owed **after** H-04's verdict is recorded.
- **Reading rule (written now):**
  - **Continue** if gross t >= 2.0 on >= 1,000 events AND net mean R > 0 AND >= 3 positive net years incl. 2021 or
    2022.
  - **Retire** if gross t < 2.0 on >= 1,000 events or net mean R <= 0.
  - **Inconclusive** below 1,000 events.
  - A significantly negative t (reversal) triggers nothing. It is recorded as consistent with the H-04/H-24
    tug-of-war and needs its own pre-registration.
- **Power:** about 2,500 events if |z| >= 0.5 holds on about 62% of about 4,000 days (normal approximation;
  D2 gives the count). SD about 1.0 R, so 80% power at a true gross of 0.057 R. Cost: D3-type cost at the London
  open, planning figure 0.10–0.18 R (higher in 2012–19). Net > 0 binds and needs a gross about 2–3× the detectable
  size.
- **Kill:**
  - Withdraw before the run (no trial) if, after H-04's verdict, the sign of z agrees with the sign of `tsmom_z_24`
    at the decision bar on > 70% of events (a repeat of R-06).
  - Withdraw if D2's count with |z| >= 0.5 is < 1,000.
  - Withdraw if **neither H-04 nor H-24 reads continue**: without a session-clientele effect the mechanism has no
    prior left, and `research-plan.md` §5 rules out new 1h trend variants.
- **Slot:** Q2, 1 trial, only if the conditions above hold. Not Q1: the free reserve excludes it. **Written into
  `preregistration-2027Q2-draft.md` now** (2026-10-17, before any H-04, H-24 or IC-5 outcome), as follow-up item 7
  with these conditions. It is past the six live follow-up items, so it is funded only from the Q2 free reserve by a

### IC-7 Trailing and time exits (passers only)
- **Evidence:** a stop or trailing rule adds value only when returns are serially correlated (Kaminski & Lo 2014,
  *J. Financial Markets* 18, *unverified*; grade S). Noise-area trend days with trailing and clock exits: SPY net
  Sharpe 1.33 (Zarattini, Aziz & Barbon 2024, SSRN, grade S).
- **Consequence:**
  - **No trailing exit on clock-drift rules (H-04, H-24, IC-5).** Their mechanism is a mean over a fixed window, so
    a trail changes the variance, not the mean. The time exit is the mechanism.
  - Trailing exits only for a momentum passer (a tsmom or H-01 survivor). They follow H-17's V0/V1 design: V1 is read
    only if V0 passes, and the pair costs 2 trials.
- **MAE/MFE discipline:** shadow and label MAE/MFE (`labels/excursion.py`) are outcomes. Designing an exit from the
  MFE of the sample it is then tested on is overfitting. An exit derived from MFE must come from forward shadow or
  pre-holdout data and be tested on data that did not shape it.
- **Slot:** Q2/Q3 follow-up reserve, only behind a passer.

## 5. The recursive loop: how survivors seed the next generation without inflating K

1. **Children are declared with the parent, before the parent runs.** The neighbour grammar for this lane is fixed
   now and capped at 2 children per passer, taken in this order:
   - **hold × 1.5 only.** One variant is pinned; the shorter hold is not a child. A longer hold lowers cost as a
     share of R, which is the binding constraint here. Running both directions would be two variants and would count
     as two;
   - the other intraday timeframe (1h↔4h; not for session rules, section 2);
   - V0→V1 exit (momentum passers only, IC-7);
   - the cost gate (H-13) on.

   A child runs only on its parent's recorded **continue** verdict (`TrialRegistry.record_verdict`). It is never
   picked by looking at sibling outcomes. Each run adds exactly 1 to the registry count. If a child is ever picked
   from a screen of several unrun variants, that screen is a discovery and its K_eff (variants screened) is added,
   as H-02's.

   **A child that passes on the same 2010–2025 sample is not a replication.** It shares the parent's data, most of
   its trades and its selection history, so it is evidence about a neighbour configuration and nothing more.
   Replication comes only from data the lineage has not seen: the forward shadow record and the one holdout scoring
   (O-10).
2. **Selection already paid for is inherited, not repaid and not hidden.** An H-02 cluster's child (IC-6) inherits
   the cluster's K_eff from H-02's row. `n_trials_effective` already adds it to every later trial under the
   owner-confirmed conservative default, so the child adds +1, not +K_eff again. A child that re-selects features
   inside its cluster is a new discovery and pays K_eff.
3. **No rescue mutations.** A failed or retired parent seeds nothing. It returns only with out-of-sample evidence
   dated after its retirement (`research/director.py`). For 1h/4h the only such source is forward shadow (IC-4's
   research-shadow ledger), never a re-cut of 2010–2025.
4. **New features enter in bulk, never one by one.** Ideas from this generation that are features (SGE calendar,
   tier-1 proximity, macro regimes) wait for the next pre-registered discovery and are screened there as groups, at
   one trial plus the groups' K_eff.
5. **The tournament keeps its own honest count.** In-app clones of a founder are deflated by `n_pop = len(members)`,
   and retired members stay in `members` (`population.py`, `tournament`), so the count is cumulative. A live
   promotion needs both the research gate (registry K) and the tournament DSR (n_pop). A founder from this lane
   enters the population only after a gate pass, so mutation on shadow data never feeds back into the registry
   count unseen.
6. **The holdout is used once.** A discovery-derived pass gets one holdout scoring (O-10). Children are never scored
   on the holdout their parent used to pass.

**Ledger after this note (no change):** registry K = 20 (Q4 2026). This lane adds 0 trials and 0 K_eff to Q1. The
end-of-Q1 projection stays at about 60 (40 registry rows + H-02 about 10 + H-14 about 12; gate-1 t about 4.0). Q2
adds at most 1 (IC-5) + ≤ 2 (IC-6) + 2 (IC-7), each conditional on a parent's verdict.
