- Daily position engine (rethink strategy.md step 2; amendment W3, PR #285; ADR 0071): `goldbot/research/position/` turns a pre-registered spec's daily signal into daily P&L in units of starting equity. Decision at the 21:00 UTC mid close, fill at the next 1m open. It covers vol targeting at 10%/yr (EWMA 60, lagged a day, cap 2x), the no-trade band (10% of target unless the spec states one) and the 3 x ATR(20) disaster stop. Costs are C1 point costs in bps from the cost of record; financing is by date (DTB3 by publication + the measured ICM markup 0.74%/yr each side, 2.5% for unmeasured symbols, FX by rate differential). It books venues A (gates), B (ETC, long/flat) and C (CME, monthly roll), and reads W3 statistics: block-bootstrap t (20-day blocks, 4,000), Sharpe, years positive (60% rounded up), drift-adjusted gross, legs, W3.4 counts, the episode-sign or exposure-matched null (1,000 draws), buy-and-hold, BH-FDR and the W3.1 power helpers. `scripts/position_screen.py` runs a batch on a W2 window; TEST-OLD and CONFIRM need `--frozen-prereg`, and a run without DTB3 needs `--allow-constant-rate` and is marked ungated. Gold loads from raw/ 1m (4,066 days 2010-01-01..2025-09-30); other markets from `raw/daily/<symbol>.parquet` when the data lane lands them. Nothing at or after 2025-10-01 is read: the 2026 file is never opened, the 2025 file is filtered at read and every frame and signal is asserted. No strategy has been run on any real window.
