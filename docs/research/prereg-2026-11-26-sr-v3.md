**What S ≥ +0.05 R needs (Gaussian approximation, computed for this card from the numbers above, no data read).**
With per-candidate net sd σ = 1.16 R and mean μ, a score with correlation ρ to net R, kept at its best share k, gives
S(k) = k·μ + ρσ·φ(Φ⁻¹(1 − k)) − max(0, μ).

| μ (net mean) | ρ = 0.03 | ρ = 0.04 | ρ = 0.05 | ρ needed for S = +0.05 R |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −0.036 R | +0.003 R (k 0.15) | +0.006 R (k 0.22) | +0.010 R (k 0.27) | **0.144** (k 0.41) |
| −0.060 R | +0.001 R | +0.002 R | +0.005 R | **0.165** |
| 0 | +0.014 R | +0.019 R | +0.023 R | **0.108** |

The approximation reproduces the measured H-101 edge (+0.004 to +0.0065 R) at the measured ρ. **The gate needs 3-5x
the information the sr-v2 inputs carry.** A better optimiser, loss or learner moves the edge by at most 0.008 R
(lifecycle §0). So sr-v3's own bodies are expected to be refused at the information ceiling (section 5.1) and to run
fit-only. The card still exists because the owner's objective is a **retraining machine that is correct**: pretrained,
versioned, abstaining honestly, monitored without false alarms, and able to carry forward any history body that does
pass (H-102, OMB-1). "A history winner counts only through sr-v3 forward ACTIVE" (G3 card, route B).

## 2. What it replaces, and what is not re-tested

- **sr-v2 is not edited.** It runs as signed to its 2026-12-27 run. Retiring it after that, unless a pair is
  mid-chain, is a research-director decision (recommended here).
- sr-v3 differs from sr-v2 in: objective (obj-v1 offset form, no `DROP_SHARE`), a history-pretrained body, an
  intercept-only live head, per-procedure K, abstention, the admission rules of the 2026-11-25 amendment, the label
  version (r3), and the monitors. A2: an objective swap on the same arms and inputs **carries sr-v2's cumulative K
  series**; it is not a fresh series.
- **Known failures, not re-tested (no learner, loss, optimiser, regularisation or seed grid on these inputs):** R-07
  meta-models, R-20/A27 (ML-M1, M1c, M2), MLNET V3, MLEXEC, PROBTREND, R-18 discovery v2, H-62 LightGBM, the
  specialists' Saturday LightGBM, **H-101 zoo-v1 (null 2026-11-25)**, and every do-not-do item of the lifecycle plan
  §4 and §7.6 (no early stopping, no init_model, no TabPFN/deep nets/TSFM, no focal/ranking losses, no PSI actuator,
  no unweighted AUC/log-loss on any fit, threshold, selection or gate path).

### 3.4 Model class and loss (R-D, R-E, R-I)

- **Body (per own unit):** a partially pooled linear regression of clipped gross R:
  ĝ(x) = a_unit + a_arm + βᵀz(x), z standardised with the frozen training scaler, median imputation from training.
- **Loss:** Σ w_i (clip(g_i, ±10) − ĝ(x_i))² + λ(‖β‖² + Σ_arm a_arm²), a_unit unpenalised, solved in closed form
  (float64 `numpy.linalg.solve`). No iterations, so **no early stopping exists** (R-C).
- **λ is a prior in effective rows, fixed:** β_j ~ N(0, τ²), τ = 0.05 R per input sd (the size of a single input at
  ρ ≈ 0.04), residual variance 1.35 R² (σ = 1.16 R): λ = 1.35/0.05² = **540 effective rows**; the same for arm
  intercepts. With Σw = n_eff, weights ×10 give the identical fit, phase copies at 1/P give the real-only fit, and an
  overlap H raises the relative penalty by H (N5 fixtures). *Deviation flagged for the quant:* N5 writes
  C_fold = 1/(λ0 · n_eff), a constant shrinkage fraction; this card uses the Bayes form (fixed prior, data dominates as
  n_eff grows). Both pass N5's four fixtures when "live vs history shrinkage ratio = 1" is read at equal n_eff.
- **Not allowed:** GBT, kNN, stacks, TabPFN, torch (R-D, R-E); `LogisticRegressionCV`, `CalibratedClassifierCV`,
  any library selection by its own metric.
- **Seeds:** the fit is deterministic; seed SD is 0 by construction. Seeds exist only for the bootstrap (20261206),
  the B2 random keep (20261207), canaries and synthetic tests (20261208). All pinned in `sr_v3.py`.

### 3.5 Decision rule (obj-v1, R-I)

- **cost(x)** = the ub95 cost of record of the decision's broker × session, the `all` exit cell, plus the replay base
  cost (`RT_BP`), in R at the candidate's own stop. Known at decision; never the realised cost (R-H). The realised
  exit-type legs enter only the read's net R; the gap (offset bias) is reported every run.
- **Keep iff ĝ(x) + δ_b,u [+ (γ_b,u − 1)(ĝ(x) − ḡ_u)] − cost(x) > 0.** Full abstention is allowed; there is no keep
  floor and no keep share. Deployed rule = judged rule.
- **ABSTAIN** (0 K, not a pass): fewer than 100 kept rows or 30 kept days in a read window, decided from label-blind
  counts before any outcome is computed (ADR 0098 §3).
- The cost-of-record version is pinned per read in the `FrozenStamp`. Rows scored under another version are not in
  that read.

## 4. Retraining (R-N, R-O, N12, N18, N19)

- **Body: frozen.** Fitted once per version off the brain, shipped as JSON/npz with lineage (A19). The deployed body
  is 2010-17 data, so it is **about 9 years stale at 2026-12**; this is stated on every report line. A refit on
  2010..2025-09 is allowed only after a recorded batched confirm read (N19, N26), as a new version with its own read.
- **Live head, weekly** (Sunday run, data cutoff Friday 21:00 UTC, as sr-v2): intercept δ_b,u per (broker, unit):
  δ = Σ v_i (g_i − ĝ_i) / (Σ v_i + n0_u), with v_i = uniqueness × 2^(−age/26 weeks), n0_u the frozen prior in effective
  rows (a0 = n0_u / history n_eff). Rows: resolved, admitted, featured **replay** labels of every candidate (never
  fills, never fill costs, never kept-only rows; the 10 % exploration sample counts as an ordinary candidate, its
  fills never enter; `limits_epoch` is a reported stratum).
- **Reserve weeks (N12):** rows decided in odd ISO weeks from 2026-10-01 never train, update or calibrate anything
  until their read is recorded; training rows whose horizon ends at or after the next reserve week's start are
  purged. Holdout minutes appear only as engine lookback for decisions on or after 2026-10-01 (ADR 0100); no sr-v3
  model is ever scored on the holdout.
- **Minimum new rows:** an update needs ≥ `MIN_UPDATE_ROWS` 50 new training rows for that (broker, unit) since the
  parent; otherwise the child is the parent (same sha, recorded `no_update:min_rows`).
- **Limits:** |δ_t − δ_t−1| ≤ 0.02 R and |δ_t| ≤ 0.05 R. A shrunk estimate beyond either is applied clipped, and the
  child is marked `out_of_scope`. Rows scored by an out-of-scope child never pool with its lineage (R-O): that child is
  a new version with its own read and K.
- **Slope γ:** frozen at 1 unless Stage A selects form (c) **and** the unit's live training n_eff reaches the N30 slope
  threshold; then at most once a quarter, γ ∈ [0.8, 1.2] and |(γ − 1)(ĝ − ḡ)| ≤ 0.05 R at the 99th percentile of the
  training rows.
- **Stage A, the adaptation harness (0 K, fit-only, before 12-11; R-N, N19):** body on 2010-14, weekly heads over
  2015-01..2017-12 for 7 configurations: (a) frozen; (b) intercept with n0 1000, 300, 100; (c) intercept + slope with
  n0 1000, 300, 100, in this simplicity order. **Rule, fixed now:** per unit, the simplest configuration whose paired
  pseudo-live S is within one day-clustered SE of the best. The script records the choice before it writes any S; the
  choice and the n0 × unit table are hashed into the artifact. History pseudo-live captures drift over time only, not
  the Dukascopy-to-broker shift. A re-tune after any live or confirm look is K +1.
- **Version tagging (N18):** child sha = H(body sha, head parameters, n0 table hash, cadence, training manifest sha,
  cost-of-record version). Lineage row per child: `objective_id, data_manifest_sha, git_commit, train_window,
  artefact_cutoff_utc, history_rows, live_rows, parent_artifact_sha, n0_table_hash, adapter_scope, out_of_scope,
  limits_epoch strata, reference_bins per source` (A17, A19). Every scored forward row stores the scoring sha **at
  scoring time**: at decision through W2-3, or until W2-3 lands at the first weekly run after the decision, by the
  child whose cutoff precedes the decision. Fit-only children are K 0 and never judged mid-read.

## 5. Evaluation

### 5.1 Stage 0 (0 K, all before any K; a refusal is NOT STARTED, recorded, 0 K)

1. **Leak suite (N25):** truncation invariance; feature shift (+1 bar must spike the fit, −1 bar must not move it);
   an oracle canary planted at +0.03 / +0.05 / +0.10 R recovered within its SE; shuffle band for within-arm AUC
   0.5 ± 4·SE(n) (R-R); holdout and post-2017 rows raise; a planted 0.52 leak and a planted 1-bar look-ahead fail.
2. **Shuffled-label keep-share check (N6, restated for the offset form):** with gross labels permuted within
   (arm, session-day) blocks, the body's β is about 0, so its keep set should equal the intercept-only keep set
   (B3/B8). Pass: keep-share difference to that set ≤ 5 percentage points in every (unit, session) cell, and keep
   share ≤ 5 % in every cell whose mean gross is below every candidate's cost. Otherwise the regularisation is too
   loose: NOT STARTED.
3. **Information ceiling (N3, A24), per own body, on the 2012-17 OOF of the expanding yearly fit (seen; a screen,
   never a go):** (a) top-decile within-arm net R (ub95 cost) > 0 for at least one arm with ≥ 300 rows in the decile;
   (b) observed within-arm Spearman IC of ĝ vs net R ≥ the required IC for that unit, computed by the synthetic-score
   method on the unit's own learn net R (200 seeds; section 0 gives the Gaussian value 0.11-0.17); (c) the oracle S
   (perfect gross sign) ≥ +0.05 R. **All three, or the body gets no K.** This card names no new information that
   raises the IC of these inputs, so (b) is expected to fail: expected **NOT STARTED (information)** for U1, U5 and
   U15. IMP-H passes this step only on its own card's history read; it is not re-screened here (no second look).
4. **Power (A22, ADR 0085):** the judged read needs, in n_eff (Q1/Q3, rho_day 0.25 until the ICC measurement lands),
   at the label-blind keep share k̂ from the logged scores and offsets:

   | k̂ | 0.10 | 0.20 | 0.33 | 0.50 | 1.00 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | kept mean implied by S = +0.05 R when μ < 0 | 0.50 R | 0.25 R | 0.15 R | 0.10 R | 0.05 R |
   | n_eff, 80 % power, one-sided α 0.05, sd 1.16·√k | 333 | 666 | 1,109 | 1,664 | 3,328 |
   | n_eff at α 0.10/2 (Holm rank 1, two units) | 475 | 949 | 1,582 | 2,373 | 4,747 |

   **Expected flow (documented counts, recomputed at Stage 0 from the brain by count only):** RBR15 about 13
   candidates a session day with a 1-day hold, so about 1 n_eff a day; the other nine 0.5-2 a day each; SUB pooled
   about 2-4 n_eff a session day; IMP-H about 2 candidates a day (OMB-1 card). **SUB reaches 666 n_eff in about 170-330
   session days**, so not before 2027-Q3 at k̂ 0.2. **No sr-v3 judged read can happen by the G3 snapshot of
   2027-01-10.** If n is not reached by `LATEST_READ` 2027-09-26: NOT STARTED (power), 0 K.
5. **Sub-hourly label gate (A9):** a timeframe with > 5 % sign flips mid vs side-aware labels is not judgeable.

### 5.3 Judged forward read and gate (reading rule, written before any data)

- **Read date:** the first Sunday run at which the unit's label-blind n_eff reaches the 5.1 table at k̂, with ≥ 100
  kept rows and ≥ 30 kept days; at the latest 2027-09-26. The K row is written before any outcome is computed.
- **Rows:** forward rows of the unit's arms decided after the procedure's first published child and after each arm's
  `retired_day`, each scored prospectively by a child inside the pre-registered head (lineage-checked). Primary: all
  such rows. Reserve-week rows only are reported beside it and must have the same sign. *(If the quant rules N12 as
  "reserve weeks only", the n_eff in 5.1 doubles in calendar time.)*
- **Statistic of record (ADR 0098, R-M):** S = mean(net R × keep) over all candidates − max(0, mean net R), per
  candidate, max(0, ·) per judged unit. Stationary day-block bootstrap (Politis & Romano 1994, *JASA* 89(428)), mean
  block 5 session days, B 10,000, seed 20261206; arm and control days drawn jointly.
- **Pass needs all of:**
  1. S ≥ +0.05 R with one-sided 95 % lower bound > 0;
  2. kept mean net R > 0;
  3. gross skill = k × (kept mean gross − all mean gross) ≥ 0;
  4. control leg: kept mean net R − side-matched control mean net R ≥ +0.05 R (G3 route B requires both edges);
  5. Holm across the run's judged units (Holm 1979) on max(p_S, p_ctrl) ≤ 0.10;
  6. **2x cost stress:** with the keep set fixed and costs doubled in the outcome, S_2x ≥ 0 (R-H); 1.5x is reported;
  7. beats every baseline in 5.2 (point);
  8. no veto (N22): shuffle out of band, oracle failure, feature-shift failure, calibration slope 95 % CI wholly
     below 0.7;
  9. **two consecutive passes**, cutoffs exactly 7 days apart (G3 route B).
- **S split, every read:** S = gross skill + cost avoidance + base, where cost avoidance = k × (mean cost all − mean
  cost kept) and base = k·μ − max(0, μ). A pass whose gross-skill CI includes 0 is reported as **cost-avoidance-led**.
- **ACTIVE (route B, a re-activation candidate; nothing trades):** a pass, then the first passer's frozen model has
  forward S ≥ 0 once it has ≥ 30 rows decided after its cutoff (a harm check; at 30 rows it fails a true +0.05 R
  model about 40 % of the time, G3 card A1). **Demo influence** additionally needs two consecutive harm checks of
  ≥ 100 unseen kept rows each (S ≥ 0, kept mean ≥ 0; power at a true kept mean 0.15 R is about 0.9), W2-3 serving,
  A18/A19 coverage and trading-safety review. **Never real money** (owner-only).
- **Report:** S, its split, kept share, kept mean net and gross, £/day at 0.01 lot, the baseline table, the DSR at
  the K of record (report-only, ADR 0085), cumulative cross-study p beside Holm (N24, when it exists), staleness.

### 5.4 Diagnostics battery (N10, A26; descriptive, never a selector)

Every run, per body: weighted OOF calibration slope and intercept of realised gross on ĝ (with CI); R²_OOS (the
regression analogue of BSS); within-arm Spearman IC and within-arm AUC of ĝ for 1[net > 0] (never pooled); train vs
OOF gap; half-split coefficient correlation and sign agreement; bootstrap Monte Carlo SE (seed SD is 0); learning
curve at 1k/3k/10k/30k/all rows; canary recovery at +0.03/0.05/0.10 R; score-decile × (win rate, net R) table;
offset bias. **Stop rule (N10):** a flat learning curve plus a calibration slope near 0 retires "new learner on the
same inputs" for good.

### 5.5 Monitoring: anytime-valid demote and kill (N21), drift warn-only (A18, R-P)

- **Harm sequence:** a predictable-plug-in empirical-Bernstein confidence sequence (Waudby-Smith & Ramdas 2024,
  *JRSS-B* 86(1); Howard et al. 2021, *Annals of Statistics* 49(2)) on per-session-day mean S contributions, each row
  clipped at ±10 R, α 0.05, model vs abstain and vs control. **Demote** (ACTIVE → shadow) when the upper sequence
  bound on S < 0. A demoted unit is **killed** for this procedure: re-promotion needs a new version and its own read.
  Rough detection times at SUB flow (to be fixed by the simulation test): a true −0.15 R in about 1-2 months, −0.05 R
  in about a year.
- **Calibration sequence:** the same construction on the mean head residual per (broker, unit); a sequence wholly
  outside [−0.05, +0.05] R marks the head `out_of_scope` (WARN), which starts a new version. It never resizes.
- **Drift (WARN only):** PSI per served input, per source, against the stored `reference_bins`, with a sample-size-
  adjusted null threshold (permutation or chi², Holm across inputs), read only at ≥ 300 effective rows. **No
  automatic size-down, demotion, retrain or promotion from PSI.** Run status from outputs: `ok` needs
  n_with_features > 0, n_weights > 0 and ≥ 1 fitted body; else **`starved`** (WARN, FAIL after 48 h). Health shows
  counts: history_rows, live_rows_added, children, out_of_scope, days since last child, recalibrated heads (R-U).
