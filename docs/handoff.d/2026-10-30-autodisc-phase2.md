- Autonomous discovery phase 2 (ADR 0058): the honesty set, generator G2 and the `autodisc_nightly` job. `autodisc/stats.py` holds pure functions with known-case tests: White RC and Hansen SPA (consistent version) on a stationary bootstrap, batch PBO (CSCV), deflated Sharpe at the ledger K with each spec's own skew and kurtosis, a circular-shift null, and a plateau check (at least 60% of neighbours positive and a neighbour mean of at least 0.5 x the peak). `autodisc/gen_gp.py` is typed GP over the leaves `PrimitiveProvider` computes, with structure and time leaves weighted 2:1 over regime. It uses NSGA-II selection on fitness, complexity and turnover, is bounded at population x (generations + 1) evaluations and is deterministic under its seed. Every evaluated candidate is ledgered before its result. Its G0 null reruns the whole search on random sides and counts in K_null. `autodisc/nightly.py` runs the search, the null and the honesty set, then writes `state/autodisc/survivors-<day>.md` (counts use thousands separators, so no 2018+ number can appear) and a health line. Grid neighbours are ledgered and counted in K. The job is off unless `research.autodisc.nightly_enabled`, because ADR 0058 section 5 keeps all search off the brain. On the Mac it runs with `python scripts/autodisc.py nightly`. It creates no arm and imports nothing that trades. Also fixed: the phase 1 CLI report raised whenever a count fell in 2018-2099. Not built: ONC K_eff (the DSR uses raw K_search, which is conservative) and novelty labels for G2 survivors. Reviews owed: quant (SPA threshold, plateau ratio, the GP null) and code.
