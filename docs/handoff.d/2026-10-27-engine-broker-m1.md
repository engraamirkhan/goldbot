The call runs on its own thread with its own bridge client (one attempt, 2 s timeout), and failed calls back off exponentially up to 60 s, so the tick thread never waits on the bridge (trading-safety HIGH on c77be48d). A decision close waits at most 3 s past close + 1.5 s for its last minute's bar; exits never wait. Broker bars that are unsound (NaN, high below low, bid above ask, or flagged by `bar_errors` as a replacement) are refused with `broker_bar_refused`. Through the bridge only: a terminal on the same host keeps the sampled bars. The bars built from sampled quotes are now used only for the current minute and as a fallback when the broker's bar is late or missing. A fallback minute gets a `broker_bar_late` dq warning, which does not block entries, and is replaced when the broker's bar arrives within 10 min (`broker_bar_replaced`). Decisions already taken stay as journalled. A bar joins only after it was fetched at or after its close, and visible_at is unchanged.
