- PUBLISHED SIGNATURES for L2 (the engine lane makes one call per CLOSED exploration trade; its fill and close stay in `cost_probes` with purpose "exploration", and `obs_id` is that row's client order id):
  `record_obs(db: Database, *, obs_id: str, account_id: str, arm_id: str, family: str, tf: str, side: int, decision_utc: pd.Timestamp, entry_utc: pd.Timestamp, exit_utc: pd.Timestamp, entry_price: float, exit_price: float, stop: float, cost_per_oz: float, censored: bool, snapshot_ref: str | None = None) -> bool`
  `db` is `Database.open(state_dir, "aux")`; timestamps tz-aware UTC (stored as epoch ns via `timeutil.epoch_ns`); `decision_utc` is the instant the arm decided (after the signal bar's close: only features available STRICTLY before it are joined); `cost_per_oz` is the measured round trip (spread + commission + swap) in USD/oz; `censored` means the outcome was cut short before either barrier; `snapshot_ref` points at the decision-time feature snapshot. R is computed here: `(side*(exit-entry) - cost_per_oz) / |entry-stop|`. Returns False when `obs_id` was already recorded: the first record stands, a revision is logged and ignored. Invalid input (side, timeframe not 15m/1h/4h/1d, times out of order, stop on the wrong side, non-finite prices or cost) is written with a `quarantine` reason, logged, and never trained on.
  `weights(db: Database, arms: Sequence[str], *, ceilings: Mapping[str, float] | None = None, now: pd.Timestamp | None = None, max_age: pd.Timedelta = MAX_WEIGHTS_AGE) -> dict[str, float]`: each arm's share in [0, 1] of the configured exploration budget (shares sum to at most 1; multiply the config ceiling by it). Equal shares (1/n, capped) when there is no run, it is older than 3 days at `now`, it does not score every requested arm, a share is out of range, the shares sum above 1, or aux.db is unreadable.
- `training_table(db, features, *, arm_id, as_of, feature_cols=None)`: one arm only (never pooled), labels known by `as_of` (exit_utc <= as_of), features through `asof_join` on `available_utc` at decision - 1 ns; more than 40 feature columns raise. `select_features` picks at most 40 without the labels (coverage, then name), so selection cannot leak them.
- Nightly (`exploration_learn`, the last step of `nightly_costs`, isolated: a failure is reported as `exploration.error` and never stops the cost table): features are the decision frame on the arm's timeframe stamped at bar close (open + tf); per family x timeframe a logistic meta-labeller (P(net R > 0)) out-of-fold through purged, embargoed time folds (k 5, embargo 1 day), scored per arm (n, Brier, AUC); then Thompson sampling on net R per arm (Normal posterior on the mean; flat prior 0 R worth 2 trades, or the family champion's backtest mean/sd worth half its trades, at most 10); share = P(best) capped at the arm's ceiling and never re-normalised upward. Weights history is kept per run (`run_ns` = the slot); each arm's `detail` carries its n, mean R, prior, OOF scores and the family model's parameters. Ceilings and configured arms are read from `settings.exploration.arm_ceilings` / `.arms` once L1/L2 add that section; until then shares sum to at most 1 with no per-arm cap. No dedicated scheduler slot: `SchedulerSettings` needs a config entry per job (config is L1/L2's).
