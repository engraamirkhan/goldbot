- **134. Telegram trades: push real money and safety only, /open and /today on demand.** Value: at 500+ fills a day only real money and safety are pushed; the rest is a digest or a pull (docs/design/trades-ux-2026-10-27.md §4). Acceptance: no message for any demo, exploration, control, probe or shadow fill (test); one silent push per real fill and close with £, R and time held; one silent push per arm retirement or read verdict; one silent off-pace notice per lab per day at most; `/open` and `/today` owner-only commands, at most 12 lines, numbers equal to the API's; no slugs, ids or logins (leak test).
