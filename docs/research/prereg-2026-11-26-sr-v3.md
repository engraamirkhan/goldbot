# Pre-registration: `sr-v3`, the history-trained, live-retrained model procedure (H-103, replaces sr-v2)

Principal ML researcher. Written 2026-11-25/26 **before any sr-v3 fit, score, label or outcome statistic exists**.
**Revision 2 (2026-11-26)** follows the owner's directives of 2026-11-25:
- "the models are trained on historical data; if the data is not sufficient, the data must be produced or organised
  for this purpose";
- "everything should be live immediately and improve and retrain on live data";
- "before deploying, the models should be trained".

Rev 1 judged sr-v3 on the ten retired arms' live signals and concluded it could not be judged before 2027-Q3. That
framing is withdrawn. The training set is now **history produced at scale**: dense per-bar labels on every graded
instrument and timeframe, plus new-information blocks. **Judging happens on history**, in this order: learn screen,
batched confirm, test_old, holdout last. Live rows are a **demo learning loop** and a harm and drift monitor.

The only data behind this card are outcome-blind counts: bars, days and r11-v2.1 tiers from `raw/` and
`raw/histdata_m1/_reports`, computed on 2026-11-26 with no price or label read.

**Status: proposed.**
- The quant signs the promotion rule by 2026-12-06.
- The spec is frozen by 2026-12-18.
- The first counted run is 2026-12-20 (G3 card).
- This card books no K. Real money stays owner-only.

**Rulings implemented:**
- ADR 0098 obj-v1 and its 2026-11-27 amendment (R-H: one cost quantile, ub95; R-I: the offset form).
- ADR 0085 amendments: Q1, Q3, Q5, R13, N12, N18, N26, A2, A4.
- ADR 0100.
- The 2026-11-25 snapshot-admission amendment.
- Lifecycle plan 2026-11-25: rulings R-B..R-V; items N1-N12, N18-N26, N30; amendments A4-A29.

**Owner-directive deviations from the lifecycle plan, demo only (section 4):**
- a daily head update (R-N says weekly);
- a weekly refit on history plus live rows (do-not-do 21);
- demo gating before a judged pass.

Versions made this way are learning versions and are **never evidence**.

**What follows for the design.**

## 2. Training set: history produced at scale

### 2.1 What is produced

**Dense labels.** Every closed bar of every (instrument, tf) is labelled, on both sides, under 8 pre-named exit
templates. Rev 1 used only the ten retired arms' signals.

**Templates T0-T7.**
- One definition repo-wide, shared with RL-1: W3-1 freezes them before any label count. If W3-1 has not frozen them
  by 12-02, the definitions below govern.
- Stop = k × ATR14_tf × sqrt(hold/h_ref), the W2-2 formula, floored at 1.5 × the session-median TR.

| Template | Exit |
|---|---|
| T0 | time exit at H |
| T1 | stop 1, target 1 |
| T2 | stop 1, target 2 |
| T3 | stop 1, target 3 |
| T4 | stop 1.5, target 1.5 |
| T5 | stop 1, trail 1 |
| T6 | stop 2, time exit at H |
| T7 | stop 1, break-even at +1 R, target 3 |

**Time barrier H by timeframe:**

| tf | 1m | 5m | 15m | 30m | 1h | 2h | 4h | 1d | 1w |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H | 60 min | 4 h | 8 h | 16 h | 1 d | 2 d | 5 d | 10 d | 20 d |

**Label.**
- Gross R at mid, with side-aware barriers (**r3**, A9), clipped at ±10 R.
- B months get close-to-close (T0) labels only, at every tf (A4).
- Bid-only HistData gets a synthetic ask from the cost of record. Its barrier labels are used only if they agree in
  sign with real-ask labels at ≥ 0.98 (N7).

**Uniqueness and weights.**
- Uniqueness is computed per instrument across all templates, sides and tfs (ADR 0085 Q3).
- The 16 heads of one bar count as **one event** for n_eff.
- Weights are normalised so Σw = Kish n_eff ÷ DEFF_day (N5).

**Offset (phase) candles, training only (R8).**
- P phases per tf ≥ 5m, at weight 1/P.
- Never used in OOF, calibration, thresholds, reads or n_eff (invariant (b)).

**Point in time.**
- Features come from fs-v1 (`goldbot/research/feature_store.py`, branch `feat/feature-store-v1`).
- A row's `available_utc` is the latest minute it reads.
- Every join is an `asof_join` on `available_utc`.
- The parity oracle against `features_at` must pass: max abs diff ≤ 1e-9, identical NaN mask, ≥ 2,000 decisions per
  instrument × tf.

**Tiers.** r11-v2.1, frozen by hash (`tiers_r11-v2.1.csv` sha256 e99a6326...; rule blob c6f283d7...).
- A and A-consistent: barrier labels and intraday features.
- B: close-to-close labels and daily features only.
- C, F and P: out, in every window.
- The tier is checked on the decision month, the label horizon and the feature lookback.
- **Dukascopy XAUUSD has no r11-v2.1 grade yet.** It is graded before any label count (data item D-1). The counts
  below are untiered.

**Windows.**
- Gold and FX: learn 2010-01..2017-12; confirm 2018-01..2025-09; test_old 2003..2009 where tiered.
- Index and energy: the R-L class windows (learn 2018-01..2021-12, confirm 2022-01..2025-09), frozen before any
  non-gold label (N8).
- The holdout (2025-10-01..2026-09-30) is sealed.
- One source per instrument-minute (A16).

### 2.2 Rows and n_eff (outcome-blind counts, computed 2026-11-26)

- Rows per head = 1m bars / tf minutes. Labels = rows × 16 heads.

**Counts that shape the design** (`raw/histdata_m1/_reports/tiers_r11-v2.1.csv`):
- HistData XAUUSD has 1,630 A/A-consistent learn days but only 149 confirm days: it is single-source, tier P, in
  2019-25. Gold therefore uses Dukascopy, once graded.

**What the table means.**
- **The history reads are powered** (5.1 table: 333-3,328 n_eff needed):
  - gold 1m-1h confirm n_eff is 2,000-7,000;
  - gold 2h is powered only at keep share ≤ 0.5;
  - gold 4h only at keep share ≤ 0.1;
  - gold 1d and 1w are pooled across classes or used as context.
- **Riley caps the input count.** Riley et al. 2019 (*Stat. Med.* 38(7):1262-1275; continuous outcome, shrinkage
  ≥ 0.9, design R² 0.0207) needs **430 n_eff per parameter**, so 40 inputs need about 17,200 n_eff.
  - Gold 1m (at ρ 0.05) and pooled FX 1m-5m can carry 40 inputs.
  - Gold 15m-1h can carry about 10-12.
  - Gold 4h can carry about 1.
- **Pooling across instruments adds less than the raw rows suggest** (rho_inst 0.5): 25 M FX rows are about 11,000
  n_eff.

### 3.3 Model, loss and size (R-C, R-D, R-E, R-I, N5)

**Body per cell.** ĝ = a_cell + a_template×side + a_instrument + βᵀz.
- z is standardised with a frozen training scaler, after a causal per-source rank transform of the source-scaled
  columns (R-G).
- Median imputation from training.

**Loss.** Σ w (clip(g, ±10) − ĝ)² + λ(‖β‖² + Σ a²).
- The cell intercept is unpenalised.
- Solved in closed form in float64. There are no iterations, so there is no early stopping.

**λ = 540 effective rows**, a fixed prior: τ = 0.05 R per input sd, residual variance 1.35 R².
- With these units, weights ×10 give the same fit, phase copies at 1/P give the real-only fit, and an overlap H
  raises the relative penalty by H.
- *Flagged for the quant:* N5 writes C_fold = 1/(λ0·n_eff), a constant shrinkage fraction. This card uses the Bayes
  form instead.

**Not allowed:** GBT, kNN, stacks, TabPFN, torch, and library selectors (R-D, R-E). GBT can come only through a later
card, after a linear block passes at ≥ +0.03 R.

**Size ladder.** Riley at 430 n_eff per parameter, on the count-only n_eff of the smallest training fold:
- L1: core + blocks ≤ 40;
- L2: core-12 + blocks;
- L3: core-6 + blocks;
- below L3: intercept-only. That is baseline B8, and it is never judged.

**Seeds.** The fit is deterministic. Seeds: bootstrap 20261206, B2 20261207, canaries 20261208.

### 3.4 Decision rule (the served rule is the judged rule)

**Keep iff ĝ(x) + δ − cost(x) > 0.**
- cost(x) is the ub95 cost of record for the broker × session (`all` cell), plus the replay base cost, in R at the
  template's stop. It is known at the decision and is never the realised cost.
- At serving, an arm candidate uses the template nearest its exit geometry.
- The arm-fire subset is reported separately (A12).

**Abstention.**
- Full abstention is allowed.
- ABSTAIN (fewer than 100 kept rows or 30 kept days) is decided from counts, at 0 K.

**Cost version.** One cost-of-record version is pinned per read.

### 3.5 Train/serve parity (blocking before any served score)

- **Code:** fs-v1, `features_at` and the engine frame agree.
- **Source:** the A8 battery. Each column needs rank correlation ≥ 0.9 between history, ICM and Vantage, or it is
  dropped before any outcome is read.
- **Live admission:** the 2026-11-25 rules.
- **Artifact:** the frozen scaler and the pinned library versions ship with it (A11).

### Step 4: the live loop (owner directive 2026-11-25), per cell

**Explore sample.**
- A randomised 10 % of model-rejected candidates is still traded on demo.
- Assignment: hash_u(candidate id) < 0.10, made before the RiskGate. Propensity 0.10 is logged. A refused sample
  candidate is logged as `explore_refused`.
- Sample fills measure replay-vs-fill on the skipped set. They never feed the H-81 cost stop or the edge cap, and
  they never become labels.
- Real-money accounts have no sample (ADR 0098 §3).

**Daily head update.** The intercept δ per (broker, cell), estimated from the new resolved replay labels of every
candidate. Never fills, and never kept-only rows.
- δ = Σ v (g − ĝ) / (Σ v + n0).
- n0 comes from Stage A: the simplest entry of `N0_GRID` within 1 SE of the best, on 2015-17 pseudo-live. The rule is
  fixed now.
- An update is applied only when there are ≥ 50 new rows.
- Each step is limited to ±0.01 R a day, and the total to ±0.05 R per body version.
- Beyond a limit, δ is clipped and the child is marked `out_of_scope`.

**Weekly refit.** A new **learning body version**, fitted on history plus every live training-week row.
- Live rows are up-weighted by `LIVE_UPWEIGHT`.
- The spec, inputs, λ and frozen scaler stay the same.
- **Reserve weeks** (odd ISO weeks from 2026-10-01, N12) never train. They are held out for harm checks and judged
  reads only.
- Rows whose horizon runs into the next reserve week are purged.

**Versions (N18).**
- Every daily child and every weekly body gets sha = H(body, head, n0 table, training manifest, cost version).
- Each has a lineage row: the A19 fields plus `reference_bins` per source.
- Every scored row stores the scoring sha at decision.

**Automatic rollback.**
- Each served version runs its own harm sequence (5.5).
- When it fires, serving reverts to the parent version, or to the history-only body v0, at the next decision.
- The fired version is quarantined and never served again. The event is logged and posted.
- A rollback never increases a size and never touches an exit.

**Every cell.** Each (tf × class) cell joins the loop as its step 1 data and step 2 report land. A class with no cost
of record ABSTAINs (do-not-do 31).

**Scope of the owner-directive deviations.**
- The daily update (R-N says weekly) and the weekly refit on live rows (do-not-do 21) apply only to **learning versions
  on demo**.
- A learning version is never a judged version and never pools into a judged read.
- Judged evidence comes only from the frozen history procedure (5.3).

## 5. Evaluation (history first)

### 5.1 Stage 0, per cell (0 K; a refusal is NOT STARTED)

**1. Leak suite (N25).**
- truncation invariance;
- feature shift: +1 bar must spike, −1 bar must not move;
- oracle canaries at +0.03, +0.05 and +0.10 R are recovered;
- shuffle band 0.5 ± 4·SE(n) (R-R);
- a planted 0.52 leak and a planted 1-bar look-ahead both fail;
- holdout, post-window and C/F/P rows raise;
- the D9 seeded-future-release test for X1 and X4.

**2. Shuffled-label keep check (N6, offset form).** With gross labels permuted in (instrument, day) blocks, the keep
set must equal the intercept-only (B8) keep set within 5 pp per (cell, session).

**3. Information ceiling (N3).**
- (a) top-decile within-cell net R > 0 for at least one template × side with ≥ 300 rows;
- (b) the required IC (synthetic-score method on the cell's learn net R, 200 seeds), reported against the observed IC
  per block;
- (c) oracle S ≥ +0.05 R.

A cell that fails (a) for the core and for every block gets no Stage L K.

**4. Power (A22).** n_eff needed at the label-blind keep share k̂:

| k̂ | 0.10 | 0.20 | 0.33 | 0.50 | 1.00 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| kept mean implied by S = +0.05 R (μ < 0) | 0.50 | 0.25 | 0.15 | 0.10 | 0.05 |
| n_eff, 80 % power, one-sided 0.05 | 333 | 666 | 1,109 | 1,664 | 3,328 |
| n_eff, Holm rank 1 of 2 at 0.10 | 475 | 949 | 1,582 | 2,373 | 4,747 |

A unit whose 2.2 n_eff for a window falls below its row is NOT STARTED for that window.

### 5.2 Baselines (N2, A23) and block tests

| Id | Baseline |
|---|---|
| B0 | abstain |
| B1 | take-all |
| B2 | random keep at the model's share, per (template × side, week) |
| B3 | per-template × side intercept |
| B4 | the best non-ML rule chosen on learn, from exactly two families: (i) cost decile q1..q9; (ii) (template × side × session) cells with learn net > 0 and n ≥ 100 |
| B5 | per cell (template × side × session × vol_tercile): keep where learn net > 0 and n ≥ 100 |
| B6 | trailing 52-week on/off switch per template × side |
| B7 | side- and hour-matched random twin (the control leg) |
| B8 | intercept-only procedure (β ≡ 0, same head) |

- A model must beat max(B0, B3, B4, B5, B6, B8) on point S. The paired CI is reported.

**Block test** (nested A/B on learn OOF 2012-17; a screen):
- ΔS = S(core + admitted blocks + X) − S(core + admitted blocks), on the same rows and folds, with the stationary
  day-block bootstrap.
- **X is admitted iff ΔS ≥ +0.01 R, its one-sided p ≤ 0.10 and its incremental within-cell IC is > 0.**
- Blocks are tested in order, X1..X5. Each test costs K 1 (A2).
- X1 is OMB-1's own read and is not re-run.

### 5.3 Judged reads (reading rule fixed now)

**Units.**

| Unit | Judged as |
|---|---|

| gold 2h + 4h | pooled |

If H-102's gold 1h+4h Stage L runs, sr-v3's gold 1h and 2h+4h Stage L **is that read**: one invocation, one K, no
second look.

**Gate.** Every condition must hold.
1. S = mean(net R × keep) − max(0, mean net R), per candidate per judged unit (R-M), is ≥ +0.05 R, with one-sided 95 %
   lower bound > 0.
2. Kept mean net > 0.
3. Gross skill, k × (kept gross − all gross), is ≥ 0.
4. Control leg: kept mean − the twin's mean ≥ +0.05 R.
5. Holm across the batch on max(p_S, p_ctrl) ≤ 0.10.
6. S ≥ 0 at 2x cost with the keep set fixed (1.5x reported).
7. The baselines in 5.2 are beaten.
8. No N22 veto: shuffle, oracle, feature shift, or calibration slope CI wholly below 0.7.

**S split.** Every read reports S = gross skill + cost avoidance + base. A pass whose gross-skill CI includes 0 is
labelled **cost-avoidance-led**.

### 5.4 Training report contents (step 2; descriptive, never a selector)

The report covers, per cell:
- counts and n_eff, and the Riley level;
- Stage 0 results;
- baselines B0-B8, and S with its split;
- kept share and £/day at 0.01 lot;
- weighted calibration slope and intercept, with CI;
- R²_OOS;
- within-cell IC and AUC (never pooled);
- the train-to-OOF gap;
- half-split coefficient sign agreement;
- a learning curve at 1k, 3k, 10k, 30k, 100k and all rows. It shows whether more dense volume helps;
- the block ΔS table;
- canary recovery;
- score decile × (win rate, net R);
- arm-fire subset S;
- offset bias;
- staleness.

**Stop rule (N10):** a flat learning curve plus a calibration slope near 0 retires "a new learner on the same inputs".

### 5.5 Monitoring

**Harm sequence**, per served version.
- A predictable plug-in empirical-Bernstein confidence sequence (Waudby-Smith & Ramdas 2024, *JRSS-B* 86(1); Howard
  et al. 2021, *Ann. Statist.* 49(2)).
- It runs on per-day mean S contributions, rows clipped at ±10 R, α 0.05, model vs abstain.
- The counterfactual is complete: replay labels cover every candidate, and the explore sample checks replay against
  fills.
- Upper bound < 0 → **automatic rollback**. On an evidence version it also demotes, and re-promotion needs a new
  read.
- A simulation test asserts a false-alarm rate ≤ α at any stopping time, and detection of an injected −0.05 R.
- Rough detection times at gold 15m flow: −0.15 R in 1-2 months, −0.05 R in about a year.

**Head calibration sequence** on the mean residual per (broker, cell). If it lies wholly outside ±0.05 R, the head is
marked `out_of_scope` (WARN, new version).

**Drift is WARN only (A18, R-P).**
- PSI is sample-size-adjusted, per served input and source, against the stored `reference_bins`, with Holm across
  inputs, at ≥ 300 effective rows.
- PSI never triggers a size-down, a demotion or a retrain.
- Status `ok` needs n_with_features > 0, n_weights > 0 and ≥ 1 fitted body. Otherwise the status is `starved` (WARN;
  FAIL after 48 h).
- Health shows counts: history_rows, live_rows_added, versions, rollbacks, out_of_scope, and days since the last
  child.
