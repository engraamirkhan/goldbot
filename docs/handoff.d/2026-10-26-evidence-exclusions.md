- Evidence exclusions (prereg Amendment 4, quant ruling 2026-10-26, blind). New append-only aux.db table `evidence_exclusions` (migration 19). Criterion v1 voids an exploration candidate or trade when its decision window (201 bars of the arm spec's timeframe, at least 1,000 minutes) overlaps a recorded dq quarantine span, or when it was decided before its engine restarted on the corrected bars. A scheduled cost probe entered inside a span is void for the cost cells only. Readers that leave void rows out: the nightly posterior, the H-62 models and their retrain counts; the n toward the 300 read; the cost table's probe fills; the daily review's refit cells. Readers that keep them: the H-61 stops, P&L and broker reconciliation. The daily review prints a "N trades void as evidence" line. The nightly learner records the lookback half itself and skips the night if it cannot (fail closed). The restart half needs `scripts/evidence_exclusions.py --reloaded icm-demo=<UTC> --reloaded icm-probe=<UTC> --apply` on the brain after deploy (dry run first). Note: swing arms read 33 days (4h) or 201 days (1d) back, so they stay void that long after the span.
