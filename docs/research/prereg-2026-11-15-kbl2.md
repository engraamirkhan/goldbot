## 2. The level extractor: KBL-1 rules 1-5 plus one rule (frozen by hash)

Condition 2: "Exactly ONE change: a slash-list rule. Text: 'Three or more price tokens joined only by '/' are
separate singletons. A lone two-token 'A/B' stays a pair under rule 2.'"

- **Rules 1-5** are KBL-1 section 2, verbatim. They are implemented by the frozen G extractor
  `goldbot/research/ladders/extract.py`. That file stays byte-identical at sha256 `f9e8a5c1...e50885`, and
  `tests/research/test_ladders_extract_kbl2.py::test_the_g_extractor_file_is_byte_identical_to_its_frozen_hash`
  holds it there.
- **Rule 2a (the one change).** "Three or more price tokens joined only by '/' are separate singletons. A lone
  two-token 'A/B' stays a pair under rule 2." As implemented, and fixed before any audit post:
  - A slash list is a maximal run of numbers in which every two neighbours are joined by '/' and whitespace only.
  - It is a list when at least 3 of its numbers are price tokens under rule 1. A number in the list that is not a
    price token (a shorthand, an out-of-band number, a year) is dropped.
  - Each price token of a list is a zero-width zone [x, x] (rule 3). It is never a member of a pair, even when a number
    outside the list is joined to it by '-', '–', '—' or 'to'. That outside number is then read as the G extractor
    reads any number with no partner.
  - Everything else is the G extractor, unchanged. The list's span is masked, the G extractor reads the rest, and
    the list's singletons join before the rule 4 merge. A lone 'A/B' is not a list, so rule 2 still decides it: one
    zone within W\*, two levels beyond.
- **Code.** `goldbot/research/ladders/extract_kbl2.py` imports the G extractor and adds the rule; it changes no
  other behaviour (test: text without a slash list reads exactly as the G extractor).
- **Frozen hash.** KBL-2 extractor sha256 `97d3be885af5664dba03b244a7a81b0c02c6ceea74d2003d9e78d954d3c83f5f`
  (`tests/research/test_ladders_extract_kbl2.py::test_the_kbl2_extractor_hash_is_the_one_recorded_in_the_card`).
- **Development.** As the ruling allows, the rule was developed only on the exposed set (posts 2026-10-01 onward) and
  on the format of the 6 failing posts (spent). It was never developed on a KBL-2 audit post.

## 5. Power check before any outcome (condition 4)

"Power check before any outcome (≥ 0.5 at 0.10 R on the ADR 0085 basis: sd 1.2, rho_day 0.25, n_eff from outcome-blind
event counts) and 2021-22 coverage for the P7 block — compute only after the audit passes."

- **Formula.**
  - n_eff = n / (1 + (m_day − 1) × 0.25), where m_day is events per day with an event.
  - power = Φ(0.10 × √n_eff / 1.2 − z), with z = 1.645 (one-sided 0.05). This is the basis on which the ruling
    reports U-G's "about 0.90 (sd 1.2, ρ_day 0.25, m_day 1.86, n_eff ≈ 1,216)".
  - One instrument, so ADR 0085's rho_inst term drops out.
- **Inputs.** n and the days come from the U-K engine's taken events after caps and refusals, at zero swap. The
  engine's exit, R and P&L columns are dropped before anything is kept or printed.
- **Output.** Events, days with events, n_eff and power. Also events, days and n_eff for 2021 and 2022 (the P7
  block's coverage), plus the median stop and target distances.
- **Command.** `python scripts/kbl2.py power`. It refuses to run unless `~/tmp/kbl2/audit/audit-result.json` shows a
  pass for this extractor hash.
- **Verdict.**
  - Power < 0.5 means NOT STARTED (power) (ADR 0085): no outcome is read, and U-K parks to Q1.
  - Thin 2021-22 coverage is reported against P7 before any outcome.
