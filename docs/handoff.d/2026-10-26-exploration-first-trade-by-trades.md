- Exploration first-trade rule: "first" is now by trades, not P&L. An arm whose trade closed today (flat or in profit, so its netted realised loss is 0) and that has nothing open is no longer fresh: its share of the pot governs again. Before, such an arm traded at its per-trade cap on every bar. A trade opened yesterday and closed today counts as today's (its loss is in today's figure).
