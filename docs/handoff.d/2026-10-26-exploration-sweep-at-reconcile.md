- Exploration: a position closed at the broker (its stop or target) is swept at every reconciliation (`reconcile_every_s`, 30 s of tick time) once its exit deal is seen (`exploration.sweep`, called from `Engine._reconcile`), so its arm, pot and host slots and its open risk are free for the next signal. Before, they were held until the next decision-bar close, which kept 1m/5m arms from re-entering. A close with no exit deal yet, or unreadable deals, is still confirmed by the bar-close sweep. Order/exit path change: for trading-safety review.
