## 0. What binds every 15m idea (read this first)

1. **Cost, not power, is the test.** The 15m yardstick is about **0.27 R per round trip** at a 1.5-ATR stop on a
   normal day (spread 0.19 of a 15m ATR ≈ 0.13 R, plus ≈ 0.14 R of the 0.37 $/oz extra cost at the mean 15m ATR of
   $1.80). London and New York are cheaper per spread (0.178 and 0.153 of a 15m ATR, holiday note baseline), but at
   H-14's cost-gate floor (ATR(15m) = the round trip) one round trip is **up to 0.67 R**. On US early-close holidays
   spread/ATR rises ×1.27–1.65 for the day and ×1.7–2.5 in New York (holiday note, Table 2). The best gross edge any
   goldbot trial has measured is +0.061 R (tsmom 1h, R-06); every 15m family tested had none (R-01, R-02, R-05).
2. **What a survivor must show (O-5/O-6 under ADR 0022 and E-6).** Per-trade payoff +2 R / −1 R (3.0/1.5 ATR), so
   the per-trade SD is about 1.41 R. Gate 1 at K ≈ 60 (the adopted Q-H02U case) needs t ≈ 4.0. With n trades on G
   entry days, m = n/G and within-day correlation ρ, the day-clustered se is 1.41·sqrt(DE/n), DE = 1 + (m − 1)ρ:

   | n trades | G days | m | ρ = 0.1: se | net mean for gross-screen t ≥ 2 at 80% power | net mean for t ≈ 4.0 (gate 1) | **gross needed at 0.27 R cost** |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 900 (floor case, 0.9% retention) | 700 | 1.3 | 0.048 R | 0.14 R | 0.19 R | **0.46 R** |
   | 1,500 | 1,000 | 1.5 | 0.037 R | 0.11 R | 0.15 R | **0.42 R** |
   | 3,000 | 1,500 | 2.0 | 0.027 R | 0.08 R | 0.11 R | **0.38 R** |
   | 9,000 (O-4 top tercile, upper case) | 3,000 | 3.0 | 0.016 R | 0.05 R | 0.07 R | **0.34 R** |

   ρ = 0.3 moves the last column by at most +0.01 R. The day-summed DSR (E-6, G ≥ 200 days) is the same statistic on
   day sums and adds skew/kurtosis, so it raises these bars a little, never lowers them. Approximation: normal
   quantiles, E[max Z_K] from Bailey and López de Prado, skew and kurtosis ignored (as `preregistration-2027Q1.md`
   "Gate-1 power", point 3). Script: the formula above; nothing was read from data.
   **Every candidate below therefore needs a gross edge of roughly 0.35–0.45 R per trade, five to seven times the
   largest gross edge goldbot has ever measured.** The prior that any 15m candidate passes is low; the expected
   outcome of O-4 is another recorded retirement, and the plan for that case is already written (`research-plan.md`
   §7).
3. **One trial screens them all.** Every directional candidate below enters through O-4 (H-14 discovery, 1 trial,
   group unit, K_eff = groups screened). None gets its own trial unless its group survives O-4 and earns O-5/O-6, or a
   written research-director note funds a Q2 neighbour from the free reserve. Overlays and label mechanics cost 0.
4. **Leakage and the cap.** Every new column is point-in-time on closed bars (a level, sweep, gap or window is known
   only once its defining bars have closed), joins any non-bar table through `asof_join` on `available_utc`, and must
   leave `pipeline.lookahead_check` at 0 columns. A survivor model has at most 39 columns + `side` = 40.

### C-1 Cost-in-R gate floor and spread regime (rank 1)

- **Mechanism.** Costs are fixed in USD per lot, R scales with ATR, so cost in R is time-varying and is the largest
  term at 15m (SoA A7; cost in R was 2–3× higher in 2012–2019 than in 2024–26). Removing candidates that cannot pay
  their round trip raises net R per trade without any alpha (H-13). Spread spikes (rollover, releases, thin days)
  are the same channel.
- **Evidence.** Measured, ours: holiday note (Dukascopy 1m, 2010-01..2025-09, XAUUSD, cost measurement, no R):
  spread/ATR 0.19 normal, ×1.27–1.65 on US holidays. Lundström 2013, Umeå Economic Studies 861 (WP): ORB returns
  differ by 150–200 bp/day across volatility states in crude oil and the S&P 500, gross *(unverified)*, S. ESMA 2018
  (regulatory): 74–89% of retail CFD accounts lose. Not an edge claim; a cost fact.
- **Data.** Exists: `spread_mean`, `spread_max` per 15m bar (data-v1, Dukascopy, not IC Markets); columns
  `spread_atr`, `spread_rel_median_48`, `em_*_cost` exist. Needs G1 for final counts; broker spreads only forward
  (H-A). `costs-v1` absent (priors pinned at the freeze if not published by 2026-12-11).
- **0-cost diagnostic D-1 (owed by the O-4 row already: "quartiles of cost in R across the gated candidates").**
  Inputs at the decision bar only (ATR14 of 15m mid, the bar's `spread_mean`, 0.37 $/oz); no price after the
  decision bar is read. Output: quartiles of round-trip cost in R (= (spread + 0.37) / (1.5 · ATR)) over the H-14
  window, by year, by session (London only / overlap / New York only) and on v3 THIN days vs others, plus the
  candidate count at gate floors 1×, 1.5× and 2× round trip, per year and per 15m test fold (`splits_for(...,
  "15m")` on a timestamps-only frame). Before `calendar-v1` it runs without the tier-1 blackout and is labelled a
  superset; it is rerun after G1 and after `calendar-v1`.

  ```

  python -I <repo>/scripts/fetch_data_release.py --download-only            # raw/: data-v1 1m 2010..2025 only
  python -I <repo>/scripts/scalp_diagnostics.py cost-r --bars raw --us-calendar raw-calendar \
      --gate-multiples 1 1.5 2 --out-json d1_cost_r.json --out-md d1_cost_r.md
  ```
  `scripts/scalp_diagnostics.py` does not exist yet: it is owed by the strategy researcher (Fri 2026-10-30, with the
  W1-5 H-13 work), built on `count_owed_events.count_h14`'s window/blackout/gate code, with the same AST guard as
  `tests/test_count_owed_events.py` (imports no label, return or statistics function) and the same holdout cut at
  `research.holdout_from`. Without `raw-calendar` it reports "blackout not applied".
- **Reading rule (written now, before D-1 runs).** Let q50 and q75 be the pooled cost-in-R quartiles at the 1× floor.
  (a) If q50 ≤ 0.35 R and q75 ≤ 0.50 R: no change; O-4 keeps the 1× floor. (b) Otherwise the strategy researcher
  proposes to the quant reviewer, before any H-14 count is pasted and before the freeze, to raise the floor to the
  smallest of 1.5× / 2× at which q75 ≤ 0.50 R, **provided** that floor leaves ≥ 55,000 candidate bars (the count at
  which the coded rule's 0.9% floor-case retention still gives about 500 trades); if no floor meets both, the 1×
  floor stays and the O-4 row records that the median candidate costs more than 0.35 R. The choice uses inputs only,
  so it is not a look at outcomes. Either way the H-13 overlay (net R with and without the filter) and H-19 (net R by
  HAR band) are reported on O-4's taken trades and never read.
- **Power.** Not a test; a count (≥ 100,000 bars expected, count owed).
- **Kill.** None as a filter. As a feature group (`microstructure`) it follows O-4's rule.

### C-2 Intrabar label resolution and excursion reporting (rank 2)

- **Mechanism (label validity, not alpha).** `labels/triple_barrier.py` resolves a bar that touches both barriers as
  "stop first". At 3.0/1.5 ATR on 15m bars a single bar must span 4.5 ATR for a tie, so ties should be rare except
  on jump bars, but any exit research with tighter stops (C-8) would bias gross R down for every candidate. data-v1
  holds 1m bid/ask OHLC, so ties can be resolved on the 1m path (still a convention inside a 1m bar).
- **Evidence.** Mechanical; `labels/excursion.py` documents the same bias for MAE/MFE.
- **Data.** Exists (1m bid/ask in data-v1). Code: E-2 would need a 1m tie-break option (not built).

  the share of H-14-window 15m bars whose own mid range ≥ 4.5 × ATR14 at the previous close (an upper bound on the

### C-3 Liquidity sweeps of session and day extremes (rank 3; H-08 at 15m)

- **Mechanism.** Stop-loss orders cluster just beyond visible extremes (Asia high/low before London, the previous
  session's and day's high/low); when price runs them, the cascade exhausts and price returns inside (reversal), or a
  close beyond them triggers further stops (continuation). Both signs are allowed: `side` is a model input and both
  sides are labelled.
- **Evidence.** Osler 2003, *JF* (NY Fed SR125), FX dealer order book, gross; Osler 2005, *JIMF* 24(2) 219–241
  (survey R9, R10): take-profits cluster at round numbers, stops just beyond them, cascades follow. R for clustering
  in FX, **N for gold**, gross only. No peer-reviewed SMC/ICT test (survey R19).
- **Data.** Exists: `market_structure` v2 (`sweep_high/low/dir` against the last confirmed swing, previous session and
  previous day, on the sweep bar only), `session_zones` (distances to Asia/London/NY/PDH/PDL/PWH/PWL). Missing (new
  family `liquidity`, F-1): sweep depth in ATR, bars since the last sweep by reference (Asia / previous session / PDH-L
  / swing), and a "swept then reclaimed within k bars" flag, all on closed bars. Needs G1 for final counts.
- **0-cost diagnostic D-3 (event counts and fold coverage, no outcome).** Per reference: sweep bars in the H-14
  window after the cost gate, per year and per 15m test fold, and the share of candidates with a sweep in the last 4
  bars.
  ```
  python -I <repo>/scripts/scalp_diagnostics.py events --bars raw --us-calendar raw-calendar \
      --events sweep_asia sweep_prev_sess sweep_pd sweep_swing --out-json d3_events.json
  ```
- **Reading rule (written now).** The `liquidity` group enters O-4 only if the pooled sweep events are ≥ 1,000 and
  every complete test fold has ≥ 30; otherwise its columns are recorded "underpowered, not tested" (not "failed") and
  stay out of the pool, so K_eff is not spent on a group that cannot be seen. In O-4 the group is read by O-4's rule
  (stability ≥ 0.7, AUC ≥ 0.53 with weekly-block lower bound > 0.50, tercile rule net > 0 on ≥ 1,000 trades).
- **Power (estimate, count owed).** About 1–2 sweep bars a day across references → 4,000–7,000 events over
  2012–2025 test folds. A rule-only follow-up at n = 4,000, m ≈ 1.2 has se ≈ 0.023 R, so t ≥ 2 needs ≈ 0.07 R
  gross, but net > 0 needs > 0.27 R gross: cost binds, not power.
- **Kill.** O-4 AUC < 0.52, or the `liquidity` group not selected (stability < 0.7): sweeps retired as a 15m entry
  signal, kept as features. A survivor that fails O-5/O-6: retired as an entry signal (O-5/O-6 rule).

### C-4 Round-number and stop-cluster levels incl. the $100 step (rank 4; H-05)

- **Mechanism.** As C-3 (Osler): orders cluster at round prices; at $4,000+ the natural levels are $50/$100, which
  the current `round_numbers` ($5/$10/$25/$50) under-weights.
- **Evidence.** Aggarwal & Lucey 2007, *Rev. Financial Economics* 16(2) (playbook 1.18): $100 levels act as barriers
  for gold, daily and intraday, gross, S (gold). Osler 2003/2005, R for FX clustering.
- **Data.** Exists for $5–$50. Missing: the $100 step (D-5), placed in family `liquidity` (F-1), and a
  "first touch today" flag. Note the levels are scale-dependent: $5 at $1,200 is 0.4% of price, at $4,000 0.13%; the
  `*_dist_atr` scaling handles distance, not salience.
- **0-cost diagnostic D-4.** Count of candidates within 0.5 ATR of each step, per year and fold (no outcome):
  ```
  python -I <repo>/scripts/scalp_diagnostics.py events --bars raw --us-calendar raw-calendar \
      --events round_50_near round_100_near --near-atr 0.5 --out-json d4_round.json

- **Reading rule.** As C-3 (≥ 1,000 events, ≥ 30 per fold, else "underpowered, not tested"); read inside O-4.
- **Power.** $100 touches are rare (estimate a few hundred a year in 2020–2025, far fewer at $1,200–1,800): likely
  below 1,000 near-events in the window, in which case the $100 column is underpowered by the rule above.
- **Kill.** As C-3.

### C-5 Tick-rate / activity features from the holiday measurement (rank 5)

- **Mechanism.** Thin markets raise cost in R (measured) and, for a sweep or breakout, a move on high relative
  activity is more likely to be order flow than noise. Intraday activity has a strong time-of-day pattern, so it must
  be compared with the same time of day (Andersen & Bollerslev 1997, intraday periodicity, survey R25
  *(unverified)*). "Stocks in play" with high relative volume carried ORB returns in US equities (Zarattini, Barbon,
  Aziz 2024, SSRN, net of commissions per the summary *(unverified)*, S, N for gold).
- **Evidence, ours.** Holiday note (BACKLOG 45; Dukascopy `volume_bid`, 2010-01..2025-09): New York `volume_bid` per
  hour ×0.20–0.40 and spread/ATR ×1.7–2.5 on US early-close holidays; CN/JP holidays thin Asia without a cost rise.
  This is liquidity, not direction.
- **Data, with a caveat that matters.** data-v1 has **no tick count**: `tick_count` is Dukascopy `volume_bid`
  (`scripts/dukascopy_year.py:163`), summed into 15m bars. Every "tick-rate" column is therefore a Dukascopy volume
  proxy, named as such. Broker ticks exist only forward (H-A, tick recorder not yet running). Existing columns:
  `tick_count_z_48`, `tick_vol_ratio_20`. Missing (family `activity`, F-1): volume_bid in the last 4 bars ÷ its
  20-day median at the same time of day; quote fraction over the last hour (1m bars present ÷ nominal); a v3 THIN-day
  flag (from `config/holidays_cme_metals.yaml`, known in advance, so no look-ahead).
- **0-cost diagnostic D-5.** Coverage and redundancy only: share of finite values on H-14-window bars; Spearman
  correlation of each new column with the existing `microstructure` and `volatility` columns over 2010–2011 (feature
  values only, no label).
  ```
  python -I <repo>/scripts/scalp_diagnostics.py coverage --bars raw --families activity microstructure volatility \
      --redundancy-to 2011-12-30 --out-json d5_activity.json
  ```
- **Reading rule.** A new column enters the pool if finite on ≥ 95% of window bars and |ρ| < 0.95 with every existing
  column (else it is a duplicate and is dropped before the group map hash). Inside O-4, the `activity` group is read
  by O-4's rule. Separately, net R of O-4's taken trades by THIN vs normal day is reported (H-B style, never read).
- **Power.** Not separately testable; a conditioning group.
- **Kill.** As O-4. If dropped as a duplicate: recorded "redundant with <column>", not "failed".

### C-6 London/New York overlap, LBMA auctions and the 08:30 ET window (rank 6)

- **Mechanism.** Liquidity concentrates in the overlap (London afternoon + New York morning), in the 30-minute order
  windows before the LBMA auctions (10:30 and 15:00 London) and at 08:30 ET (US data, old COMEX pit open).
  Concentrated flow can make a 15m move more informative, and it makes costs cheapest in R (NY spread/ATR 0.153).
- **Evidence.** IBA/LBMA auction rules (playbook 2.9), P. The pre-2015 fix front-running edge is gone (Caminschi &
  Heaney 2014, *J. Futures Markets* 34(11), GC and GLD 2008–2013, gross, S; venue reformed 2015): **do not test fix
  front-running** (R-08). 08:30 ET releases dominate gold's intraday volatility (Cai, Cheung, Wong 2001, *JFM* 21(3),
  COMEX 1994–97, R for volatility); direction: none documented except FOMC adjustment beyond 5 min (Awartani,
  Hussain, Virk 2024, *IRFA* 95 *(unverified)*, S).
- **Data.** Exists: DST-aware sessions (`min_since_london_open`, `min_since_newyork_open`, `session_id`). Missing (D-5,
  family `activity`): minutes to/since the AM and PM auction, an overlap flag, minutes since 08:30 ET on non-tier-1
  days. Tier-1 bars are already removed by the blackout (needs `calendar-v1`, owner key due 2026-11-30).
- **0-cost diagnostic D-6.** Counts of H-14 candidates per window (pre-AM-auction 30 min, pre-PM-auction 30 min,
  overlap, 08:30–09:30 ET non-tier-1), per year and fold, with the D-1 cost quartiles per window.
  ```
  python -I <repo>/scripts/scalp_diagnostics.py events --bars raw --us-calendar raw-calendar \

- **Reading rule.** As C-5 (coverage/redundancy for entry; read inside O-4). The per-window cost quartiles feed C-1's

### C-7 Post-jump continuation vs reversal (rank 7; H-10 at 15m)

- **Mechanism.** A jump on news is information and may continue; a jump without news is often a liquidity event
  (stop cascade) and may revert.
- **Evidence.** Lee & Mykland 2008, *RFS* 21(6) (survey R17), a jump detector, S as a detector; post-jump drift
  untested for gold. Prices adjust within minutes after releases (Elder, Miao, Ramchander 2012, *JBF* 36(1), 2002–08
  *(unverified)*), so 15m continuation after scheduled news is doubtful (SoA B3).
- **Data.** Exists: `jumps` (`jump_z`, `jump_flag`, `jump_sign`, `bars_since_jump`) in family `volatility`. The
  news/no-news split needs `calendar-v1` (`min_since_last_tier1`, opt-in `--us-calendar-features`), which the H-14
  spec currently leaves out ("no macro … columns"); tier-1 events are inside the blackout anyway, so the split is
  mostly tier-2 and is not available (H-11 blocked, owner ruling C).
- **0-cost diagnostic D-7.** Jump-bar counts in the window, per year and fold:
  `python -I <repo>/scripts/scalp_diagnostics.py events --bars raw --us-calendar raw-calendar --events jump_k4 --out-json d7_jumps.json`
- **Reading rule.** Read inside O-4 as part of `volatility` (no separate entry). No change to the H-14 spec.
- **Power.** At k = 4 bipower sigmas, expect well under one jump bar a day; a separate rule would likely sit below the
  1,000-event floor (count owed). Not proposed as a trial.
- **Kill.** As O-4.

### C-8 Cost-aware exits: wider barrier and runner (rank 8; next generation)

- **Mechanism.** Cost in R falls in proportion to the stop: at 2.25 ATR the typical round trip is ≈ 0.18 R instead
  of 0.27 R. A runner (H-17's V1: 50% at 2 ATR, chandelier 2.5 ATR) pays only with serial correlation.
- **Evidence.** Kaminski & Lo 2014 (trailing exits pay only with serial correlation, S); Zarattini, Aziz, Barbon 2024
  (noise-area trend days with trailing and clock exits, SPY, net Sharpe 1.33, S, N for gold); Kurth, Eisler, Rej,
  Bouchaud 2026, arXiv 2607.01550 (≈100 futures 1995–2025: short-term trend gone on small-tick contracts since about
  2009; gold is small-tick), S, which argues against runners at 15m.
- **Data.** Exists (exit policies in `labels/exit_policy.py`). An exit change is a new label, so a new trial.
- **0-cost diagnostic D-8.** Only after O-4's verdict: MAE/MFE distribution of O-4's taken trades (C-2), already
  part of O-4's record. Before O-4 nothing is run.
- **Reading rule (for a Q2 neighbour, written now).** Funded from the free reserve by a written research-director
  note **only if** O-4 reads continue for some group; the neighbour is that group's O-5/O-6 configuration with
  barrier 4.5/2.25 ATR and 24 bars (flat 16:30 New York unchanged), read by O-5/O-6's rule (design gates + day-summed
  DSR ≥ 0.95 at G ≥ 200). It is never chosen by comparing exits on O-4's data.
- **Power.** As the O-5/O-6 table (§0.2) with cost 0.18 R: gross ≈ 0.25–0.37 R needed.
- **Kill.** O-4 stop or inconclusive → not funded. Neighbour fails → retired with its parent.
