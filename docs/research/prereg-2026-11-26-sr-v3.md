### 2.3 Riley and the ladder (quant issue 1, closing text)

"2.2 Riley and the ladder are computed per judged unit on the smallest training fold (2010-11 for OOF 2012), at rho_day
0.25 (0.05 only with the Q1 file), × the Kish factor. Parameters = β columns + template×side and instrument levels,
counted as ridge effective df at λ = 540 on a label-blind fixture. The ladder level per unit is published in this card
before Stage 0."

**Remedy chosen now, before any label is read** (orchestrator ruling on issue 1). Three parts, applied in this order:
1. **(a) Pool per timeframe.** Gold, FX10 and every other graded A/A-consistent instrument share one body per
   timeframe, with instrument levels.
2. **(b) First OOF year 2014.** Combined with (a) for any unit still below L3 after (a). The smallest fold becomes
   2010-13.
3. **One judged head per timeframe (lifecycle A12: "one pre-named head feeds filters; the others are
   diagnostic").**
   - The judged head is **T2 (stop 1, target 2), both sides**. Side enters as a β column, so there are no
     template×side levels in the judged body.
   - T0-T1 and T3-T7 are produced and fitted as diagnostic bodies, and they feed RL-1. They are never judged.
   - Without this choice the 15 template×side intercepts alone exceed every unit's budget.

**Size rule (rev 4, quant ruling Q1).** The gate is Riley on ridge effective df, computed label-blind on the
smallest training fold before Stage 0. It is not replaced by an OOF calibration slope or canaries. Those stay N22
vetoes and are reported, with Riley, in the training report. Each parameter is charged against the n_eff that informs
it:
- unit intercept and instrument levels: n_i = Kish n_eff ÷ [1 + (m̄ − 1)·ρ_u,day], where m̄ = Σm²/Σm per
  instrument-day and ρ_u,day is the residual (not outcome) same-day ICC, upper 95 % CI (D-12);
- β column j: n_j = Kish n_eff ÷ [1 + (m̄ − 1)·ρ_x,j,day·ρ_u,day], and when pooled, further
  ÷ [1 + (k − 1)·ρ_x,j,inst·ρ_u,inst]. ρ_x,j,day and ρ_x,j,inst are the within-day and same-day cross-instrument ICCs
  of column j on the fold's features, upper 95 % CI;
- df_p = n_p/(n_p + λ_p). Gate: Σ_p 430·df_p / n_p ≤ 1.

The β budget is the longest prefix of the level order (for the level) or the served order (for the served body) that meets the gate. Columns never enter out of order.
Level = prefix length (L3 ≥ 6, L2 ≥ 12, L1 ≥ 40). The ladder table is republished with these inputs before Stage 0
and recomputed mechanically at Stage 0.

**Level order (label-blind, fixed now):** core-12 in its 3.1 order, then the rest of P30 in its 3.1 order. The level
(L3/L2/L1) is the longest prefix of this order that meets the gate. It is set before Stage 0 and never changes with
block admission.

**Served order (after block admission):** core-6; then each admitted block, whole, in order X1..X6; then the rest of
core-12; then the rest of P30. The served body is the longest prefix of the served order that meets the gate, and
never fewer than core-6. A block enters whole or not at all. The first admitted block that does not fit ends the block
section, and no later block skips ahead.

X6 is appended last, label-blind, for three reasons: X1..X5 were registered in this order before X6 existed, and inserting ahead would change their admission chances after registration; X6 has the lowest stated prior (about 15 %); and a tight Riley budget should spend df on the higher-prior blocks first. No reserved slot: serving outside the Riley gate is refused. A unit whose budget ends before X6 reports X6 as 'not served (budget)', never as rejected; X6's block test (K 1) still runs wherever a gold unit reaches L3.

**Label-blind tie rule (signed, quant Q2).** Per unit at Stage 0, before any label of the unit is read, the 2.3 gate
is computed for the gold body and the pooled body on the same fold set. The unit uses the body with the higher level.
On equal level it uses the gold body. The choice, its inputs and their hashes are recorded in the Stage 0 report before
Stage L. The judged read population is the chosen body's (gold candidates only for the gold body), and its rows are the
revision 9 D1 population (rows usable under the served body's columns): gates 1-14, B0-B8, the twin, the S split and N22 are counted on it. 0 K. Instrument levels are ridge-shrunk towards the unit intercept with their own fixed prior
λ_a = 1.35/τ_a². It is never estimated from this unit's labels. The training report gives gold's shrunk and unshrunk
levels.

**LAMBDA_INST (quant ruling D-12 (d), ADR 0085 amendment "quant rulings D-12 a-d"):** "τ_a = max(0.01 R, upper 95 % CI
over the control grid and ≥2 replayed non-sr-v3/H-102 families, both-sides and long-only variants); λ_a uses the
pooled-body row (13,500 / 3,416 / 1,324 until the families run) for any membership, since it is the smallest λ of the
memberships considered; frozen by hash before Stage 0." The values come from `binding.tau_a` (pooled rows) of
`docs/research/rho-measurement-2026-11.json`. The families ran on 2026-11-26. The pooled-body τ_a is 0.041 / 0.055 /
0.063 R, so **λ_a = 820 / 441 / 341** (<= 60 min / 1-4 h / > 4 h). These are frozen by that file's hash before Stage 0.

**ρ_u,inst and ρ_x,j,inst are day-mean correlations (quant ruling D-12 (a)):** "ρ_u,inst (and ρ_x,j,inst) is the
cross-instrument correlation of instrument-day mean residuals (D-12 'day-mean'), upper 95 % CI, for the body's
membership and hold bucket; the per-candidate pair ICC never enters 1+(k−1)ρ_inst."

- n_eff per instrument is capped at about 3.5 a day for a 60-min hold, and about 1,380/hold a day for longer holds,
  whatever the bar count. More rows on the same days add nothing.
- Pooling helps only if instruments are nearly independent. A measured ρ_inst (R13: the upper 95 % CI of the
  within-class same-day ICC, from other families' learn candidates) with the Q1 file and (b) gives these levels
  (computed now, label-blind):

  | ρ_inst | 1m | 5m | 15m and slower |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0.2 | ~~L2~~ void | ~~below L3~~ void | ~~below L3~~ void |
  | 0.1 | ~~L1~~ void | ~~L3~~ void | ~~below L3~~ void |

  **Void (quant ruling D-12 (a), 2026-11-25).** These rows assumed a pair-level ρ_inst. The measured day-mean
  ρ_u,inst (D-12) is 0.40 for the pooled body and 0.44 for FX10 at ≤ 60 min, upper 95 % CI. No level above is
  predicted. The ladder is recomputed mechanically at Stage 0 from the committed D-12 file.

**The rule is mechanical.** At Stage 0 the ladder is recomputed by the Q1 formula from whatever measurements are
committed by then:
- ρ_x per column (D-12a, 12-02);
- residual ρ_u,day and ρ_u,inst and τ_a (D-12, 12-09);
- the per-tf Kish factor.

It is not re-chosen. **A unit below L3 is intercept-only (B8): NOT STARTED (size), 0 K.**

A unit with no usable graded rows under ADR 0104 §4 (for the column set at that level) is NOT STARTED (data), 0 K. No
body is fitted or served, not even intercept-only. Its arms run rule-only as today. The sufficiency contract carries it
as SHORT, with remedies D-1 (ii), D-13 and D-14. 1d and 1w are re-assessed by these same rules at each monthly regrade;
no other route opens them.

The rev-3 table above is the floor (ρ_x = 1). Every measured ρ_x < 1 raises a column's n_j, so slow-moving columns

#### 3.2.1 X6 price action (rev 7): columns, lookbacks, evidence

**Why this block.** Every served and training column today (3.1, registry `COLUMN_LOOKBACKS`) is an indicator: ATR,
RSI, EMA distance and slope, returns, session clock, vol estimators, Donchian, ADX, TSMOM, session high/low distance,
compression. None describes the shape of a bar, the swing structure, or where price sits against higher-timeframe
swing areas. The T2 head (stop 1, target 2, both sides) is a barrier label, so the room to the nearest higher-timeframe
area in the trade's direction against the room behind it is mechanically related to which barrier is hit first. That
is the information X6 names. It is not a claim that candlestick or SMC rules make money: they do not (R-09, H-40, R-RS).

**Columns (frozen; signed = enters as side × value, neutral 0).** All have bounded memory by construction: none is
NEEDS_STATE, so a fixed tail of bars reproduces the full-history value exactly, and the engine needs no carried state.

### 3.3 Model, loss and size (R-C, R-D, R-E, R-I, N5)

**Body per judged unit (timeframe), head T2.** ĝ = a_unit + a_instrument + βᵀz. Pooled across graded instruments per (a), or gold alone by the tie rule in 2.3. Diagnostic heads (T0-T1, T3-T7) get bodies of the same form; they are never judged.
- z is standardised with a frozen training scaler, after a causal per-source rank transform of the source-scaled
  columns (R-G).
- Median imputation from training.

**Loss.** Σ w (clip(g, ±10) − ĝ)² + λ(‖β‖²) + λ_a Σ a_instrument².
- The cell intercept is unpenalised.
- Solved in closed form in float64. There are no iterations, so there is no early stopping.

**λ = 540 effective rows**, a fixed prior: τ = 0.05 R per input sd, residual variance 1.35 R².
- With these units, weights ×10 give the same fit, phase copies at 1/P give the real-only fit, and an overlap H
  raises the relative penalty by H.
- **Quant ruling 2: the fixed 540-row prior is ACCEPTED.** The N5 test is amended: the shrinkage of a standardised
  coefficient equals n_eff/(n_eff + 540) on a fixture. The weights ×10, phase-copy and overlap-H tests are kept.

**Not allowed:** GBT, kNN, stacks, TabPFN, torch, and library selectors (R-D, R-E). GBT can come only through a later
card, after a linear block passes at ≥ +0.03 R.

**Size ladder (section 2.3).** The β budget comes from Riley, as ridge effective df on the smallest training fold.
- L1 (≥ 40): core + blocks ≤ 40.
- L2 (≥ 12): core-12 + blocks.
- L3 (≥ 6): core-6 + blocks.
- Below L3: intercept-only (B8), never judged.
- Block columns enter the served order right after core-6, whole blocks only (2.3). The level never depends on block admission.

**Seeds.** The fit is deterministic. Seeds: bootstrap 20261206, B2 20261207, canaries 20261208.

### 3.4 Decision rule (the served rule is the judged rule)

**Keep iff ĝ(x) + δ − cost(x) > 0.**
- **cost(x) (quant issue 3, closing text):** "cost(x) = full spread (once) + commission + slippage at ub95 + expected
  swap over the template's horizon (rollovers counted in [t, t+H], era swap of record). Realised net R in every read
  charges swap per rollover actually held. Test: a fixture decomposes cost(x) into exactly one spread, one commission,
  one slippage and n_rollover × swap."
- cost(x) uses **decision-time information only**. It never contains a realised leg. PR #461 found that the replay's
  realised cost leg leaks the outcome: the stop overshoot is charged only on stop exits. Training labels are gross, so
  they carry no cost leg at all.
- At serving, an arm candidate uses the template nearest its exit geometry.
- The arm-fire subset is reported separately (A12).

**Abstention.**
- Full abstention is allowed.
- ABSTAIN (fewer than 100 kept rows or 30 kept days) is decided from counts, at 0 K.

**Cost version.** One cost-of-record version is pinned per read.

**1m and 5m (PR #463 trading-safety F3; quant ruling, signed).** Gold 1m and 5m cells may gate with `GATE_WAIT_S` ≤ 5 s, once all three conditions hold. Until then they score in shadow.
1. **Latency measured.** At least 200 shadow decisions per cell on icm-probe record decision-to-feature-vector wait and decision-to-fill time. The p95 wait is ≤ 5 s and is pinned as `GATE_WAIT_P95_S` per cell.
2. **Wait charged in cost(x).** For gated 1m/5m cells, the slippage in cost(x) is measured from the decision-time mid (bar close) to the fill, not from the send price. It is taken at ub95 over those decisions and the cell's demo fills, and pinned in the cost of record before gating starts. Replay labels keep entry at the open of the minute containing decision + send_delay + `GATE_WAIT_P95_S`, and the time barrier runs from that entry.
3. **Fail-open reported separately.** Candidates that fail open (no vector within `GATE_WAIT_S`) are tagged and are excluded from the gated read and from n. Their share, by session and by ATR tercile, is reported in every read. A cell where it exceeds 5 % does not gate (the L2 check, per cell).

### Step 4: the live loop (owner directive 2026-11-25), per cell

**Explore sample.**
- A randomised 10 % of model-rejected candidates is still traded on demo.
- Assignment: hash_u(candidate id) < 0.10, made before the RiskGate. Propensity 0.10 is logged. A refused sample
  candidate is logged as `explore_refused`.
- Explore orders pass the RiskGate at the arm's normal size, never larger. They count toward every cap, drawdown halt,
  the kill switch and the money stops (PR #463 trading-safety F2). They are left out only of the H-81 cost stop, the
  edge cap, edge reads and n.
- Sample fills measure replay-vs-fill on the skipped set. They never feed the H-81 cost stop or the edge cap, and
  they never become labels.
- Real-money accounts have no sample (ADR 0098 §3).

**Daily head update.** The intercept δ per (broker, cell), estimated from the new resolved replay labels of every
candidate. Never fills, and never kept-only rows.
- δ = Σ v (g − ĝ) / (Σ v + n0).
- n0 comes from Stage A: the simplest entry of `N0_GRID` within 1 SE of the best, on 2015-17 pseudo-live. The rule is
  fixed now.
- An update is applied only when there are ≥ 50 new rows.
- Each step is limited to ±0.01 R a day.
- "The cumulative cap is measured against v0: |keep-threshold shift vs v0| ≤ 0.05 R, otherwise out_of_scope." Beyond
  a limit, δ is clipped and the child is marked `out_of_scope`.
- "All history reads score with δ ≡ 0. The head and n0 are live-only, so the n0 choice on 2015-17 touches no judged
  row."

**Weekly refit.** A new **learning body version**, fitted on history plus every live training-week row. "Weekly
refit history = 2010-17 until the confirm read is recorded, then 2010-01..2025-09; never 2025-10..2026-09."
- Live rows are up-weighted by `LIVE_UPWEIGHT`.
- The spec, inputs, λ and frozen scaler stay the same.
- **Reserve weeks** (odd ISO weeks from 2026-10-01, N12) never train. They are held out for harm checks and judged
  reads only.
- Rows whose horizon runs into the next reserve week are purged.

**Versions (N18).**
- Every daily child and every weekly body gets sha = H(body, head, n0 table, training manifest, cost version).
- Each has a lineage row: the A19 fields plus `reference_bins` per source.
- Every scored row stores the scoring sha at decision.

**Automatic rollback.**
- "Harm sequences run per body lineage, pooled across children inside the head limits, plus one cumulative per-cell
  sequence over all learning versions versus abstain." Each runs as in 5.5.
- When it fires, serving reverts to the parent version, or to the history-only body v0, at the next decision.
- The fired version is quarantined and never served again. The event is logged and posted.
- A rollback never increases a size and never touches an exit.

**Keep-rate breaker (safety, label-free, K 0, thresholds fixed now).** For each (account, cell) served version, the
keep share of gated candidates (explore samples excluded) is measured over the trailing 50 candidates or 5 trading
days, whichever is longer. It is compared with v0's keep share on 2015-17 pseudo-live, recorded in the training report.
If the share falls below max(0.25 × v0 share, 5 %), or the cell refuses every gated candidate on 2 consecutive trading
days with ≥ 10 candidates, serving reverts to the parent at the next decision. A second harm sequence per cell, run as
in 5.5, compares the served model with the pre-model rule (keep-all). An upper bound below 0 suspends gating for the
cell as under H2.

**Suspension (trading-safety H2).** If v0 fires, or the cumulative per-cell sequence fires, or the breaker trips on the
parent, the cell stops gating. The pre-model rule decides every candidate (the gate returns None, never a refusal).
`model_gate_suspended` is logged and posted at FAIL. Gating comes back only through a new version that passes the
pre-serve check and a trading-safety-reviewed change.

**ABSTAIN at serving (trading-safety H3).** At serving, ABSTAIN means the model does not gate. The cell's candidates go
to the pre-model rule unchanged. ABSTAIN never refuses a candidate (test 23).

**Pre-serve check (every daily child and weekly body, automatic, K 0; trading-safety M1).** A version serves only if
all of these pass, otherwise the parent stays:
- the A19 fail-closed load passes;
- train/serve parity (test 13) holds on the last 500 decision vectors;
- the artifact is deterministic (test 14);
- |keep-threshold shift vs v0| ≤ 0.05 R, measured on the body plus the head;
- its label-free keep share on the last 4 weeks of candidates is within ±50 % relative of its parent's and above the
  breaker floor.

The results go to `sr_v3_versions` with the version's sha.

**Scope of every live action (trading-safety M4).** The gate, explore sample, head update, refit, rollback and
suspension act only on a new entry's first unit, before the RiskGate. They never see or change a stop, target, time
barrier, weekend close, kill switch, reconciliation stop, pyramid add or size. Explore orders pass the RiskGate at the
arm's normal size, never larger, and count toward every cap, drawdown halt and the kill switch. They are left out only
of the H-81 cost stop and the edge cap. Test 22: with the gate refusing everything, every exit of an open position fires
unchanged, and explore fills count toward the daily and weekly caps. Explore P&L also counts toward the money and kill
stops; it is excluded only from edge reads and n (PR #463 trading-safety F2).

**Stale (trading-safety L1).** Stale means one of: no successful daily job for 7 days; the A19 load fails; or no
feature vector within `GATE_WAIT_S` of the bar close. A stale cell fails open to the pre-model rule (counted in
`gate_fail_open`).

**Every cell.** Each (tf × class) cell joins the loop as its step 1 data and step 2 report land. A class with no cost
of record ABSTAINs (do-not-do 31).

**Scope of the owner-directive deviations.**
- The daily update (R-N says weekly) and the weekly refit on live rows (do-not-do 21) apply only to **learning versions
  on demo**.
- A learning version is never a judged version and never pools into a judged read.
- Judged evidence comes only from the frozen history procedure (5.3).
- **Quant ruling 1:** acceptable on demo with the issue 8 text above, tests 4 and 16, the N12 reserve weeks, and no
  pooling with judged reads.
- **Quant ruling 4: demo gating is K 0 if all of the following hold:**
  - deployment is unconditional on outcome. Every cell with a merged report and a Stage 0 pass deploys, including an
    intercept-only (B8) body for a unit below L3;
  - updates and rollbacks are automatic and pre-specified;
  - any human choice made after seeing learning-version outcomes is K +1 (A2);
  - records carry `evidence_class: learning_version` and `counts_toward_reads = false`.
  These conditions are written as an ADR 0061 amendment in this PR.

### 5.5 Monitoring

**Harm sequence**, per served version.
- A predictable plug-in empirical-Bernstein confidence sequence (Waudby-Smith & Ramdas 2024, *JRSS-B* 86(1); Howard
  et al. 2021, *Ann. Statist.* 49(2)).
- It runs on per-day mean S contributions, rows clipped at ±10 R, α 0.05, model vs abstain.
- The counterfactual is complete: replay labels cover every candidate, and the explore sample checks replay against
  fills.
- Upper bound < 0 → **automatic rollback**. On an evidence version it also demotes, and re-promotion needs a new
  read.
- A simulation test asserts a false-alarm rate ≤ α at any stopping time, and detection of an injected −0.05 R.
- Rough detection times at gold 15m flow: −0.15 R in 1-2 months, −0.05 R in about a year.

**Head calibration sequence** on the mean residual per (broker, cell). If it lies wholly outside ±0.05 R, the head is
marked `out_of_scope` (WARN, new version).

**Drift is WARN only (A18, R-P).**
- PSI is sample-size-adjusted, per served input and source, against the stored `reference_bins`, with Holm across
  inputs, at ≥ 300 effective rows.
- PSI never triggers a size-down, a demotion or a retrain.
- Status `ok` needs n_with_features > 0, n_weights > 0 and ≥ 1 fitted body. Otherwise the status is `starved` (WARN;
  FAIL after 48 h).
- Health shows counts: history_rows, live_rows_added, versions, rollbacks, out_of_scope, and days since the last
  child.
