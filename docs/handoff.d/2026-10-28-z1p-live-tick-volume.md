- Z1P produced no candidates live (2026-10-27: 0 against 11.7 a day expected). Root cause: the 1m/5m exploration pass (`exploration._minutes`) built the minute that had just closed from the engine's sampled quotes. That minute's `tick_count` was the number of distinct quotes polled (at most four a second at the 0.25 s loop). The 60 minutes before it came from the broker's M1 bars, whose `tick_count` is MT5 `tick_volume`. So v_t was compared with the median of a different feed and never reached 2.5 x it. This broke H-81 section 0 ("compared with its own trailing median on the same feed"). The broker's bars do carry tick volume: `mt5_adapter` renames `tick_volume` to `tick_count`, and `history.rates_to_bars` keeps it. Fix: with broker bars on, every minute whose broker bar has been fetched is the broker's (`broker_bars.fetched`, laid over the sampled minutes). The minute just closed also waits for its bar, as a decision close does (`broker_bars.awaiting`, now shared with `hold`: at most 3 s past close + 1.5 s, while no finished call covered it). The rule and its thresholds are unchanged. The same fix applies to the other volume rules (Z2P's v_t < v_{t-2}, Z8P, Z5P) and to the 1m/5m arms' spread gate. Past the hold the minute is decided on the sampled bar, as the engine's own closes are. Reviews owed: trading-safety (the minute pass's 3 s wait is on the entry path; exits do not wait) and quant (live now matches the backtest's volume definition).
