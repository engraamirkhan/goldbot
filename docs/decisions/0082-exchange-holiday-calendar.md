# 0082: Exchange holidays from a published calendar with point-in-time availability

Status: proposed (data-engineer, 2026-11-13; BACKLOG 152, R145). Reviews: quant-reviewer (event sets of S05-S07, S6
contract), code-reviewer.

## Context
`dispatcher.build_ctx` built `holiday_<US|UK|DE>` as weekdays with no bar in the long daily series of US500, UK100
and DE40 over the whole window. A data gap read as a holiday and the set was known only after the fact. Measured on
the explore window (decisions 2010-2017): US and UK had 192 and 193 "holidays", of which about 150 each were two
multi-month holes in the long files (2011-09-23..2012-01-18 and 2013-02-26..05-22), while every real US/UK holiday
from mid-2013 on was missed (the later source is a CFD feed that prints on exchange holidays). S6 (EVAL-2
truncation) could not cut scheduled events because ctx carried no publication time, so S05-S07 were "kept whole".

## Decision
- Holidays come from `config/research/exchange_holidays.yaml`, generated by `scripts/build_exchange_holidays.py`
  from `exchange_calendars` at a pinned version (4.13.2; XNYS, XLON, XETR; 1990-2027). Free, open source, maintained
  from the exchanges' published notices. Build-time only: not a project dependency; the file is versioned
  (`xcals-4.13.2-r1`), hashed into the EVAL-2 harness and validated on every load (weekdays, sorted, unique, UTC,
  regular dates known before the day; a breach raises).
- Every date carries `available_utc`: regular rules 1 Dec of the prior year (an assumed publication floor, not a
  measured date); ad hoc closures the day after the notice; the day after the closure when unannounced or the notice
  was not verified (9/11, Nixon 1994). A new ad hoc date in a newer exchange_calendars fails the build until its
  notice is recorded.
- `Ctx.avail[name]` carries the publication instant of every scheduled set (holidays, and calendar-v1 release events
  via `data.us_events_avail`, the earliest `available_utc` per instant). S05, S06 and S07 read a date only once
  published at the decision bar's close. `truncate_ctx` cuts a set with `avail` at publication <= cut in both modes;
  the strict cut now passes on S05-S07.

Rejected: keep inferring from bars with a data-quality filter (still after the fact, and a CFD source never shows a
holiday); a hand-written YAML like `holidays_cme_metals.yaml` (about 900 dates over 38 years, error-prone, no source
of record); a runtime dependency on exchange_calendars (a pinned snapshot is reproducible and reviewable).

## Consequences
- S05-S07 event sets change on every window, so prior S05-S07 reads are void for the new harness hash and the units
  need new registry rows (BACKLOG 236). Explore counts old -> new: S05-V0 103 -> 199, S05-V1 70 -> 149, S06-V0
  656 -> 652, S06-V1 313 -> 311, S06-V2 372 -> 367, S07-V0 625 -> 692, S07-V1 604 -> 605.
- S07's "next meeting under 10 days ahead" skip now sees only published meetings; calendar-v1 gives FOMC dates a
  7-day lead, so it under-skips until FOMC rows carry the Fed's schedule date (BACKLOG 235).
- Windows before 1990-01-01 raise; the calendar must be extended before 2027-12-31 (BACKLOG 237).
- Early closes are not listed (S05 exits at the T-1 close as before; BACKLOG 237).
- What would change this: a measured publication date earlier/later than the floor for a holiday a detector trades
  within a month of 1 December, or exchange_calendars dropping maintenance (then the snapshot is frozen and the
  exchanges' own notices are transcribed).
