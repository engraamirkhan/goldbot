- Slow fills no longer block entries (live issue 2026-10-27, vantage-demo; ADR 0052). A `place_order` that does not answer (the bridge's read timeout or a lost connection) is now an ambiguous order, looked up at the broker by its comment (BACKLOG 72: adopted, or not placed after two clean looks 120 s apart), never a tick error, so entries wait only until the look-up settles it instead of a whole decision bar. Through the bridge, entries go out on a worker thread with a client of their own and the account's new `order_timeout_s` (accounts.yaml; vantage-demo 35 s, default 15 s, at most 60 s); the tick thread waits at most 5 s for the reply (was up to 15 s), then carries on with its passes. A late reply is journalled `order_late` and brings the look-up forward to that pass. One order in flight per account (a second is refused before its row is written). A tick read that times out behind our own order in flight is not a tick error. Known limit (trading-safety MEDIUM): the MT5 box serves one call at a time, so engine-side closes do not keep per-tick cadence while an order is in flight; worst exit delay `exit_delay_bound_s`, about 45-55 s at 35 s (not a regression), covered by the broker-side stop; BACKLOG 135 fixes it before any real-money account. A second order while one is in flight is journalled `entry_refused:order_in_flight` (auto, probe, approval), never a tick error. An error the bridge or terminal answered with still raises as before (row `sending`, LOW-C). Code: `goldbot/engine/order_send.py`. Review owed: trading-safety-reviewer (order path), code-reviewer.
