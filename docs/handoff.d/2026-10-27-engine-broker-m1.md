- Engine 1m close lag fixed: completed 1m bars now come from the broker's own M1 bars (`goldbot/engine/broker_bars.py`, D28). The bars built from sampled quotes are now used only for the current minute and as a fallback when the broker's bar is late or missing. A fallback minute gets a `broker_bar_late` dq warning, which does not block entries, and is replaced when the broker's bar arrives within 10 min (`broker_bar_replaced`). Decisions already taken stay as journalled. A bar joins only after it was fetched at or after its close, and visible_at is unchanged. Engine state now has a `broker_bars_1m` field with counts; the store golden was regenerated for that key only. This is on in production (bridge or terminal) and off for paper, replays and backtests.
