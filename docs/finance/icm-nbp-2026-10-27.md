# IC Markets (Seychelles) negative-balance protection: public-document check, 2026-10-27

Context: ADR 0055 / BACKLOG 27 live pilot. Trading-safety wants written NBP confirmation before the first live order.
Read-only desk research of IC Markets' own public documents; no contact with IC Markets. Not legal advice.

## Verdict

**NOT SETTLED. Do not treat NBP as confirmed.** The Seychelles client terms refer to NBP only conditionally ("to the extent
that Negative Balance Protection applies to you") and never say who it applies to. The Seychelles risk notice says the
company "may" bear the loss. The Help Centre says the opposite. Written confirmation from IC Markets is required.

## Entity

All three documents name the counterparty as Raw Trading Ltd (trading as IC Markets Global), Seychelles FSA licence SD018.

## Clauses

1. Terms and Conditions, clause 4.5(c) (account administration). Verbatim:
   "we may set off any amount we owe you in any other Account, without notice, provided that we shall not exercise any right
   of set-off in respect of a negative balance or negative equity to the extent that Negative Balance Protection applies to
   you. Where Negative Balance Protection applies, the Company shall not recover, transfer, set off or otherwise apply funds,
   assets or positive balances held in any other Account of the Client for the purpose of satisfying such negative balance."
2. Terms and Conditions, clause 4.6, closing sentence. Verbatim: "Where Negative Balance Protection applies, any negative
   balance shall be limited to zero following the close-out or other relevant determination of the Client's positions, and
   the Company shall not set off any positive balance, funds, Financial Products or other assets held in another Account
   against such negative balance."
3. Terms and Conditions, section 12.6 (prohibited conduct). Verbatim: "the ordinary application or benefit of Negative
   Balance Protection ... shall not, by itself, constitute prohibited conduct. In particular, where applicable law limits
   the liability of a Retail Client in respect of restricted speculative investments ..." and "The Company shall not regard
   the existence of a negative balance ... or the Company's assumption of a loss pursuant to an applicable client-protection
   mechanism as evidence of prohibited conduct solely because such outcome occurred."
4. Terms and Conditions, clause 16.4 (adverse). Verbatim: "we have the right (at any time) to set-off any Losses incurred or
   any amounts you owe in respect of your Transactions or any debit balances in any Account. If any Loss or debit balance
   exceeds all amounts held, you must immediately pay us any excess whether demanded or not."
5. Risk Disclosure Notice, risk (e) (adverse or soft). Verbatim: "It is possible for adverse market movements to result in
   the loss of your account balance in full or even more. In case you lose more than your current account balance, we may
   bear the negative consequences of such adverse events."
6. Order Execution Policy, abuse paragraph. Verbatim: "...IC determines in its sole discretion that you voluntarily and/or
   involuntarily undertook to abuse the NBP offered by IC ..." This presumes an NBP is "offered" but does not define it.
7. Help Centre (adverse). "Do you offer negative balance protection?" answers "In case you lose more than your current
   account balance, you should be aware that you will bear the negative consequences"; the CFD FAQ says IC "cannot guarantee
   that your account will not enter into a negative balance".

## What this means

| Question | Answer from public documents |
|---|---|
| Does NBP exist in the SC terms? | Yes, referred to (4.5, 4.6, 12.6, OEP). |
| Does it apply to retail clients of the SC entity? | Not stated. "Applies to you" is undefined; no clause names SC retail clients as covered. |
| Does it apply to MT5 Raw Spread accounts? | Not stated; no account-type carve-in or carve-out found. |
| Is it a guarantee? | No. Risk notice says "may bear"; clause 16.4 and the Help Centre point the other way. |
| Date of terms | Not shown; cannot say the PDF is the current version. |

Worth noting: clauses 4.5 and 4.6 are written so that, if NBP applies, the balance floors at zero and other accounts are not
touched. That is the behaviour we want, but only a statement that it applies to this client and account type settles it.
The same T&C wording about "a Retail Client in respect of restricted speculative investments" reads as a reference to
regulation in other jurisdictions, not a Seychelles rule. Third-party review sites disagree and were not relied on.

## Gap and the message to send

Public documents do not settle it. The owner can send this from the account email (no account number needed in the first
message; support may ask for it through their own secure channel):

> Subject: Written confirmation of negative balance protection
>
> I hold a retail MT5 Raw Spread account (GBP) with IC Markets Global, Raw Trading Ltd (Seychelles FSA licence SD018). Your
> Terms and Conditions (clauses 4.5, 4.6 and 12.6) refer to Negative Balance Protection "to the extent that it applies to
> you", but your Risk Disclosure Notice says the Company "may" bear losses and your Help Centre says clients bear negative
> balances. Please confirm in writing: (1) does Negative Balance Protection apply to my retail account under Raw Trading
> Ltd; (2) if so, is it a contractual guarantee that my balance cannot go below zero, including after a gap through stops
> or a weekend gap; (3) are any conditions or exclusions applicable to MT5 Raw Spread accounts; and (4) which version and
> date of the Terms and Conditions I am bound by.

Until a written yes is on file, the pilot should size on the assumption that a gap loss above the balance is owed to the
broker (clause 16.4). Recommendation only: sizing and going live remain owner decisions.
