# Learning class registration 2026-11-02 (demo only, unqualified)

The owner, 2026-11-02: "fix the limits of our system to trade ... for the system to be not bound to test, learn and
improve signals". When asked whether a demo "learning" class should exist beside the qualification rule (ADR 0061), the
owner answered YES.

This document pre-registers the arms of that class, their controls and their stops, before their first learning fill.
- The rule: ADR 0061 amendment 2026-11-02.
- The engine and the limits: ADR 0072.
- The review round of PR #295 added the research lead's quant rulings (1)-(5) and the trading-safety conditions.

## What a learning arm is, and is not

- **What it is.** A `role: learning` row of `config/exploration_arms.yaml`. It enters on a demo probe_only account
  without a history qualification, inside its `learning` block, which sets:
  - this prereg;
  - its own K row in the trial registry (`k_id`, the config hash of `research.arms.learning_k_config`);
  - a dedicated, matched random control (same timeframe, stop, target and hold);
  - a loss cap of 20 x its per-trade cap;
  - an end at most 45 days away;
  - its registration day `since`. Only fills sent on or after `since` count toward its read and its loss (quant
    ruling (1)).
- **What it is not: evidence of an edge.**
  - Its fills never qualify a strategy: a qualification citing a learning K row is refused at load.
  - It never enters a family ranking: the perf report and the daily review give it its own section.
  - The bandit never allocates it, and it never trains a style model.
  - A promising read is a lead for a pre-registered history qualification (screen 2010-2017, confirm 2018-2025-09),
    nothing more.
- **Size.** Minimum lot, one unit: a learning arm with growth sizing or pyramid adds is refused. At most 6 learning
  positions are open on an account at once (`learning_max_open`).

## Stops

The engine applies these in `engine/exploration_learning.py` and `exploration._send`, whichever comes first:

1. **Read due.** At 300 admissible fills since `since`, the arm stops entering. An admissible fill is filled, closed
   by the arm's own barrier and has its P&L recorded. Censored fills (the Friday flat, a kill switch, a moved stop) are
   counted and reported apart, never in the 300 (quant ruling (5)). The read itself is the evaluator's:
   - it compares the arm's net R with its own control's on the same risk day x session x side;
   - it decides only whether a history qualification is worth a registry trial.
2. **Loss cap**, in two places:
   - **Retirement.** Once the cumulative net P&L since `since` reaches minus `loss_cap_gbp`, the arm retires. A fill
     whose P&L is unreadable counts as a loss at its stop plus its round trip, never 0.
   - **Each entry** (trading-safety L1). An entry is refused (`learning_loss_cap`) when three amounts together would
     reach the cap: that realised loss, the arm's open risk at its stops, and the new trade's loss at its stop with
     its round trip.
3. **End.** Past `until` (2026-12-17, 45 days after registration), the arm stops entering.

- **The control.** An arm's dedicated control stops with it; it also stops when its arm is suspended by its cost stop
  (trading-safety L2).
- **Fail closed.** An unreadable ledger stops every learning entry until a read succeeds.
- **H-61 and H-81.** The stops in `config/arm_stops.yaml` still apply on top.

- **The controls:**
  - New ids, forward only, random entries seeded by their own id. Each takes a fixed 5% share of its scalp pot and is

    match the window and exits exactly but the cadence only at the family control's rate. Z7's half-hour slots are not
    reproduced. The read compares the arm and its control on the same risk day x session x side cells, so the cadence
    affects the read's precision, not its bias.

## K and the budget

- **The rows.** 30 K rows: 15 arms and their 15 dedicated controls, family `learning_class`, status `learning`. They
  are written by `scripts/record_learning_k_rows.py` on the brain before the first learning fill.
- **Budget.** Each row is a trial: it counts in K and in the 2026Q4 budget. The script checks the budget under the
  registry lock and writes nothing if the rows do not fit. Raising the budget is the owner's decision.
- **Fail closed.** Until the rows exist, every learning arm and every dedicated control is refused.
