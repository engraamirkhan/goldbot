New `goldbot/ops/learning_ledger.py` reads closed exploration, control and cost-probe trades from the trade ledger (`goldbot.api.trades_ledger.build_ledger`, imported at call time) and drops any trade whose (account, broker position) is already in the closed-trade record, so nothing is counted twice. Exploration and probe closes are still NEVER written to `closed_trades*.jsonl` and `load_closed_trades`, the phase gate, throttle and stop rule are untouched (a test asserts the record and its files are unchanged with exploration and probe trades present). `perf-report` gains, per account, a section "Exploration, control and cost-probe closes (not in the phase-gate record)": by kind (exploration, control, cost probe) and by kind / family with the random controls (`random_bracket`) listed beside the families; net only where commission and swap are both recorded, the 30-trade "insufficient sample" rule, and the groups count in the multiple-groups note; an unreadable aux.db is named in the report's warnings. The closed-trade sections are unchanged. `pnl_basis` counts these closes in the 30-day window and says where they came from ("0 of 1,458 closed trades ... (exploration 1,300, control 20, probes 158)"); still WARN-only, an unreadable aux.db is a WARN with the reason. Review round: an exploration family is read against the random control, not against zero. Its row carries `excess_r` (mean net R less the control's mean net R, matched by risk day x session x side with the control weighted by the family's cell mix; trades in a cell without control trades are dropped and counted) and a Welch 90% interval on the difference only when both sides have 30+ trades; the verdict is "above / below / not distinguishable from the random control", and no learning row reads "mean R above zero". A line under the tables says the bandit chooses which arms trade and when, so pooled arms carry selection bias and no row is evidence (claims go through a registry trial). Voided rows (`evidence_exclusions`) are skipped; censored rows are shown as "(+N censored)" beside the row and never enter R or money (also in `pnl_basis`). Learning gross is pnl + commission + swap, never the ledger's modelled-spread gross. The record sections are byte-identical with or without learning trades (the learning tables carry their own currency). The multiple-groups note now says ", plus K learning groups (kind rows overlap family rows)". A ledger failure is a report error, the health run builds the ledger once (`HealthContext.memo`), the cost-probe source opens without migrating aux.db (`build_ledger(read_only=True)`, `cost_probe.probes(migrate=False)`), and `--account <id>` resolves ledger names through the accounts config.
