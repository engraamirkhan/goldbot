- **234. Measure rules-v1 shock precision on the brain's real headlines.** Value: the fixture is 48 hand-written headlines; it bounds gross errors, not live precision. Acceptance: hand-label at least 300 collected headlines (shock, rates, dollar) on the brain (never published), report rule-shock precision/recall and blackout minutes per week; if precision < 0.8, raise `news.rule_shock_min_confidence` or narrow the lexicon as rules-v2 (a new `scorer` value, never a silent rewrite of rules-v1 rows).
