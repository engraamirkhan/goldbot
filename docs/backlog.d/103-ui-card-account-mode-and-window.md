- **103. Approval card says demo or real money, why it asks, and uses the proposal's own window.** Value: the card the owner decides on does not say whether the account is demo or real money, nor why a demo entry is asking at all (ADR 0036: demo enters automatically; a demo card appears only when propose is forced by the re-arm lock, the 12% kill switch or an unreadable risk file). A demo card and a real-money card look identical, which invites approving real money with demo habits. The countdown bar and the expired copy assume 90 s (`web/src/lib/approval.ts:8` WINDOW_S; `web/src/screens/ProposalCard.tsx:46` scales by WINDOW_S, `:217` says "within 90 s"), so any other `window_s` shows a full bar until the last 90 s and wrong copy. The 60-min countdown in the e2e shots is seed data (`scripts/e2e_server.py:99` window_s=3600), not engine behaviour. Acceptance: API `Proposal` gains `account_mode` (demo | live | paper), `window_s` and `ask_reason` (null on live; on demo the forced-propose kind in plain words, from `ForcedPropose`); OpenAPI and `web/src/lib/schema.d.ts` regenerated. The card header carries a text badge "REAL MONEY" (accent, not colour alone) or "DEMO" and, on demo, one line "Asking because: <reason>"; bar and expired copy use `window_s`; Telegram card (`goldbot/telegram/approvals.py`) carries the same badge and reason line. The e2e seed uses window_s=90 for one card and marks the other demo-forced. Vitest covers live/demo/paper badges, a 300 s window bar at 50%, expired copy with window_s; e2e asserts the badge at 390 px. Before/after shots via `scripts/ui_snapshots.mjs`. Design note: `docs/design/ui-91-92-approval-card.md` (sections 0-5, 7; signed off 2026-10-19; terminal_not_demo is treated as real money with Approve disabled, pending trading-safety).
