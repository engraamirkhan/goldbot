`docs/deploy/free-hosting-options.md` (revision 2, 2026-10-11). **No trades exist, so every figure here is an

## 1. Bad news first

1. **Costs are real now, but income is not.** The demo earns no money. Real income needs the paper -> tiny-live gate
   (at least 150 paper trades) and `unlock_live`. In tiny-live, risk per trade is 0.1% (£1 at £1,000), and the
   minimum-lot exception is never applied in tiny-live (ADR 0004). A 15m minimum lot risks about £8.20, so **at
   £1,000 tiny-live cannot place a trade at all.** No strategy has passed the research gates yet; the earliest 15m
   trial is Q2 2027 (`docs/research/scorecard-2026-10.md`). On the current plan, **the system cannot earn real money
   before the Azure trial ends (about Oct 2027).** The owner should plan to pay the post-trial hosting out of pocket
   at first.
2. **At £1,000, hosting is a large share of any realistic return.** The post-trial lean budget is £10-15 a month,
   which is 1.0-1.5% of the account a month. ADR 0004 puts an honest return with a real edge at 1-4% a month. So
   hosting alone takes between a quarter and all of that return.
3. **The staff agents may be the largest running cost.** `agents.monthly_cap_usd: 40` (about £30 a month) is three
   times the post-trial VPS. It is a real cost if an Anthropic API key is in the keyring, and it does not depend on
   any trial. It is listed separately below so the owner can decide on it (recommendations, decision D1).

## 3. Trading-cost assumptions (priors until the cost table measures them)

From `config/settings.yaml` `costs:` and `goldbot/execution/costs.py` `prior_extra_cost_usd`:

- **Extra cost beyond the spread: 0.37 $/oz round trip** = 2 x slippage prior 0.15 $/oz + 2 x commission 3.5 USD
  per lot side / 100 oz (0.07 $/oz). This is the figure frozen in the Q1/Q2 pre-registrations.
- **Spread: assumed 0.25 $/oz** (range 0.10-0.45). This is ADR 0004's "about $0.20-0.30 per 0.01 lot" for a Raw
  account. 0.45 $/oz is the RiskGate's `max_spread_points: 45` ceiling (assuming 1 point = $0.01 on ICM XAUUSD,
  which is unverified, BACKLOG 27). Nothing has been measured; the first cost table replaces it.
- **Swap prior:** long -60 USD per lot per night, short 0, triple on Wednesday.
- **Contract:** 100 oz per lot, minimum 0.01 lot = 1 oz (design defaults, BACKLOG 27 to verify).
- **Gold: $4,150/oz** (ADR 0004, 28 Sep 2026, range 3,920-4,500). **GBPUSD 1.33.** Both are assumptions to
  re-read at the first ledger.

**One minimum-lot (0.01) round trip, stated as an assumption:**

| Component | $/oz | USD per 0.01 lot | GBP per 0.01 lot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spread (assumed) | 0.25 | 0.25 | £0.19 |
| Slippage prior, entry + exit | 0.30 | 0.30 | £0.23 |
| Commission, both sides | 0.07 | 0.07 | £0.05 |
| **Round trip** | **0.62** | **0.62** | **£0.47** (range £0.35-0.62 for spreads of 0.10-0.45) |
| Swap, one night long (prior) | n/a | 0.60 | £0.45 (£1.35 on the triple Wednesday) |

In relative terms, £0.47 is 0.015% of the notional at $4,150, or **0.057 R** on a 15m trade (1.5 x ATR stop about
$10.90, ADR 0004 estimate) and 0.028 R on 1h. The gold price does not change the $/oz cost. It changes the cost in R
only through ATR, which scales with price. At 20 trades a month the trading costs are about £9.30 (1.1 R); at 40,
about £18.60.

## 4. Break-even

Unit: **£8.20 per R.** That is the 15m minimum lot at £1,000 (0.82% risk, ADR 0004 s.2), the only trade that fits
the account. The design's 0.5% (£5) cannot be placed, and the 1% cap (£10) is the upper bound. Break-even means
**net closed P&L (after spread, commission, swap and slippage) equal to the running costs**:

| Scenario | £ a month needed | R a month at £8.20 | % of £1,000 | Net R per trade at 20 trades a month | Gross R per trade (adding 0.057 R cost) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L, now | £0 | 0 | 0% | 0 | 0.057 |
| P, now | about £1 | 0.12 | 0.1% | 0.006 | 0.063 |
| **L, after the trial** | **£12.50** (10-15) | **1.5 R** (1.2-1.8) | **1.25%** | **0.076** | **0.13** |
| P, after the trial | £13.50 | 1.6 R | 1.35% | 0.082 | 0.14 |
| F, now | £31 | 3.8 R | 3.1% | 0.19 | 0.25 |
| F, after the trial | £43.60 | 5.3 R | 4.4% | 0.27 | 0.32 |

How to read it: in ADR 0004's Monte Carlo a net +0.1 R edge makes about £16 a month at £1,000. That covers scenario
L after the trial, with little to spare. Scenario F needs about +0.27 R net per trade, which no documented systematic
gold edge reaches (ADR 0004 s.8). The best result so far is tsmom at +0.06 R gross and **negative net**
(scorecard 2026-10). Sample size behind every break-even figure: 0 trades.

Break-even falls in proportion to equity: at £2,000 the lean post-trial cost is 0.6% a month.

## 5. Dates and the 60-day rule

| Date (approximate until the sign-up dates are known) | Event | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-up + 30 days (about **2026-11-11**) | Azure $200 credit ends; upgrade to PAYG or lose the free services | owner upgrades, sets a budget alert of a few £ (hosting doc s.6) |
| Monthly | Oracle and Azure free pages re-read | finance-auditor, in the monthly budget step |
| Sign-up + 10 months (about **2027-08-13**) | **60-day warning** for the Azure trial | flag on #34 and in the audit |
| Sign-up + 11 months (about 2027-09-12) | owner decision due: pay, migrate to the Oracle Wine bridge, or move | program-director brings decision D2 |
| Sign-up + 12 months (about **2027-10-12**) | **Azure trial ends. This is the date the system is meant to cover its own costs** | deallocate before then unless the owner chose to pay |

**Covering costs by the trial end** means that, over the three months before it (Jul-Sep 2027), the net closed P&L
averages at least the post-trial budget of the chosen scenario (£12.50 for L). Because demo money is not real
(section 1), it is measured two ways: (a) **demo cover**, the same test on demo P&L at the real £1,000 balance, which
shows whether the system would cover its costs; and (b) **real cover**, the test on live money, which is the only one
that pays the bill. The audit reports both. On the current plan, (b) is £0 until the live gate.

**60-day warning rule.** Whenever a free trial, credit or free tier that the system depends on ends within 60 days
(or a provider announces a cut), the monthly audit raises a HIGH finding to the program-director, with the cost after
the end and the options. The program-director records the owner's decision on #34 by **T-30**. If no paid option is
recorded by **T-7**, the resource is deallocated or migrated, so it never rolls into billing by default. The
**exits-never-gated** rule applies during any migration: the bridge is moved only with the book flat or with server
stops in place.

The actual sign-up dates go into the next budget (the owner states the dates only, never the account details).
