- **108. Mid-quarter exploration arm with a flat prior.** Value: a new exploration arm can start in a quarter whose trial budget is spent (2026Q4: 69/69). An H-60 history prior is a counted look, so such an arm starts flat, as the Vantage twins did (docs/research/time-structure/learning-loop.md L-4). Acceptance: (1) the arm loader refuses an arm with no dated pre-registration amendment committed before its first fill; (2) its prior is flat, and no history run is made or read; (3) it enters H-61's Bonferroni m and K at its read; (4) the ADR 0043 pots and per-trade caps bind unchanged (test).
